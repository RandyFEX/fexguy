<h2 id="final-expenses-your-family-will-have-to-pay"><strong>Final Expenses Your Family Will Have To Pay</strong></h2>

<p>A life insurance policy is often purchased to help cover <a href="/how-much-does-a-funeral-cost/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">funeral expenses.</a></p>

<p>Without life insurance, your family will be left to pay the final expenses. How much does a funeral cost? The funeral cost can be quite expensive, and the funeral expenses can vary depending on the type of service and goods used during the service.</p>

<p><strong>Casket</strong> – This is commonly the largest <a href="/pay-for-a-funeral-without-life-insurance/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">funeral</a> expense. Starting at around $2,400, the casket can get really expensive if you would like to be buried in metal or bronze.</p>

<p><strong>Embalming</strong> – This is needed if there will be visitation or viewing. Embalming will not be required if there won’t be any viewing. According to the National Funeral Directors Association, embalming costs around $750.</p>

<p><strong>Cremation:</strong> direct cremation costs between $500 and $1,500, depending on your area. In most metro areas, direct cremation costs between $800 and $1,200.</p>

<p><strong>Memorial Service,</strong> according to NFDA this service costs around $2,000. The memorial service includes planning, preparing obituaries, getting permits and death certificates, and viewing and coordinating with the gravesite.</p>

<p><strong>Gravesite</strong> – the price of a plot in a cemetery depends on many variables, like the type of cemetery or the size of the plot. In some larger cities, most plots cost between $1,000 and $2,500 or higher. In a private transaction, it is possible to purchase plots in advance in some states.</p>

<p><strong>Transportation</strong>– the use of a hearse cost around $300.  A van or service car will cost another $150. </p>

<p><strong>Obituary</strong> –It may cost up to $600 to publish an obituary in a national newspaper. The cost may vary depending on the publishing day, the obituary’s length, and if there’s a picture.</p>

<p><strong>Gravestone</strong> costs around $1,000 to pay for a flat grave marker, while a kerbed or upright may cost up to $10,000. Prices depend on the color, material, and finish of the marker. You can choose from sandstone, granite, concrete, or metal. Metal markers are the most expensive, but if you’re a military veteran you will have a gravemarker for free.</p>

<p><strong>Flowers</strong>– funeral flowers, including bouquets, sprays, and boutonnieres, can cost around $200 to $1,000 for the whole complete package.</p>

<p><strong>Other Expenses:</strong></p>

<p>These are the other expenses your survivor will have to pay when you pass away:</p>

<p><strong>Travel expenses</strong> – if your survivors don’t live in the same city, they may have to make a flight and stay in a hotel for a few days to be with their loved ones during the funeral.</p>

<p><strong>Lost Wages</strong>– your death will not only cause emotional grieving, but it will be impossible for your loved ones to go back to work after a traumatic loss. It would take a few weeks to a month before your spouse can return to work.</p>

<p><strong>Taxes and debt</strong> – your spouse will be responsible for paying your medical bills and taxes. If your spouse is still living in your own house, they will owe some type of estate tax, which they may not be ready to do without selling the home.</p>

<p><strong>Counseling</strong> – your spouse may need to go on counseling to process their emotions and grief. Counseling sessions cost around $100 or more an hour.</p>

<p>Losing a loved one is tough, and having no life insurance for your death will be harder on your spouse. Life without life insurance is difficult. We often hear no life insurance stories.</p>

<p>There is limited financial assistance available for funeral and <a href="/how-much-does-final-expense-insurance-cost/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">burial costs</a>. If your spouse does not have the financial means to pay for your funeral and needs help with funeral costs, she may have to do with these alternatives:</p>

<p><strong>Loan</strong> – your spouse will have to get a loan to fund the funeral expenses. They will not only have to pay for the funeral but also pay interest on the loan.</p>

<p><strong>Funeral payment plan</strong> – some funeral directors offer loans for those unable to pay. You will only have to deposit up-front and pay back in installments. Your spouse would need to shop around to find a funeral director that offers excellent value.</p>

<h2 id="programs-that-can-help-your-family-if-you-die-without-life-insurance"><br><strong>Programs That Can Help Your Family If You Die Without Life Insurance</strong></h2>

<p>No life insurance how to pay for the funeral, here are some ways how to finance a funeral:</p>

<p><strong>Social Security </strong></p>

<p>The Social Security Administration provides financial assistance for widows, the surviving spouse will receive a one-time death benefit. The death benefit today is $255.</p>

<p>The spouse and children may be entitled to receive a monthly benefit. They may qualify for monthly benefits based on the work record and earnings of the decedent. The amount will be based on the percentage of the worker’s wages and length of work history.</p>

<p>The recipients will need to meet all the requirements before receiving assistance.</p>

<p><br><strong>Charities and Government Authorities</strong></p>

<p>Many charities may be able to help.</p>

<p>When someone dies, and there’s no way for their family to pay for the funeral, the government has a few ways they can help. Indigent burial programs can help those who are needy in some states and counties. Some are managed on a country level, and some states have state-level programs.</p>

<p><br><strong>Veteran’s Benefit</strong></p>

<p>The family of a deceased veteran may qualify for ongoing burial benefits from Veterans Affairs.</p>

<p>The VA’s pension is available to low-income surviving spouses or children of veterans who served during the war. VA burial benefit for service-related death is up to $2,000 toward final expenses. The VA pays up to $796 for non-service-related deaths and $300 for decedents’ hospitalization at the time of death.</p>

<p>A small plot or interment allowance may also be available to the family.</p>

<p><br><strong>Indigent Burial Assistance</strong></p>

<p>The spouse may qualify for indigent burial assistance without life insurance.</p>

<p>Some localities offer burial assistance for a family who cannot afford to bury a loved one. Indigent burial assistance varies widely depending on the locality. Some indigent burial assistance pays up to $1,100 for a funeral or $552 for cremation when the family cannot afford the final expenses.</p>

<p><br><strong>Crowdfunding</strong></p>

<p>You can crowdsource for your loved ones after a tragedy and hope that many people will feel your heartfelt appeal.</p>

<p>You can crowdsource through gofundme.com. More and more GoFundMePages are springing in times of death. This program helps some to cope with the initial final expenses.</p>

<p>If this appeal reached an adequate level, the family would have a foundation to build on.</p>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions"><strong>  Frequently Asked Questions </strong></h2>

<p><strong>What happens if a person dies without life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>If a person dies without life insurance, their family will have to foot the funeral and burial expenses alone. Most of the time, dying without life insurance creates financial stress on the loved one left behind.</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens if someone dies has no life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>If someone dies without life insurance, their loved ones will have to take care of the funeral, burial, and final expenses out of their pockets. Funerals are expensive, and it may cause significant financial stress on the family if they do not have the resources to pay for the funeral.</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens if spouse dies without life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>If your spouse dies without life insurance, the living spouse will have to find a way to pay for the funeral and final expenses. The average funeral costs $9,500 to $12,500. Without life insurance, the living spouse must find a way to pay the final expenses independently.</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens if a parent dies without life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>If a parent dies without life insurance, their kids will have to worry about paying their funeral and burial expenses. They must pay all the costs out of their pockets. If they don’t have the funds, they may go into debt to cover the funeral cost.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can you live without life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, not everyone needs life insurance. People with enough assets to pay for their own final expenses and leave enough funds to their family in their demise don’t need life insurance. Also, people who do not have anybody who depends on them can also forgo buying life insurance.</p>

<p><br><strong>What to do if there is no life insurance policy?</strong></p>

<p>If there is no life insurance, you can use your credit card (often up to 25+% interest rate) to pay for the final expenses. You can also ask the funeral home for a payment plan. You can also check social security or veterans’ benefits to pay for final expenses.</p>

<p><br><strong>How do you bury someone with no life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>You can arrange for direct cremation because it is cheaper. You can also ask for assistance from family or friends or the community. If there’s no one to help make these arrangements, then your state may provide assistance, such as covering final expenses at a reduced cost (through Medicaid).</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens to someone who dies with no money?</strong></p>
