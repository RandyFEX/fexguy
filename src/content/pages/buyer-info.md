<h2 id="h-3-consider-your-health-amp-illnesses"># 3 – CONSIDER YOUR HEALTH &amp; ILLNESSES</h2>

<p>Most people underestimate how poor their health is, and overestimate how long the will live.</p>

<p><strong>AVERAGE AGE OF DEATH IN THE UNITED STATES:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Women:</strong> 79.1</li>
<li><strong>Men:</strong> 73.2 years</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>AVERAGE LIFESPAN REDUCTION BY HEALTH ISSUE &amp; MEDICATION USAGE:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li><strong>CIRRHOSIS:</strong> Average 1-12 years average lifespan</li>
<li><strong>CONGESTIVE HEART FAILURE</strong>: Only 50% of patients live 5 years, 35% only live 10 years</li>
<li><strong>COPD: </strong>8-9 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>DEMENTIA:</strong> 8-10 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>DIABETES TYPE 2: </strong>3-4 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>DIABETIC NEUROPATHY: </strong>3 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>EMPHYSEMA: </strong>9 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>HEART ATTACK: </strong>8-10% reduction in life span on average</li>
<li><strong>HIGH BLOOD PRESSURE: </strong>5-7 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>KIDNEY Stage 3: </strong>Average survival rate from diagnosis is 6 years</li>
<li><strong>KIDNEY Stage 4:  </strong>Average survival rate from diagnosis is 4 years</li>
<li><strong>KIDNEY Stage 5:  </strong>Terminal Illness</li>
<li><strong>OVERWEIGHT (MODERATE): </strong>3 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>OVERWEIGHT (SEVERE): </strong>10 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>SMOKING: </strong>14 years average reduced life expectancy</li>
<li><strong>STROKE: </strong>Only 5-10 years average lifespan</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>COMORBITITIES</strong>: 3 years average reduced life expectancy if you have multiple comorbidities (multiple illnesses or conditions that require you to take medications).</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH CONDITIONS THAT REQUIRE A 2-YEAR WAITING PERIOD POLICY</strong>:</p>

<ul>
<li>Activity of Daily Living issues (need assistance eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, walking, etc.)</li>
<li>AID’s, HIV, ARC</li>
<li>Alzheimer’s</li>
<li>Autism</li>
<li>Cancer (remission or currently being treated. Cured cancers is OK in most cases)</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Hospice</li>
<li>Hospitalized currently</li>
<li>Leukemia (remission or currently being treated. Cured cancers is OK in most cases)</li>
<li>Nursing home confinement</li>
<li>Organ transplants in the last 5 years</li>
<li>Terminal Illness</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>UNINSURABLE CONDITIONS:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Over age 89 years old</li>
<li>Cognitive deficiencies</li>
<li>Comatose individuals</li>
<li>Downs Syndrome</li>
</ul>
