<h2 id="does-transamerica-offer-first-day-coverage"><br><strong>Does TransAmerica offer first-day coverage?</strong></h2>

<p>Yes. Transamerica offers first-day burial insurance products, but they tend to be more expensive than other companies.    </p>

<p>If you qualify, their Immediate Solution and 10-Pay Solution come with 1st-day coverage. You will be 100% covered from the first day, and your beneficiary will receive your full death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<h2 id="pros-of-transamerica-burial-insurance"><strong>Pros of TransAmerica burial insurance </strong></h2>

<p><strong>No height &amp; weight chart</strong> – No height and weight chart; you can qualify even if you are on the heavier side<br><strong>Coverage amount</strong> – Higher coverage amount available<br><strong>Pricing</strong> – Competitive premium rates for the Immediate Solution preferred plan</p>

<h2 id="cons-of-transamerica-burial-insurance"><strong>Cons of TransAmerica burial insurance</strong> </h2>

<p><strong>Higher rates</strong> – Higher premium rate on Immediate Solution standard plan<br><strong>Underwriting</strong> – The company may ask for the attending physician’s statement if they want to clarify something about your health<br><strong>Waiting period</strong> – Higher premium rate on Easy Solution plan with a waiting period</p>

<h2 id="transamerica-burial-insurance-riders"><br><strong>TransAmerica burial insurance riders</strong></h2>

<p> <strong>IMMEDIATE SOLUTION </strong> <strong>RIDERS</strong></p>

<p><strong>Additional Riders at no extra cost</strong></p>

<p><strong>1. Accelerated death benefit rider</strong> – this benefit will be available when you are diagnosed with a qualifying event like a terminal illness.</p>

<p><strong>2. Accelerated Death benefit rider with Nursing home rider</strong> – You won’t qualify for the nursing home benefit if you already need assistance with daily living activities, including eating, dressing, bathing, toileting, continence, and transferring.</p>

<p>(Riders not available in New York)</p>

<p><br><strong>Optional rider</strong>:</p>

<p><strong>Children’s and Grandchildren’s Benefit rider </strong>is available at an additional cost of $2.00 per year for each $1,000 coverage amount.&#160; The parent or grandparent must be between 18 and 75, and the child must be at least 15 days old and under 18. Coverage is available for up to 9 children, from $1,000 to $5,000.</p>

<p>With this burial insurance plan, you only need to pay a premium for 10 years, and your coverage is guaranteed to age 121. After the first ten years of paying, you stop paying for the succeeding years.</p>

<p><br><strong>OTHER BENEFITS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>POLICY LOANS </strong>– you can borrow up to 100% of your cash value. This is tax-free. Loans reduce your cash value amount and are subtracted from your death benefit. The interest on policy loans varies by state.</p>

<h2 id="when-does-transamerica-make-sense"><br><strong>When does TransAmerica burial insurance make the most sense?</strong></h2>

<p>Based on our Transamerica burial insurance review, we found that Transamerica is an excellent option if your health qualifies you for their Immediate Solution preferred plan. Their rate is comparable with other insurance companies offering first-day coverage.</p>

<p>Their Immediate Solution standard plan accepts many health conditions but is more expensive. Their premium rate for this plan is more expensive than most. </p>

<p>Stay away from their Easy Solution because it comes with a waiting period. If you have some pre-existing medical condition that only qualifies you for this plan, we can get you approved with another company at a lower rate.</p>

<h2 id="transamerica-underwriting-guidelines"><br><strong>TransAmerica burial insurance underwriting guidelines</strong></h2>

<p>To determine what burial insurance plan to offer you,
Transamerica will check the following:</p>

<ol>
<li>Your answer to the health questions on the application</li>
<li>Your prescription history</li>
<li>Medical Information Bureau file</li>
</ol>

<p>Transamerica does not conduct personal health interviews. They only do this if you leave something on the application and they need to verify your health.</p>

<p><strong>Here are some areas for which Transamerica may be more lenient or strict with underwriting:</strong></p>

<p>1.  COPD or emphysema without oxygen use will qualify for an Immediate Solution standard rate. Mild COPD is eligible for the preferred rate.</p>

<p>2. They don’t care for mental health problems of 45-85 years old. Bipolar disorder and depression using one medication without hospitalization and no loss of school or work may qualify for an Immediate Solution preferred rate.</p>

<p>Bipolar patients using two medications with two episodes or a two-week loss of school or work will be offered a standard rate. Bipolar or depression patients on disability will qualify for Easy Solution.</p>

<p>3. People with diabetes on insulin can be eligible for the Immediate Solution standard rate. Diabetics with complications like retinopathy, neuropathy, diabetic coma, or insulin shock will qualify for standard rate immediate coverage.</p>

<p>4. Mild to moderate cases of Parkinson’s disease more than two years ago after the age of 30 would be eligible for Immediate Solution at a standard rate.</p>

<p>5. Atrial fibrillation of more than two years could get an Immediate Solution standard. It occurred more than five years ago as the preferred rate.</p>

<p>6. Mild autism for ages 6 and older will qualify for the preferred rate.</p>

<p>7. Applicants with heart attack, TIA, angina, angioplasty, stent, bypass pacemaker implant, kidney disease treatment, or diagnosis within 12 months or longer will qualify for the standard rate.</p>

<p>8. Marijuana users who use it less than three times a week can be eligible for an Immediate Solution preferred rate.</p>

<p>9. Alcohol or drug abuse in recovery for 2-5 years qualifies for Easy Solution, while those in recovery for 5-8 years will be eligible for Immediate Solution standard rate.</p>

<p>10. Transamerica takes Direct Express card payments.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>The answer to the health questions is the biggest factor in determining your eligibility for coverage. Here are the three sets of questions Transamerica uses to know about your health condition.</p>

<p><br><strong>KNOCKOUT QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>If you respond YES to any question in this Section, you do not qualify for coverage.</p>

<p><strong>1) </strong>Is the proposed Insured hospitalized, bedridden, residing in a nursing home or long-term care facility, receiving hospice or home health care, confined to a wheelchair, been advised or planning to have inpatient surgery, or currently waiting for an organ transplant? </p>

<p><strong>2)</strong> Has the proposed insured ever:</p>

<ul>
<li>Been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for Alzheimer’s disease, senile dementia, organic brain disease, mental incapacity, Lou Gehrig’s disease (ALS), Downs Syndrome, Huntington’s disease, sickle cell anemia, Spina Bifida not surgically corrected, <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance/cystic-fibrosis/" target="_blank">cystic fibrosis</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance/cerebral-palsy/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">cerebral palsy</a> or any terminal medical condition? </li>
<li>Tested positive for the antibodies to the AIDS (HIV) virus or been medically diagnosed with or received treatment for HIV, Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), or AIDS Related Complex (ARC)? </li>
<li>Been in a <a href="/burial-insurance/diabetic-coma/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">diabetic coma</a> or had or been advised to have an amputation due to disease or disorder? </li>
</ul>

<p><strong>3) </strong>Within the past two years has the proposed Insured:</p>

<ul>
<li>Been diagnosed with, treated for, or advised to receive treatment for cancer (other than basal cell carcinoma)? </li>
<li>Undergone testing by a medical professional for which the results have not been received?</li>
</ul>

<p>If all your answer is NO in the knockout section, proceed to
the standard section.</p>

<p><br><strong>STANDARD SECTION</strong></p>

<p><strong>4) </strong>Has the proposed Insured been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for diabetes (other than gestational diabetes) before the age of 18? </p>

<p><strong>5) </strong>Within the past four years has the proposed Insured been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for cancer (other than basal cell carcinoma)? </p>

<p><strong>6)</strong> Within the past one year has the proposed Insured:</p>

<ul>
<li>Used illegal drugs or been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance/drug-alcohol-abuse/" target="_blank">alcohol abuse</a>, <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance/drug-abuse-treatment/" target="_blank">drug abuse</a>, or <a href="/burial-insurance/muscular-dystrophy/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">muscular dystrophy</a>? </li>
<li>Had more than 12 <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance/epilepsy-seizures/" target="_blank">seizures</a> or been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for congestive heart failure, <a href="/burial-insurance/cirrhosis/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">cirrhosis</a>, <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance/hepatitis-b/" target="_blank">hepatitis </a>B or C, or other liver diseases? </li>
<li>Been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for heart attack, stroke (CVA), transient ischemic attack (TIA), aneurysm, angina, or had or been advised to have heart surgery of any kind including bypass surgery or pacemaker implant? </li>
<li>Used oxygen to assist in breathing due to a disease or disorder, received kidney dialysis, or been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for kidney failure due to a disease or disorder? </li>
</ul>

<p>If One Question in the standard section is answered
“Yes,” Transamerica will offer you their Easy Solution graded death
benefit plan.</p>

<p>If two or more Questions in this section are answered
“Yes,” you are not eligible for any coverage.</p>

<p><br><strong>GRADED SECTION </strong></p>

<p><strong>7) </strong>Within the past two years has the proposed Insured:</p>

<ul>
<li>Had or been treated for a heart attack, angina (chest pain), stroke (CVA), transient ischemic attack (TIA), aneurysm, circulatory or blood disorder, heart surgery including bypass or irregular heart rhythm such as atrial fibrillation? </li>
<li>Had more than 12 seizures, taken insulin shots, or been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for congestive heart failure, cirrhosis, hepatitis B or C, or other liver diseases? </li>
<li>Used illegal drugs or been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for alcohol abuse or drug abuse? </li>
</ul>

<p><strong>8)</strong> Within the past four years has the proposed Insured been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for kidney disease? </p>

<p><strong>9)</strong> Has the proposed Insured ever been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for Parkinson’s disease, multiple sclerosis, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) including emphysema, chronic asthma, black lung, or other chronic respiratory diseases? </p>

<p><strong>10)</strong> Is the proposed Insured currently under the age of 50 and if so, has the proposed Insured within the past five years been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised to receive treatment for any mental disorder such as manic or clinical depression, schizophrenia, bipolar disease or post-traumatic stress syndrome?</p>

<p>Transamerica will offer you its Immediate Solution Standard plan if you say yes to only one question in this section.</p>

<p>Transamerica would offer you their Easy Solution with a graded death benefit if you answered yes to two or more of these health questions.</p>

<h2 id="transamerica-company-overview"><br><strong>TransAmerica company overview </strong></h2>

<p>Transamerica was founded in 1905 in San Francisco by an Italian American entrepreneur, Amadeo Giannini. Transamerica was created as a holding <a href="https://www.transamerica.com/individual/what-we-offer/products/insurance/final-expense-life/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">company</a> after the merger of Bank of Italy with Bank of America in 1928.</p>

<p>In 1930, the company purchased Occidental Life Insurance and started offering life insurance. Life insurance became their flagship product and remained the biggest part of the company’s operation. Their life insurance products include term life, whole life, final expense, and other supplemental products like long-term care and accidental death insurance.</p>

<p>In 1956, the company became Transamerica Corporation. It became a part of one of the world’s leading financial institutions Aegon in 1999. The company recently acquired two other life insurance companies, Monumental Life and Stonebridge Life. These companies are now offering Transamerica Premier Life Insurance burial insurance products.</p>

<p>Today, Transamerica is involved in banking, airlines, movies, and the car rental business. </p>
