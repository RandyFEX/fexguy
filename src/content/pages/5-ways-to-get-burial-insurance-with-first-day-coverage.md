<h2 id="5-ways-to-get-burial-insurance-with-first-day-coverage"><br><strong>5 Ways To Get Burial Insurance With First-day Coverage</strong></h2>

<h2 id="1-answer-the-health-questions"><br><strong>#1 Answer The Health Questions</strong></h2>

<p>The number one way to get burial insurance with first-day coverage is to answer health questions. Simplified issue life insurance that asks health questions offers first-day coverage.</p>

<p>You don’t need to undergo a medical exam, but you will have to answer the health questions. Asking health questions is the insurance company’s way of gauging your risk. It is important, to be honest about your health condition. Lying on an insurance application constitutes fraud. Insurance fraud is a serious crime, and it may cause the insurance company to deny the beneficiary of your death benefits.</p>

<p>By answering the health questions, you can qualify for first-day coverage and save on your monthly premium.</p>

<p>Most insurance companies are lenient on underwriting, and many health issues are considered for first-day coverage. However, some health issues are such a high risk that the insurance companies don’t want to take the risk of offering first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>The following health issues are accepted for 1st-day coverage or benefits with most policies:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Aneurisms</li>
<li>Asthma</li>
<li>Bipolar</li>
<li>Blood clots</li>
<li>Bronchitis</li>
<li>Cancer in the past</li>
<li>COPD with some companies</li>
<li>Diabetes &amp; diabetic medications like Metformin, Insulin, and others</li>
<li>Diabetic neuropathy</li>
<li>Gout</li>
<li>Heart defibrillator or pacemaker</li>
<li>High Blood Pressure &amp; HBP medications</li>
<li>Pain medications</li>
<li>Schizophrenia</li>
<li>Seizures</li>
<li>Surgeries</li>
<li>Ulcerative colitis</li>
<li>Urinary issues</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>The following health issues may result in a waiting period:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>AIDS, ARC, or HIV</li>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Angina within the last 12 months</li>
<li>Recent cancer and cancer treatment</li>
<li>Confined to hospital, hospice care, or nursing home</li>
<li>Diabetic coma within the last 24 months</li>
<li>Heart attack within the previous 24 months</li>
<li>Heart or circulatory surgery within the last 24 months</li>
<li>Oxygen Use except for sleep apnea</li>
<li>Organ transplant or been advised to receive an organ transplant</li>
<li>Renal failure, dialysis</li>
<li>Terminal illness</li>
</ul>

<p>If you have some health issues, the best way to get life insurance with first-day coverage is to find an insurance company that never asks about your health issue. Unfortunately, you can’t do it on your own.</p>

<p>You need to speak to an independent insurance agent knowledgeable in insurance underwriting. Your agent will ask about your health information to determine the company that accepts multiple health conditions and will give you first-day coverage.</p>

<h2 id="2-avoid-insurance-companies-offering-burial-insurance-on-tv"><br><strong>#2 Avoid Insurance Companies Offering Burial Insurance On Tv</strong></h2>

<p>The number two way to get burial insurance with first-day coverage is to avoid insurance companies that offer burial insurance on TV.</p>

<p>Most insurance companies that have insurance commercials on television typically offer insurance that comes with a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>To guarantee insurance coverage for everybody regardless of their health conditions, these companies with television commercials offer guaranteed issue life insurance with a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance only offers first-day coverage for accidental death. However, it does not cover the natural causes of death for the first two years. You must live for the first two years before your beneficiary will receive the full death benefit.</p>

<h2 id="3-avoid-junk-mail-burial-insurance"><br><strong>#3 Avoid Junk Mail Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p>The number three way to get burial insurance with first-day coverage is to avoid junk mail burial insurance.</p>

<p>Junk mail burial insurance doesn’t require a licensed agent and asks no health questions. No wonder these policies are 30% to 367% higher than burial insurance that asks health questions and offers first-day coverage.</p>

<p>Burial insurance companies that advertise through the mail never ask health questions; when an insurance company never asks health questions, it only sells guaranteed issue life insurance with a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>If you want first-day life insurance coverage, avoid burial insurance in the mail. Always go for life insurance that asks health questions. By simply answering health questions, you can qualify for first-day coverage. You also won’t be paying extra money for higher premiums.</p>

<h2 id="4-never-settle-for-2-year-waiting-period"><br><strong>#4 Never Settle For 2-Year Waiting Period If You Can Qualify For First-day Coverage</strong></h2>

<p>Always request first-day coverage insurance from your life insurance agent. Even if you have some health issues, do not settle for a two-year waiting period if you can qualify for first-day coverage.</p>

<p>The television and junk mail life insurance advertisers often prey on uninformed seniors. They make people believe that the two-year waiting period is a standard part of burial insurance. I’m here to tell you that nothing can be further from the truth.</p>

<p>Every life insurance company has different underwriting criteria. Some health issues will be declined by one, while others will accept it. The secret to getting first-day coverage is knowing which life insurance company will accept your health issues, which leads us to the next tip.</p>

<h2 id="what-are-the-different-insurance-benefit-periods"><br><strong>What Are The Different Insurance Benefit Periods?</strong></h2>

<p><strong>1. NO WAITING PERIOD</strong></p>

<p>This is the best burial insurance option, giving immediate coverage with a 100% benefit payout from day one. If you qualify for burial insurance with no waiting period, your beneficiary will receive the full death benefit even after making only one payment on your policy.</p>

<p>The good news is many people qualify for burial insurance with first-day coverage no waiting period even when they have some health issues.</p>

<p><br><strong>2. PARTIAL WAITING PERIOD</strong></p>

<p>Burial insurance with a partial waiting period offers immediate coverage but with a phase-in benefit schedule. This plan is also called a graded benefit.</p>

<p>The graded benefit plan works this way:</p>

<p>The insurance company will pay 30-50% of the coverage amount if you die during the first year. If you die during the 13-24th months, your beneficiary will receive 60-70% of the coverage amount. After 24 months, they will pay 100% death benefit to your beneficiary.</p>

<p>There are some health issues where a partial waiting period is your best option for insurance coverage.</p>

<p>These health issues may result in a partial waiting period or graded plan:</p>

<ul>
<li>Amputation due to diabetics</li>
<li><a href="https://www.cancer.org/research/cancer-facts-statistics/all-cancer-facts-figures/cancer-facts-figures-2020.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Cancer</a> within the last 24 months</li>
<li>Congestive heart failure</li>
<li>Treatment for alcohol or drug abuse</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>3. TWO-YEAR WAITING PERIOD (Guaranteed issue life insurance)</strong></p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance offers immediate coverage for the accidental cause of death. However, it has a two-year waiting period for death’s health-related or natural cause.</p>

<p>If you die during the two-year waiting period, your beneficiary will only receive a return of premium plus 7-10% interest.</p>

<p>The insurance carrier will not ask health questions with the guaranteed issue life insurance. Your application is guaranteed to be approved regardless of your health condition.</p>

<p>If you have been diagnosed with a chronic illness, terminal illness, or you are in poor health. This plan may be your only option for coverage.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">A pre-existing waiting period is required before a new life insurance policy pays out the death benefit if the policyholder has a pre-existing medical condition.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>Do all life insurance policies have a waiting period?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">No, not all life insurance policies have a waiting period. Some life insurance policies, such as first-day coverage life insurance, do not have a waiting period.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>What is a 2 year waiting period for life insurance?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">A 2 year waiting period for life insurance is the amount of time required before a new life insurance policy pays out the death benefit. The waiting period is generally two years for guaranteed issue-only life insurance policies (which we do not recommend purchasing in most cases).</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>What is the effective date of an insurance policy?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The effective date of an insurance policy is the date on which coverage begins.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>How fast does insurance kick in?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The effective date of your insurance will depend on the type of policy you purchased.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>How do you get around a waiting period for insurance?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The best way to prevent a waiting period is to buy life insurance when you are younger and healthier. This way, you will not have to worry about a pre-existing condition or any other health issues that could cause a waiting period.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>Does AIG life insurance have a waiting period?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">Yes, AIG has a two-year waiting period because they only sell guaranteed issue life insurance.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>What are the risks of having a waiting period in insurance?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The main risk of waiting is that you may die during the waiting period, and your beneficiaries will not receive any death benefits.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>How soon does life insurance take effect?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The effective date of your insurance will depend on the type of policy you purchased.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>Why do you need to answer health questions to get first-day coverage?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The best way to get burial insurance with first-day coverage is to choose a policy that asks health questions. Insurance companies that ask health questions offer first-day coverage insurance.</p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d"><br><strong>What type of insurance do most companies advertise on TV?</strong></p>

<p id="8213c6f6-868b-43ee-8a16-a16cad89576d">The most popular type of insurance advertised on TV is a guaranteed issue life insurance that never asks health questions and has a two-year waiting period. We do not recommend these types of plans.</p>
