<h1>Senior Benefits</h1>

<p>Final Expense Guy Senior Benefits division helps people find and qualify for 1st-day coverage burial, cremation, and final expense life insurance.</p>

<p><strong>In order to qualify you must meet 3 criteria: </strong></p>

<ol>
<li>You must be between the age ag 50 and 79 years old</li>
<li>You must be a US Citizen or permanent resident</li>
<li>You must have a valid checking or savings account for the billing with a bank or credit union for payment.</li>
</ol>

<p>If you qualify, we are will need to ask you some questions to determine what plans and pricing you will qualify for. </p>

<p>Approvals are all done over the phone, and no money is required to get approved.</p>

<p>Even if you have multiple health problems, we will do our best to get you qualified for 1st-day coverage or benefits. If we cant help you, we will do our best to refer you to a higher-risk company that may be a better option for you based on your age and overall health. </p>

<p><strong>Here are some medical conditions that we cannot get people approved for 1st-day coverage or benefits:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>ADLs – unable to perform bathing, dressing, eating, transfering, toiletring, continence </li>
<li>Alzheimers</li>
<li>Cancer diagnosis or treatment in the last 24 months</li>
<li>Confined to bed </li>
<li>Daily oxygen use</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Hospice patients</li>
<li>Hospitalized (currently in hospital)</li>
<li>Kidney dialysis</li>
<li>Terminal illness</li>
</ul>
