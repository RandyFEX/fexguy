<h1>Foresters Burial Insurance Review – Pros &amp; Cons</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:<br>• </strong>Foresters offers solid coverage, but often isn’t the cheapest option<br>• Some plans include waiting periods before full benefits actually pay<br>• Coverage amounts are smaller compared to traditional life insurance policies<br>• You must go through an agent, no easy online quotes or signup<br>• Customer complaints and slow claims processes show up in reviews<br>• Better companies may offer lower rates for the same coverage</p>

<p>Foresters burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed to cover final expenses, usually with smaller coverage amounts like $5,000 to $35,000. It’s easier to qualify for, with simplified underwriting and no medical exam in many cases, which makes it appealing if your health isn’t perfect. The tradeoff is cost and flexibility. Some policies include waiting periods, and pricing can be higher than other carriers offering similar coverage. It’s a decent option in certain situations, but rarely the best value if you actually shop around.</p>

<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>What to Ask Before Buying Foresters Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<h3 id="h-what-insurance-plans-do-foresters-offer"><strong>What Insurance Plans Do Foresters Offer?</strong></h3>

<p>Foresters burial insurance plans are called PlanRight whole life insurance.</p>

<p>It is a simplified issue insurance plan with no medical exam. You only need to answer a set of health questions to qualify.</p>

<p>There are three benefit options that you can qualify for, depending on your health status, and the death benefit can be either level, graded, or modified.</p>

<h3>#1 – <strong>PLANRIGHT LEVEL DEATH BENEFIT PLAN</strong></h3>

<p>This is a reasonably good option in certain situations and can be competitively priced for specific health issues.</p>

<p><strong>Features:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Lifetime coverage</li>
<li>Level premium</li>
<li>Available in all 50 states</li>
<li>Maximum death benefit: $35,000</li>
<li>Benefit schedule: First-day coverage, no waiting period</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer NO to all the health questions and are clear with the prescription history check, you will qualify for the Foresters level death benefit plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<p>Once approved, 100% of the death benefits will be paid to your beneficiary when you die. The maximum death benefit you can buy is $35,000.</p>

<p>The level death benefit plan is their best plan. If you are generally healthy, you can qualify for this plan. This plan is also designed to accept minor health conditions for first-day coverage.</p>

<h3><br><strong>#2 –</strong> <strong>PLANRIGHT GRADED DEATH BENEFIT PLAN</strong></h3>

<p>The graded death benefit by Foresters is often overly expensive for most people, and we would rarely even consider recommending this coverage option.</p>

<p><strong>Features:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Lifetime coverage</li>
<li>Level premium</li>
<li>Maximum Face amount: $20,000</li>
<li>Available in all 50 states</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Death Benefit schedule:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-year – 30%</li>
<li>Second-year – 70%</li>
<li>Third-year – 100%</li>
</ul>

<p>To qualify for the graded death benefit plan, you must answer no to all the questions in the knockout section and no to all the questions in the modified section. </p>

<p>You will notice that all the questions here are related to chronic illnesses. If you answer yes to any questions, you will be approved for the graded death benefit plan.</p>

<h3><br><strong>#3 –</strong> <strong>PLANRIGHT MODIFIED DEATH BENEFIT PLAN</strong></h3>

<p>This is a terrible plan price-wise, which costs more than other plans with two-year waiting periods. </p>

<p><strong>Features:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>2-year waiting period</li>
<li>Lifetime coverage</li>
<li>Level premium</li>
<li>Maximum Face amount: $15,000</li>
<li>Available in all 50 states</li>
</ul>

<p>PlanRight Modified death benefit plan is designed for people with significant health problems.</p>

<p>The maximum face amount you can buy is only $15,000. The death benefit is limited for the first two years of the policy. If you die from a natural cause during the first two years, your beneficiary will only receive a return of your premiums plus 10% interest.</p>

<p>Foresters will only pay 100% death benefit during the waiting period for accidental death. You must outlive the&#160;<strong>two-year waiting period</strong>&#160;for your beneficiary to receive your full death benefit.</p>

<p>In our opinion, getting a guaranteed issue no-health questions policy will be a much better choice than accepting a Foresters Modified plan.</p>

<h3 id="h-can-you-get-foresters-burial-insurance-policy-with-no-waiting-period"><strong>Can You Get Foresters Burial Insurance Policy With No Waiting Period?</strong></h3>

<p>YES, Foresters offers a first-day coverage plan with no waiting period with their level coverage option.</p>

<p>If you qualify, the PlanRight level death benefit plan gives you full coverage from the first day. Your beneficiary will receive your full death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<h3 id="h-does-foresters-have-any-hidden-fine-print-in-their-policy-gi-amp-graded-only"><strong>Does Foresters Have Any Hidden “Fine Print” In Their Policy? (GI &amp; GRADED ONLY)</strong></h3>

<p>Foresters don’t have any “fine print” clauses in their policies. Just be aware that their two lower-tier plans have waiting periods that many life insurance agents don’t explain very well.</p>

<p>Also, make sure you read your policy once you get it in the mail to verify the agent who helped you answered all the health questions correctly.</p>

<h2 id="other-benefits"><strong>Are There Any Riders Or Other Benefits With Foresters Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<h3><strong>Foresters Riders</strong></h3>

<p>Foresters automatically include up to three riders with your base coverage, with no additional premium.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Accelerated Death Benefit Rider: </strong>“(for terminal illness) may allow the owner to accelerate a portion of the death benefit and receive a payment if the insured is diagnosed with a terminal illness.”</li>
<li><strong>Common Carrier Accidental Death Rider:</strong> “May provide an additional benefit if death is due to an accident or accidental injury that happens while riding as a fare-paying passenger on a common carrier such as a plane, bus, or train.”</li>
<li><strong>Family Health Benefit Rider:</strong> “May provide a payment to help cover some family health expenses (such as an ambulance ride) that occur as a result of certain natural disasters.”</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Foresters Other Benefits</strong></h3>

<ul>
<li>You can add, at an extra cost, an optional Accidental Death Rider to PlanRight preferred (with a level death benefit), which can pay an additional benefit in case of accidental death.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="getting-approved"><strong>How Do I Get Approved By Foresters?</strong></h2>

<p>Foresters’ 1st-day coverage life insurance will ask if you have ever had some health issues, or had other health issues in the last 12, 24, or 36 months.</p>

<h3 id="h-what-are-foresters-burial-insurance-application-questions"><strong>What Are Foresters Burial Insurance Application Questions?</strong></h3>

<p><strong>Here are the questions you will find in the application:</strong></p>

<p><strong>PART A: KNOCKOUT QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>Medical Questions (For purposes of these questions, “you” means the proposed insured, “diagnosed,” “advised,” “tested” and “treatment” mean by a licensed physician or medical practitioner, and “terminal illness” means an illness that would reasonably be expected to cause death within 12 months.)</p>

<p><strong>1.</strong>&#160;Are you currently: a resident in a nursing home&#160;or skilled nursing facility; a patient in a hospital&#160;or psychiatric facility; receiving, or have been advised to receive, skilled nursing care, hospice care, or home healthcare; confined to a correctional facility? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>2.</strong>&#160;Do you require a wheelchair&#160;due to a chronic illness or disease, or do you require assistance (from anyone) with activities of daily living&#160;such as taking medications, bathing, dressing, eating, or toileting? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>3</strong>.&#160;<strong>a)</strong>&#160;Within the past 12 months, have you: a) Used or been advised to use oxygen equipment to assist with breathing (excluding use for sleep apnea) or had or been advised to have kidney dialysis? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>b)</strong>&#160;Been advised to have surgery, hospitalization, or a diagnostic test (excluding tests related to the Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)) which has not yet been started, completed, or for which results are not known?</p>

<p><strong>c)</strong>&#160;Ever been diagnosed with Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) or AIDS Related Complex (ARC) or tested positive for Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>d)</strong>&#160;Ever received or been advised to receive an organ or tissue transplant? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>e)</strong>&#160;Have you ever been diagnosed with Alzheimer’s disease, dementia, Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), or a terminal illness? (Terminal illness is defined as any illness diagnosed that would reasonably be expected to cause death within 12 months.) (Yes – No)</p>

<p>If you answer “YES” to any of the following questions, your application for coverage will be denied, and you will be advised not to complete or submit the application.</p>

<p>If you answer “NO” to all the questions in the knockout section, you need to answer the questions in the modified section to determine what plan you will qualify for.</p>

<p>You also need to circle the conditions to which you answered “YES.”</p>

<p><br><strong>PART B: MODIFIED DEATH BENEFIT HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>If all “No” answers in Part A, complete Part B. Complete all questions and circle the condition(s) to which each “Yes” answer, if any, applies.</p>

<p><strong>a)</strong>&#160;Within the past two years, have you had, or been diagnosed with, or received or been advised to receive treatment or medication for: Alchohol or drug abuse, or have you used illegal drugs? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>b)</strong>&#160;Within the past 2 years, had heart or circulatory surgery, or had or been diagnosed as having a heart attack, congestive heart failure, cardiomyopathy, stroke, Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA), aneurysm, or brain tumor? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>c)</strong>&#160;Within the past 2 years, been diagnosed with or had treatment for a diabetic coma, insulin shock, or had an amputation due to complications of diabetes?</p>

<p><strong>d)</strong>&#160;Within the past 3 years, been diagnosed with or had treatment for cancer (excluding basal skin cancer)? (Yes – No)</p>

<p>If all “No” answers, complete Part C. Complete all questions and circle the condition(s) to which each “Yes” answer if any, applies.</p>

<p><strong>If you answered “Yes” to any questions in Part B, select Foresters PlanRight with a modified death benefit.</strong></p>

<p><br><strong>PART C: GRADED DEATH BENEFIT</strong></p>

<p><strong>1.</strong>&#160;Within the past 2 years, has the proposed insured been diagnosed with, prescribed medication for, or had or been advised to have treatment for:</p>

<p><strong>a)</strong>&#160;Parkinson’s disease or Systemic Lupus (SLE)? (Yes – No)</p>

<p><strong>b)</strong>&#160;Cirrhosis of the liver, chronic hepatitis or other liver disorder, kidney failure, or other chronic kidney diseases?</p>

<p><strong>c)</strong>&#160;Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) or emphysema? (Yes – No)</p>

<p>If a “Yes” answer is in Part C, select Foresters PlanRight (With a graded death benefit) in Section 4.</p>

<p>If all “No” answers, select Foresters PlanRight (With a level death benefit) in Section 4.</p>

<p><strong>Note:</strong>&#160;Your application may still be declined in this section if your prescription history shows medications that are a red flag on the conditions asked in this section. Don’t worry, though. We have other more lenient companies with your medical condition and can get you approved for coverage.</p>
