<h2 id="does-moo-offer-first-day-coverage"><strong>Does Mutual Of Omaha Offer First-day Coverage?</strong></h2>

<p>Yes, Mutual of Omaha has a burial insurance plan with first-day coverage for their <strong>“Level Death Benefit”</strong> plan.  If you qualify, you will be fully covered from the first day.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, their <strong>“Graded Benefit”</strong> plan has a 2-year waiting period. </p>

<p>Their burial insurance plan is called the Living Promise plan. It has a level death benefit that can be used for burial, cremation, final expenses, or basic life insurance needs.  They have fairly lenient underwriting, and you can qualify for first-day coverage if you have some health issues.</p>

<h2 id="pros-of-mutual-of-omaha"><strong>Pros Of Mutual Of Omaha Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>First-day coverage </strong>– The Living Promise level benefit plan comes with first-day coverage and no waiting period.</p>

<p><strong>Competitive pricing</strong> – their level benefit plan has low pricing.</p>

<p><strong>Accelerated death benefit rider – </strong>available with level benefit plan.</p>

<p><strong>Application process</strong> – In-person or digital online application completed by a licensed agent.</p>

<h2 id="cons-of-mutual-of-omaha"><strong>Cons Of Mutual Of Omaha </strong></h2>

<p><strong>Has build chart</strong> – You may not qualify if you are on the heavy side (obese or morbidly obese).</p>

<p><strong>Waiting period</strong> – The graded benefit plan comes with a 2-year waiting period.</p>

<p><strong>Not all health issues will qualify</strong> – significant medical conditions can be denied even with the graded plan.</p>

<h2 id="mutual-of-omaha-riders"><br><strong>Mutual Of Omaha Burial Insurance Riders</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Living Promise Level Benefit Plan Only</strong></p>

<p>Accelerated death benefit for terminal illness or nursing home confinement rider (not available in New York) can be accessed under these circumstances:</p>

<ul>
<li>If the insured is diagnosed with a terminal illness, that will result in death within 12 months or less.</li>
<li>If the insured is confined to a nursing home for 90 consecutive days and expected to be confined their whole life.</li>
<li>If the insured cannot perform at least two of the activities of daily living because of chronic illness.</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>Optional:</strong> Accidental Death Benefit Rider</p>

<p>This rider would double the death benefit value if the insured died from an accident.</p>

<p><br><strong>OTHER BENEFITS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>POLICY LOANS </strong>– you can borrow up to 100% of your cash value. This is tax-free. Loans reduce your cash value amount and are subtracted from your death benefit. The interest on policy loans varies by state.</p>

<p>A graded plan should be your last option. You should only apply for this policy if you have significant health issues. Other than that, let our agents at Final Expense Guy review your health history to get a reasonable price and full first-day coverage!</p>

<h2 id="moo-underwriting-guidelines"><br><strong>Mutual Of Omaha Underwriting Guidelines</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Mutual of Omaha will determine your eligibility through the following:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Your answers to their health questions</li>
<li>Medical Information Bureau (MIB) records</li>
<li>Your height and weight</li>
<li>Your prescription history</li>
</ul>

<p>There are two sets of health questions with Omaha Mutual Life Insurance Company.</p>

<p><strong>The “knockout” questions can be found in the first part of the application.</strong> A yes answer to the knockout questions will disqualify you from the level death benefit plan.</p>

<p>The second set of health questions is for the graded benefit plan. If you answer yes to any of these conditions, you will be approved for the graded benefit plan.</p>

<p><br><strong>THE KNOCKOUT QUESTIONS FOR MUTUAL OF OMAHA ARE</strong>:</p>

<p><strong>Is the applicant currently:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Bedridden or confined to any hospital, nursing home, long-term care facility, or skilled nursing facility; or receiving or been advised to receive care in a nursing home, hospice care, or home health care?</li>
<li>Requiring assistance with activities of daily living such as taking medications, bathing, dressing, eating, toileting, getting in and out of a chair or bed, or control of bowel or bladder problems?</li>
<li>Requiring any of the following (other than for fractures, bone, or joint surgery, including replacement): wheelchair, electric scooter, or oxygen equipment to assist breathing (excluding use for sleep apnea)?</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>Has the Proposed Insured ever been:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Diagnosed as having Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-Related Complex (ARC), or Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection (symptomatic or asymptomatic) or been treated for AIDS, ARC, or HIV by a physician or healthcare provider?</li>
<li>Diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised by a physician or health care provider to receive treatment for Alzheimer’s Disease, Dementia, Huntington’s Disease, Sickle Cell Anemia, Myelodysplastic Syndrome (MDS), Lou Gehrig’s Disease (ALS), Quadriplegia, Paraplegia, Down’s Syndrome, mental incapacity, congestive heart failure, Cirrhosis, Metastatic Cancer or recurrent Cancer of the same type?</li>
<li>Diagnosed with insulin shock, diabetic coma, or had an amputation due to diabetic complications or diagnosed with End-Stage Renal Disease or requiring dialysis?</li>
<li>Advised to receive or have received an organ or bone marrow transplant?</li>
<li>Diagnosed by a physician or health care provider as having a terminal medical condition that is expected to result in death within the next twelve 12 months?</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>In the past 12 months, has the Proposed Insured been:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Advised by a physician to have a surgical operation, diagnostic testing other than for routine screening purposes or for those related to HIV/AIDS, treatment, hospitalization, or other procedure which has not been done or for which results are not known?</li>
<li>Diagnosed by a physician or health care provider as having heart disease or heart surgery of any kind?</li>
<li>In the past 2 years, has the Proposed Insured been diagnosed with, been treated for, or advised by a physician or health care provider to receive treatment for any form of cancer (except basal or squamous cell skin cancer)</li>
</ul>

<p>Your reply to these health questions will determine if you will qualify for the level death benefit plan.</p>

<p>Mutual of Omaha will also examine your prescription history and MIB records as part of their risk analysis.</p>

You will need to speak with a representative, provide your personal information and policy number, and request they cancel your plan. You will receive a confirmation email about the cancellation afterward.</p>
