That waiting period matters because if you pass early, your family won’t get the full payout. That’s why picking the right company the first time matters. <h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-cystic-fibrosis"><strong>What is my best insurance option if I have a history of cystic fibrosis?</strong></h2>

<p>If you have cystic fibrosis, your best option for insurance coverage is a level death benefit plan.</p>

<p>Best Option: Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p>You will be fully covered from the first day and have no waiting period. When you pass away, your beneficiaries will receive 100% of the death benefit.</p>

<p><br><strong>CYSTIC FIBROSIS WITH SUPPLEMENTAL OXYGEN USE</strong></p>

<p>For cystic fibrosis patients with other medical conditions or supplemental oxygen use, you will qualify for guaranteed acceptance life insurance.</p>

<p>Best Option: Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p>Guaranteed acceptance life insurance does not require a medical exam or health questions. It has a two-year waiting period. If you die during the first two years of the policy, your beneficiary will get all the premiums that you paid, plus an additional 10%. They would only get the full death benefit if you die after 24 months.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>Do I need a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with cystic fibrosis.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple, and you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-cystic-fibrosis"><br><strong>Burial insurance underwriting if you have cystic fibrosis</strong></h2>

<p>Burial insurance companies conduct underwriting by asking health questions and checking your prescription history to verify your medical condition.</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>You will typically see Cystic fibrosis asked this way:</p>

<ul>
<li>Have you ever been diagnosed with or been treated for Cystic fibrosis or other <a href="/burial-insurance/lung-disease/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">lung diseases</a>?</li>
<li>Have you ever been treated for any chronic <a href="https://www.cff.org/What-is-CF/About-Cystic-Fibrosis/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">lung conditions</a>?</li>
<li>Within the past 24 months, have you been medically diagnosed or treated or taken medication for cystic fibrosis or required oxygen equipment to assist in breathing?</li>
</ul>

<p>Some burial insurance companies consider Cystic fibrosis as a chronic lung condition like <a href="/burial-insurance/asthma/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">asthma</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance/copd/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">COPD</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance/emphysema/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">emphysema</a>, and <a href="/burial-insurance/chronic-bronchitis/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">chronic bronchitis</a>. If you have cystic fibrosis, you need to answer “yes” to the lung disease question since cystic fibrosis is a lung disease.</p>

<p>We work with insurance companies that don’t even ask about cystic fibrosis on the health questionnaire. Insurance companies that don’t ask about this medical condition will easily approve your application with first-day coverage.</p>

<p id="WHAT-MEDICATION-DO-THE-INSURANCE-COMPANIES-LOOK-FOR-WHEN-I-HAVE-CYSTIC-FIBROSIS"><strong>PRESCRIPTION HISTORY CHECK:</strong></p>

<p>Insurance companies will electronically review your medication history to verify your health conditions.</p>

<p>There are certain medications they will be on the lookout for when it comes to cystic fibrosis.</p>

<p><strong>Here’s a list of common CF medications that, if taken, will be considered a cystic fibrosis patient.</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Ivacaftor (Kalydeco)</li>
<li>Dornase Alfa (Pulmozyme)</li>
<li>Orkambi</li>
<li>Antibiotics to treat and prevent lung infection</li>
<li>Anti-inflammatory medications to reduce airway swelling</li>
<li>Mucus-thinning agents to help keep airway passages clear</li>
<li>Oral pancreatic enzymes help absorb nutrients in the digestive system</li>
<li>Inhaled bronchodilators</li>
</ul>

<p>Taking Cystic fibrosis medications will not make you ineligible for burial insurance coverage. Most of the medications and treatments are a complete non-issue with burial insurance companies.</p>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions"><strong>  Frequently asked questions </strong></h2>

<p><strong>Can I get life insurance with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, people with cystic fibrosis can get life insurance. They may even qualify for a first-day coverage plan if they do not use supplemental oxygen daily.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is cystic fibrosis a pre existing condition?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, cystic fibrosis is a pre-existing condition if it is diagnosed before you apply for life insurance coverage.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is cystic fibrosis a chronic or terminal illness?</strong></p>

<p>Cystic fibrosis is a genetic disease of the mucus glands affecting children’s and adults’ respiratory and digestive systems. Cystic fibrosis is a chronic illness that can get worse over time.</p>

<p><br><strong>What kills you when you have cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Cystic fibrosis can be life-threatening. People with cystic fibrosis die because of respiratory failure.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can I get burial insurance with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, you can get burial insurance even if you have cystic fibrosis. If you are 50-85 years old, you may easily qualify for a first-day coverage plan if you do not use supplemental oxygen daily.</p>

<p><br><strong>Will cystic fibrosis ever be cured?</strong></p>

<p>Unfortunately, there are no cures for cystic fibrosis. However, many treatments can help ease the symptoms, slow the progression of the disease, and improve the quality of life.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is cystic fibrosis considered immunocompromised?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, people with cystic fibrosis are considered immunocompromised because they have a weak immune system, especially those who had lung transplants. People with cystic fibrosis have an increased risk of severe illness from Covid.</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens to the lungs with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>People with cystic fibrosis have thickened mucus in the lungs, pancreas, and other organs. The mucus in the lungs can block the airways and create lung damage in the long run.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can I get funeral insurance with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, you can get funeral insurance if you have cystic fibrosis. Many life insurance companies offer life insurance coverage to people with cystic fibrosis and other lung diseases.</p>

<p><br><strong>How do insurance companies know if you have a pre-existing condition?</strong></p>

<p>Life insurance companies offering first-day coverage plans ask health questions on the application. They can also access your medical records through the Medical Information Bureau.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is cystic fibrosis always fatal?</strong></p>

<p>Cystic fibrosis can be fatal because it tends to get worse over time. A serious lung infection can be fatal because they can force the lungs to stop working properly.</p>

<p><br><strong>How long can you live with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>People with cystic fibrosis who live to adulthood may live for about 44 years.</p>

<p><br><strong>What happens if cystic fibrosis is left untreated?</strong></p>

<p>If cystic fibrosis is left untreated, people with cystic fibrosis may develop a chest infection and chronic diarrhea.&#160;</p>

<p><br><strong>Can I get cremation insurance with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, people with cystic fibrosis (CF) can get cremation insurance. Many insurance companies offer coverage to people with cystic fibrosis, they may even qualify for a first-day coverage plan.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can CF go away?</strong></p>

<p>Sadly, there is no cure for cystic fibrosis. Modern treatments can ease symptoms and improve the quality of life. Early diagnosis and treatment can slow the progression of this disease and prolong life.</p>

<p><br><strong>What triggers cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Cystic fibrosis is caused by the mutation of a gene called cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator. If this gene is not working properly cystic fibrosis may get worse.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is there a mild form of cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, atypical cystic fibrosis is a milder form of cystic fibrosis. Individuals with atypical cystic fibrosis might only have mild dysfunction in the organ systems.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can you get cystic fibrosis at any age?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, cystic fibrosis may manifest at any age. Some adults with no symptoms during childhood may still be diagnosed with cystic fibrosis later in life.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can I get final expense insurance with cystic fibrosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, you can get final expense insurance with cystic fibrosis. Final expense insurance is available to people who are 50-85 years old.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can cystic fibrosis go undiagnosed?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, atypical cystic fibrosis or a mild form of CF can remain undiagnosed until adulthood. Cystic fibrosis is often diagnosed during the early years, but people diagnosed with CF in adulthood have a higher life expectancy than those diagnosed during childhood.</p>

<p><br><strong>How does cystic fibrosis affect the body?</strong></p>

<p>Cystic fibrosis can cause thick mucus in the lungs, pancreas, and intestines. This condition can lead to malnutrition, frequent respiratory infections, and breathing problems.</p>

<p><br><strong>Is cystic fibrosis autoimmune disease?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, cystic fibrosis is considered an autoimmune disease because it can cause the body to attack its tissue.</p>
