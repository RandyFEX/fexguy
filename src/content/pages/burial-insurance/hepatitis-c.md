<h1>Burial Insurance with Hepatitis C</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong></p>

<p>• Hepatitis C doesn’t disqualify you, but liver damage drives decisions<br>• Treated or cured cases can qualify for better first-day coverage<br>• Active or severe cases often lead to higher costs or waiting periods<br>• Guaranteed issue plans cost more and delay full payout for 2 years<br>• Applying wrong can get you declined or stuck with worse options</p>

<p>Getting burial insurance with Hepatitis C depends on your current liver health and treatment status. Burial insurance, which is a type of whole life insurance, is often available even with this condition. If you’ve been treated successfully and your liver function is stable, you may qualify for immediate coverage at better rates. If the condition is active or there’s liver damage like cirrhosis, you’re more likely to be placed into a higher-cost policy with a waiting period. The key mistake people make is applying without matching their exact health profile to the right company.</p>

<h2 id="best-option-for-people-with-hepatitis-c"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Hepatitis C?</strong></h2>

<p>Your ability to qualify for burial insurance with Hepatitis C depends on the following factors:</p>

<ul>
<li>Date of your Hepatitis C diagnosis</li>
<li>The time that passed since your Hepatitis C treatment</li>
<li>Prescription medications taken</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>TREATMENT STOPPED WITHIN 2 YEARS</strong></p>

<p>If your treatment stopped two years ago because you recovered and your body was able to fight off the infection, you would qualify for the best plan and the lowest rate</p>

<p>Most life insurance companies that only have a two-year lookback period on Hepatitis C will offer you a level death benefit with first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best option:</strong> Level Death Benefit Plan</p>

<p>This plan with first-day coverage has a level premium that remains the same your whole life. The level death benefit plan is a simplified issue policy with no medical exam and just health questions to answer. Your death benefit will never decrease, and your beneficiary will receive a 100% death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<p><strong>CURRENT DIAGNOSIS AND TREATMENT (Less than 2 years)</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company asks about Hepatitis C within a minimum period of two years. If you were diagnosed with Hepatitis C in the last two years and still undergoing any type of treatment, your best option is first-day benefits.</p>

<p>A first-day benefit plan will also cover you from the first day but your death benefit payout is phased in over time.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> First-day benefits</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam"><br><strong>Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with Hepatitis C.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-hepatitis-c"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Hepatitis C</strong></h2>

<p>Burial insurance companies have two ways they conduct their underwriting (medical approval process).</p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong>&#160;– They may ask you a series of health questions.&#160;<strong>Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</strong></p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong>&#160;– They will electronically review your prescription history. If you are on Hepatitis C medications, this will tell them that you are being treated for Hepatitis C.</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>You will typically see Hepatitis C asked this way in the health questionnaire:</p>

<ul>
<li>During the past 24 months, have you been treated for liver disease?</li>
<li>Have you ever or been diagnosed with, or received or been advised to receive treatment or medication for hepatitis or liver disease?</li>
<li>Have you been diagnosed within the prior 2 years with or received, or been advised to receive treatment for Chronic hepatitis, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, or liver cirrhosis?</li>
</ul>

<p>In the health questionnaire, you will also notice that some insurance companies do not specifically ask about Hepatitis C. However, they ask if you have liver disease. You need to answer “yes” to the liver disease question since Hepatitis C is a form of liver disease.</p>
