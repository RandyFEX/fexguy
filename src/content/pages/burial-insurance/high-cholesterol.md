<h1>Burial Insurance with High Cholesterol</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• High cholesterol rarely disqualifies you from life insurance coverage<br>• Poor control can raise your rates or limit your policy options<br>• Some companies charge more even for mild or treated cases<br>• Applying with the wrong company can cost you better pricing<br>• Very high levels with other issues can lead to denial</p>

<p>Getting burial insurance with high cholesterol is usually easier than people think, but it depends on how well it’s controlled. Burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed for people with health conditions, and high cholesterol alone often won’t stop you from qualifying. In fact, many people with controlled cholesterol can still get standard or even better rates. The problem is when cholesterol is very high or combined with other health issues, which can raise your cost or limit your options. That’s why choosing the right company matters more than the condition itself.</p>

<h2 id="burial-insurance-underwriting-for-high-cholesterol"><strong>Burial insurance underwriting if you have high cholesterol</strong></h2>

<p>The insurance company will check your prescription history to verify your health and any history of high cholesterol levels.</p>

<p><strong>Some common medications prescribed for cholesterol control are:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Atorvastatin (Lipitor)</li>
<li>Fluvastatin (Lescol)</li>
<li>Pitavastatin (Livalo)</li>
<li>Pravastatin (Pravachol)</li>
<li>Rosuvastatin calcium (Crestor)</li>
<li>Simvastatin (Zocor)</li>
</ul>

<p>Taking any of these medications will not affect your burial insurance eligibility with most life insurance companies. If you are taking other prescription medications, additional underwriting will be necessary to qualify for 1st-day coverage.&#160;</p>
