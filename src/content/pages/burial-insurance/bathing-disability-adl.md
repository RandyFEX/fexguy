<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-need-help-with-bathing"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With Bathing?</strong></h2>

<p>Life insurance companies define bathing as the ability to clean the body using a sponge, tub, or shower and reaching different parts of the body to soap, rinse and dry.&#160;It also includes getting a basin of water and managing faucets.&#160; You must also be able to get in safely from the shower, shower chair, or bathtub.</p>

<p id="BURIAL-INSURANCE-OPTION-IF-YOU-HAVE-A-BATHING-DISABILITY-OR-NEED-HELP-WITH-ADL">To secure a new policy, if you need help with bathing, your best option is to buy a guaranteed issue burial insurance policy. You will qualify for this plan regardless of your bathing disability or needing help with activities of daily living.</p>

<p>A guaranteed issue (GI) burial insurance policy has no health questions or prescription history assessment of any kind. You don’t even have to take a medical exam to qualify for coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>WHAT IS GUARANTEED ISSUE LIFE INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>This coverage has a death limitation for a natural cause of death during the first two years of the policy. The company will pay a full death benefit only if you die from an accident during the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>If you die from illness, the insurance company will return all your premiums to your beneficiaries plus 10% interest. After the two-year waiting period, you are fully protected for life. At this point, the insurance company will pay the full death benefit to your beneficiaries if you die for any reason.</p>

<p>While it costs a bit more than insurance policies with underwriting, you will not have to worry about the possibility of being denied coverage because of your inability to dress on your own or perform any of the activities of daily living.</p>

<h2 id="why-you-need-guaranteed-issue-burial-insurance"><br><strong>Why Do You Need A Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance If You Need Help With Bathing?</strong></h2>

<p>We recommend buying guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with bathing and other <a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ADL</a>s because it’s the only life insurance plan you will qualify for because of your condition.</p>

<p>If you are relatively healthy, but you need help with ADLs, you can still have an average life expectancy and easily outlive the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>Avoid postponing your application for coverage because your health may worsen, and you could develop other health conditions such as diabetes or high blood pressure.</p>

<p>Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with bathing is a wise investment as you won’t lose money with this plan because your premiums will be returned plus interest if you die during the waiting period. Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance policy will cost much less in the long run than having no insurance coverage at all.</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>AGE REQUIREMENT</strong></p>

<p>Every final expense company has its age requirement, but most companies accept applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><br><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every insurance company has their insurance products licensed in every state. Make sure the company has a guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state before you buy.</p>

<p><br><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must possess the mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, if you have a severe cognitive impairment like Alzheimer’s disease or dementia and you are not able to legally consent to an agreement, the company may reject your application.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help with bathing or any ADLs.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-if-you-need-help-with-bathing"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With Bathing</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company with medical underwriting (health questions) will ask about bathing disability and your ability to perform activities of daily living in your life insurance application.</p>

<p><strong>You will always find burial insurance companies ask about ADL’s in the following way (or something very close to this):</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Are you currently receiving home health care, or require help with activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, and continence?</li>
<li>Do you currently need assistance with bathing, transferring, eating, toileting, dressing, or are you bedridden?</li>
<li>Are you scheduled for admission or currently confined to a nursing facility or assisted living facility?</li>
<li>Do you currently receive home health care, or in the last two years, have you received home health care more than three times?</li>
<li>Do you currently use a wheelchair due to a chronic illness or disease?</li>
</ul>

<p>Other applications won’t directly ask you if you need help with bathing or other ADLs, but the other questions will answer it for you. For example, if you need assistance with bathing, you will likely receive home health care services and live in a nursing home or assisted living facility.</p>

<p>If you answer “yes” to any of these questions, your application will automatically decline for first-day coverage insurance. However, you have another option for coverage called guaranteed issue burial insurance (which does not have any health questions).</p>

<p id="HOW-DOES-BATHING-DISABILITY-AFFECT-LIFE-INSURANCE"><br><strong>HOW DOES NEEDING HELP WITH BATHING AFFECT LIFE INSURANCE?</strong></p>

<p>Having a bathing disability or needing assistance from somebody else to take a bath will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the best-priced burial insurance policy.</p>

<p><strong>Bathing is an important and potentially challenging self-care activity, and needing help with bathing is associated with several health problems such as:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (<a href="https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis/symptoms-causes/syc-20354022#:~:text=Amyotrophic%20lateral%20sclerosis%20(a%2Dmy,who%20was%20diagnosed%20with%20it." target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ALS</a>)</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Cerebral palsy</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Heart attack</li>
<li>Injury</li>
<li>Kidney failure</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Parkinson’s disease</li>
<li>Spina bifida</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
</ul>

<p>Almost any of these medical conditions can prevent you from taking a bath on your own or performing any other ADLs.</p>

<p><strong>Life insurance providers will react in one of two ways when they learn you have a bathing disability or are unable to do ADL’s:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>They will automatically decline your application</li>
<li>They will offer guaranteed issue burial insurance with a two-year waiting period.</li>
</ul>

<p>No life insurance carrier will offer you first-day coverage during the first two years if you need help performing daily living activities.</p>
