<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-down-syndrome"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Down Syndrome?</strong></h2>

<p>Limitations on functionality and mobility have an impact on your eligibility for life insurance coverage. Insurance companies will consider the physical or mental impairment you may have.</p>

<p><strong>An applicant must have the mental and cognitive ability to understand policy details to be approved for a policy.</strong> Beyond these things, the insurance companies will also review your overall health before making a decision.</p>

<p>People with Down syndrome may have additional health issues like congenital heart disease as well as a life expectancy that is shorter than the average person. That is the reason why your life insurance option is limited to a guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p>With guaranteed issue burial insurance, you will have to wait two years before being covered for a natural cause of death. If you were to die during the waiting period, the company would refund all the premiums you paid plus interest.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="FACTORS-THAT-AFFECT-INSURANCE-ELIGIBILITY"><br><strong>OTHER FACTORS THAT MAY AFFECT INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY</strong></p>

<p><br><strong>ACTIVITIES OF DAILY LIVING</strong></p>

<p>Every final expense insurance company will ask if you need help with activities of daily living in their life insurance application. There are six ADLs used by life insurance companies to determine if you will qualify for coverage.</p>

<ol>
<li>Eating</li>
<li>Bathing</li>
<li>Dressing</li>
<li>Toileting</li>
<li>Transferring</li>
<li>Continence</li>
</ol>

<p>Most people with Downs syndrome need help with ADL’s, which means a guaranteed issue life insurance policy will be the best choice.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>DOWN SYNDROME AND MEDICATION</strong></p>

<p>The medication you use to treat your medical condition will have a direct impact on your level of insurability. Medication used to treat other underlying conditions, such as heart disease, gastrointestinal problems, and diabetes, may be a red flag to life insurance companies.</p>

<p><strong>The insurance company will want to know:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>What medications you are taking</li>
<li>Your amount of dosage</li>
<li>How long you have been taking the medication</li>
</ol>

<p>Additionally, inform them if you are taking drugs to treat other health issues unrelated to Down syndrome or mental health issues. These medications will also be considered during the underwriting process.</p>

<p><br><strong>DOWN SYNDROME, DEPRESSION, AND ANXIETY</strong></p>

<p>Depression and anxiety are two of the most frequently diagnosed mental health issues of people with Down syndrome. Adults often show symptoms of depression and lose the ability to enjoy many activities they used to love.</p>

<p>Most adults with Down syndrome who suffer from depression and anxiety may be taking more than one medication and receiving treatment from a psychiatrist. </p>

<p>A person with depression and anxiety can experience temporary hospitalization. If you have been hospitalized due to major depression, it will affect your burial insurance eligibility. In the case of severe or clinical depression with a disability, you are looking at guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><br><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER INTO A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must have financial decision ability and mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, if you have dementia or Alzheimer’s disease and cannot legally consent to an agreement, the company may reject your application.</p>

<p><strong>AGE LIMITATIONS</strong></p>

<p>Every funeral insurance company has its own age availability, but most guaranteed issue life insurance companies accept Down syndrome applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><br><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every burial insurance company has its products licensed in every state. Make sure the company you will apply to has a guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Have Down Syndrome, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with Down syndrome.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple, and you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-down-syndrome"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Down Syndrome</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p>You can hardly see Down syndrome asked in the health questionnaire. However, life insurance companies care about Down syndrome because people with Down syndrome can develop health <a href="https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/down-syndrome/symptoms-causes/syc-20355977" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">complications</a>. </p>

<p>People with Down syndrome also have a life expectancy that is shorter than the average person. According to the National Association for Down Syndrome, the average Down syndrome life expectancy is 60 years old.</p>

<p><strong>These health complications can include:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Congenital heart defects</li>
<li>Gastrointestinal defects</li>
<li>Hearing loss</li>

<h2 id="why-you-need-guaranteed-issue-life-insurance"><br><strong>Why Do You Need Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance If You Have Down Syndrome?</strong></h2>

<p>We recommend buying guaranteed issue burial insurance if you have Down syndrome because it’s the only life insurance plan you will qualify for.</p>

<p>Postponing your application for coverage is not a wise decision. Your health may get worse at any moment, and you could develop other health conditions such as heart disease or diabetes.</p>

<p>A guaranteed acceptance policy may be a little more expensive than plans with immediate coverage, but your approval is guaranteed. Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance if you have Down syndrome is worth it in the long run, you won’t lose money with this plan, and it cost much less than having no insurance coverage at all.</p>

<h2 id="benefits-of-guaranteed-issue-life-insurance"><br><strong>Benefits Of Guaranteed Issue Life Insurance </strong></h2>

<p><strong>EASY AND QUICK APPLICATION</strong></p>

<p>Very simple application process. When you apply for guaranteed issue life insurance, you will only need to provide basic personal information, beneficiary and payment information.</p>

<p>The application does not have any health-related questions on it. The company will never check your prescription history or your medical history or pull your medical records.</p>

<p>You will easily qualify for a guaranteed acceptance policy because all you need is the ability to sign your name on the application. You can sign over the phone or through your computer. We will send it to the insurance company, and in a few minutes, it will be approved; it’s easy!</p>

<p><br><strong>LEVEL PREMIUM</strong></p>

<p>One of the great benefits of a guaranteed acceptance policy is that your insurance premium will never change. Your monthly insurance bill will never increase because you locked in the premium at your current age, and it will remain the same for the rest of your life. As long as you keep paying your premiums on time, it will never increase for any reason.</p>

<p><br><strong>LEVEL DEATH BENEFIT</strong></p>

<p>Your death benefit is fixed, and it is guaranteed to stay the same and never decrease for any reason. Your beneficiaries will receive the full death benefit when you pass away after the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p><br><strong>NOT CANCELLABLE</strong></p>

<p>Some people complain about their life insurance policy canceling because of advancing age or worsening health conditions. Guaranteed issue burial insurance will never be canceled because of age or declining health condition as long as you pay your premiums on time.</p>

<p><br><strong>CASH VALUE ACCUMULATION</strong></p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance is whole life insurance that accumulates cash value over the years. The accumulated cash value can be used to pay your premiums, or you can borrow against it to be used for any purpose.</p>

<p>If you or your loved one has Down syndrome, a guaranteed issue policy is your excellent choice for life insurance coverage. GI policy doesn’t ask any health questions. If you happen to pass away during the first two years, your beneficiary will get all the premiums you paid plus 10% interest.</p>
