<h2 id="best-option-for-people-with-gout"><strong>What is my best insurance option if I have a history of gout?</strong></h2>

<p>Gout as a standalone condition is a non-issue with most insurance companies unless you have other serious health problems.</p>

<p>Your best burial insurance option with gout is a level death benefit plan. The great thing about a level death benefit plan is that you are covered from the first day, and you don’t have a waiting period. You will also pay the lowest premium with this plan.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit plan</p>

<p><br><strong>MOBILITY ISSUES &amp; ACTIVITIES OF DAILY LIVING</strong></p>

<p>There are some cases when severe gout can cause disability and <a href="https://healthtalk.org/gout/gout-mobility-and-footwear" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">mobility</a> issues. Severe gout may cause you to require help in performing the <a href="/burial-insurance/adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">activities of daily living</a> (ADL), such as eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, and continence.</p>

<p>Every life insurance company that asks health questions wants to know if you need help with any of these activities of daily living. If you need help with any of these ADLs, finding a company that offers you a level death benefit plan with first-day coverage will be hard. In this case, your only option for insurance coverage is guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue or guaranteed acceptance burial insurance doesn’t ask health questions when you apply. Your approval is guaranteed even if you have severe gout and you can’t perform activities of daily living on your own.</p>

<p>You must be aware that guaranteed issue burial insurance has a two-year waiting period, and it cost more than a level death benefit plan. Guaranteed-issue policies will pay the death benefit if you die from an accident during the waiting period. However, if you die from natural causes during the waiting period, your beneficiary will only receive all the premiums you paid plus 7-10% interest.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-a-medical-exam"><br><strong>Do I need to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with gout?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with gout.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="burial-insurance-underwriting"><br><strong><strong>Burial insurance underwriting if you have gout</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Most insurance companies never ask about gout in the health questionnaire.</p>

<p>Since most insurance carriers don’t ask about gout on their health questionnaire, you will easily qualify for first-day coverage. You will not qualify for first-day coverage only if you answered yes to the knockout section due to other health impairments or if you cannot perform any of the activities of daily living due to gout.</p>

<p>If you have no other health issues aside from gout, you will be eligible for a level death benefit plan without a waiting period.</p>
