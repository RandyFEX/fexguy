<h2 id="guaranteed-issue-burial-insurance"><br><strong>Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p>You can also purchase <a href="/burial-insurance/guaranteed-issue-life-insurance-for-seniors/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">guaranteed issue burial insurance</a> online. These policies are whole life insurance policies that last your entire life. </p>

<p>You are not required to take a medical exam or answer health questions to qualify. You can be approved for insurance coverage regardless of your current health or pre-existing medical condition. </p>

<p>The company will not look at your prescription history or MIB. This is an excellent burial insurance plan if you cannot qualify for a simplified issue with immediate coverage.</p>

<p>Time is the most significant advantage of a guaranteed acceptance policy. Some companies can approve your policy in less than 48 hours, others in under 15 minutes. This is the best option for those who need life insurance coverage quickly due to a court or business loan requirements.</p>

<p>Your premium will be based on age, gender, and state of residence. You can buy a face amount from $2,000 to $25,000 range. If you only need burial insurance for your parents to cover your funeral and final expenses, guaranteed issue burial insurance, which can be bought online, can be your best option.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue burial insurance has a graded death
benefit, which means the company will only pay the full death benefit if you
die during the waiting period from an accident. </p>

<p>If you die from a natural cause such as an illness during the two-year waiting period, your beneficiary will only receive all the premiums you paid into the policy plus 7-10% interest, depending on the insurance company. </p>

<p>If you die after the waiting period for any reason, the full death benefit will be received by your beneficiaries.</p>
