<h2 id="best-insurance-option"><br><strong><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With Dressing?</strong></strong></h2>

<p>Life insurance companies use the ability to dress independently to measure an individual’s functional capacity, especially older adults or those with disabilities.</p>

<p>If the applicant cannot perform dressing independently, they will usually need long-term care services such as in-home care, nursing, or assisted living facilities. Needing <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/wadepfau/2015/12/22/the-importance-of-planning-for-long-term-care/#45d742ba40c3" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">long-term care</a> is a red flag for most life insurance companies.</p>

<p>Anybody who needs help with dressing will be able to qualify for a guaranteed issue life insurance policy.</p>

<p>Guaranteed Issue (GI) policies don’t require you to answer any underwriting questions because your health is not considered. </p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>WHAT IS GUARANTEED ISSUE LIFE INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>This coverage has a death limitation for a natural cause of death during the first two years of the policy. The company will pay a full death benefit only if you die from an accident during the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>If you die from illness, the insurance company will return all your premiums to your beneficiaries plus 10% interest. After the two-year waiting period, you are fully protected for life. At this point, the insurance company will pay the full death benefit to your beneficiaries if you die for any reason.</p>

<p>While it costs a bit more than insurance policies with underwriting, you will not have to worry about the possibility of being denied coverage because of your inability to dress on your own or perform any of the activities of daily living.</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>#1 –&#160;AGE REQUIREMENT –&#160;</strong>Every final expense company has its own age availability, but most guaranteed issue burial insurance companies accept applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><strong>#2 – STATE AVAILABILITY –&#160;</strong>Not every insurance company offers its insurance products in every state. Make sure the company has a guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state before applying.</p>

<p><strong>#3 – ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT –&#160;</strong>You must possess a mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, the company may reject your application if you have advanced dementia or Alzheimer’s disease and cannot legally consent to an agreement.</p>

<p>We recommend buying guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with dressing and other ADLs because it’s the only life insurance plan you will qualify for. If you are relatively healthy and only need help with dressing, you can have an average life expectancy and easily outlive the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>Postponing your application for life insurance coverage is not a wise decision, as your health may decline at any moment.</p>

<p>Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with dressing is worth it because you won’t lose money with this plan, and it costs much less in the long run than having no insurance coverage at all.</p>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help dressing.</p>

<h2 id="life-insurance-underwriting"><br><strong><strong>Life Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With Dressing</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company that has underwriting (health questions) will ask if you need help with dressing or other activities of daily living on their application.</p>

<p><strong>Common underwriting questions about dressing and other ADL include:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Do you need assistance performing daily living activities such as dressing, eating, bathing, transferring, toileting or continence?</li>
<li>Do you currently use a wheelchair due to chronic illness or disability?</li>
<li>Do you currently receive home health care, or in the last 24 months, did you receive home health care services for more than three separate periods of care?</li>
<li>Are you currently confined to an assisted living or nursing facility?</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer YES to any of these questions, your application for traditional life insurance will automatically be declined. However, you have another option for coverage which is a guaranteed acceptance burial insurance that does not have any health questions.</p>

<p id="HOW-DOES-NEEDING-HELP-WITH-DRESSING-AFFECT-LIFE-INSURANCE"><br><strong>HOW DOES NEEDING HELP WITH DRESSING AFFECT LIFE INSURANCE?</strong></p>

<p>Needing help from another person to get dressed will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the best-priced insurance coverage.</p>

<p>Dressing is an important self-care activity you must do on your own. Inability to get dressed independently is associated with several health problems, such as:</p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Cerebral palsy</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Heart attack</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Parkinson’s disease</li>
<li>Spina bifida</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
</ul>

<p>Any of these medical conditions may cause you to be unable to dress independently. In some cases, the inability to get dressed may be due to cognitive impairment and forgetting the process.</p>

<p>It’s not just about the inability to get dressed independently but the medical condition that prevents you from doing so. These underlying medical conditions concern life insurance companies because they can cause premature death.</p>

<p><strong>Anything that can cause premature death is considered high risk by life insurance companies.</strong></p>

<p>Applicants needing help with dressing and other activities of daily living are considered high-risk applicants. Therefore, they will need to pay more premiums and have a short waiting period if they need help in performing ADLs.</p>
