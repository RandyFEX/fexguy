<h2 id="best-option-for-people-with-aids"><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Aids Or HIV?</strong></h2>

<p>If you have AIDS or HIV, you <strong>WILL NOT</strong> qualify for a plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue life insurance</p>

<p>A guaranteed issue life insurance policy is available to everyone regardless of health condition. It does not require an applicant to take a medical exam or answer any health-related questions to qualify.</p>

<p>This policy is typically reserved for people who cannot be eligible for any other type of life insurance. As long as you are a U.S. citizen and you are between the ages of 40-85, you will qualify for coverage (different companies have different age requirements).</p>

<p>Guaranteed final expense insurance policies are small whole life insurance policies that cover final expenses. The coverage ranges from $1,000 up to $25,000. </p>

<p><br><strong>There are three major disadvantages of guaranteed issue policy:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Guaranteed issue is one of the most expensive types of life insurance because the insurance company takes a higher risk than policies with medical underwriting.</li>
<li>Guaranteed issue life insurance can only provide up to $25,000 in coverage.</li>
<li>Guaranteed issue life insurance has a mandatory two-year waiting period.</li>
</ol>

<p>If you die of natural causes during this two-year waiting period, your guaranteed issue policy will not pay the full death benefit to your beneficiary. Instead, the company will refund all the premiums paid plus 10% interest.</p>

<p><br><strong>WHY DO YOU NEED TO BUY BURIAL INSURANCE IF YOU HAVE AIDS?</strong></p>

<p><strong>The first purpose of burial life insurance for AIDS is to cover your final expenses because funerals are expensive</strong>!</p>

<p>The average cost of a funeral and burial is about $10,000. AIDS life insurance can be used to pay funeral and burial costs. Burial insurance can provide financial protection against the funeral cost and peace of mind to your surviving family members.</p>

<p>More often, people with AIDS leave their families with massive medical debt and burial expenses to pay. Burial insurance, final expense insurance, or funeral insurance plans can provide a source of funds for the survivors to use so they can pay medical bills incurred before death.</p>

<p>Burial insurance will give you peace of mind knowing that your family will be taken care of when you pass away.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-a-medical-exam"><br><strong>If I Have AIDS Or HIV, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with AIDS or HIV.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer basic questions about your health. You don’t need to answer health questions with guaranteed issue life insurance. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="underwriting-for-aids-or-hiv"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have AIDS Or HIV</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company underwriting asks about AIDS and HIV in the health questionnaire.</p>

<p><strong>You will typically see AIDS and HIV asked this way:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Have you tested positive for exposure to the HIV infection or been diagnosed as having ARC or AIDS caused by the HIV infection or other sickness or condition derived from such infection?</li>
<li>Have you tested positive for exposure to the HIV infection or been diagnosed as having ARC or AIDS caused by the HIV infection or other sickness or condition derived from such infection?</li>
<li>Have you ever been diagnosed with, received, or been advised to receive treatment or medication for Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-Related Complex (ARC), or AIDS-related conditions?</li>
</ul>
