Burial insurance, which is a type of whole life insurance, is often easier to qualify for, especially if other policies decline you. The mistake people make is assuming they only have one option or hiding their use. That can backfire and cost your family the payout later.</p>

<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-medical-marijuana-history"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Medical Marijuana Use?</strong></h2>

<p><strong>MEDICAL MARIJUANA FOR MINOR HEALTH CONDITIONS</strong></p>

<p>If you are using marijuana to treat a minor health condition and are otherwise healthy, you can qualify for a level death benefit.</p>

<p>A level death benefit is the best rate class for burial insurance. This is great because you will pay the lowest possible premium on your life insurance, and your coverage starts on the first day. You will have no waiting period on your plan. Your beneficiaries will receive your full death benefit when you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p>It is important to note that whether or not you will qualify for a level death benefit plan if you’re a marijuana user entirely depends on the individual company. Every life insurance company is different, and they have different underwriting requirements. It is best to seek expert advice from an independent life insurance agency about your specific case.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Leved death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>MEDICAL MARIJUANA FOR SEVERE MEDICAL CONDITIONS</strong></p>

<p>If you use medical marijuana to treat a severe medical condition like a recent cancer diagnosis or terminal illness, you will be declined for a first-day coverage plan, but there is another option. You can apply and receive approval for a guaranteed issue burial insurance even if you use marijuana to treat a severe medical condition.</p>

<p>The guaranteed acceptance policy has no underwriting or health questions and doesn’t ask about marijuana use or your medical condition. Guaranteed issue burial insurance costs more than the level death benefit, but you can be approved for coverage as quickly as 15 minutes.</p>

<p>You can easily qualify for this plan if you are a U.S. citizen between 50 and 80 years old.</p>

<p>The guaranteed issue policy has a two-year waiting period for a natural cause of death. The policy will pay for accidental heath if you pass away during the waiting period. If you pass away during the first two years as a consequence of any medical issues, your beneficiaries will receive all your premiums plus 10% interest.</p>

<p>After the first two years, it will pay full death benefits for any cause of death.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance for medical marijuana users.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-medical-marijuana"><br><strong>Underwriting Burial Insurance With Medical Marijuana</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Medical marijuana comes in different forms such as marijuana plant material, tincture, capsules, vapes, balms, lotion, and&#160;<a href="/burial-insurance-cbd-oil/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">CBD oil</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Some insurance companies will ask about marijuana use this way:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Other than prescribed by a physician, have you ever used marijuana, stimulants, narcotics, sedatives, hallucinogens, or any prescription drugs?</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer yes, please provide us with the name, frequency, amount, length of use, and date last used.</p>

<p>The amount of medical marijuana you consume and the frequency of use may be considered by insurers when determining how much you’ll pay for coverage and if you will have a waiting period.</p>

<p id="HOW-MEDICAL-MARIJUANA-AFFECT-LIFE-INSURANCE"><br><strong>HOW DOES MEDICAL MARIJUANA AFFECT LIFE INSURANCE</strong>?</p>

<p>Medical prescriptions for marijuana are allowed in many states if you have a medical marijuana card. Because it is legal, there are no legal issues regarding the law enforcement agency and qualifying for life insurance.</p>

<p>Medical marijuana use is common for some medical and psychological conditions. If you have a marijuana prescription, the insurance company may want further information about your medical condition.</p>

<p><strong>There are different forms of medical marijuana prescription, including the following:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Marijuana plant material</li>
<li>Tinctures</li>
<li>Capsules</li>
<li>Edibles</li>
<li>Lozenges</li>
<li>Oral sprays</li>
<li>Vaporizing</li>
<li>Dermal patches</li>
</ul>

<p>If you are using medical marijuana in any form and if you have a medical marijuana card, the insurance company will look at two things:</p>

<ul>
<li>The medical condition being treated by marijuana</li>
<li>Your other health conditions</li>
</ul>

<p>It is not marijuana use that plays a major role in underwriting but the reason for your marijuana prescription.&#160;The type of medical condition being treated by marijuana is more important because it can impact your life expectancy.</p>

<p>The major factor in getting approval and getting the best rates is to apply to a marijuana-friendly life insurance company. The best way to do this is to work with an independent life insurance agency that represents multiple companies.</p>

<p><br><strong>DO YOU NEED TO DISCLOSE YOUR MARIJUANA USE?</strong></p>

<p>You are not obliged to disclose your marijuana use if the insurance company does not ask about it. Some companies don’t ask about marijuana use; generally, they will only ask about your health and any medical conditions you have had in the past.</p>

<p>If the insurance company asks about marijuana use, answer it honestly – do not lie. Tell them about your marijuana use, including the amount you use and the frequency of usage. Lying about marijuana use on an insurance application is a fraud, and you risk losing your benefits if you are dishonest on your application.</p>

<p id="RIGHTS-OF-A-MARIJUANA-USER"><br><strong>RIGHTS OF A MARIJUANA USER</strong></p>

<p>A valid concern of most marijuana users is privacy. They worry that admission to using marijuana can lead to exposure to the possibility of arrest.</p>

<p>You will not get in trouble with the law for admitting marijuana use on your life insurance application.</p>

<p>If you use medical marijuana, your insurance agent cannot report you to your employer, family or law enforcement agency. You are fully protected when you apply for life insurance.</p>

<p>As a marijuana user, your rights are protected by the HIPPA law (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 1996), which guarantees the privacy of individually identifiable health information.</p>

<p>HIPPA protects all your medical information. Nobody can access your medical records without prior written consent from you. Your insurance agent, your doctor, or anyone directly related to your health information cannot disclose your records without your permission.</p>
