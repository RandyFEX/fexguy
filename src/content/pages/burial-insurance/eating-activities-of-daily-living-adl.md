<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-need-help-with-eating"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With Eating?</strong></h2>

<p><a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Activities of daily living</a> are the fundamental <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Activities_of_daily_living" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">skills</a> needed to manage basic physical needs. </p>

<p>Eating is one of the activities of daily living that you must do on your own to qualify for life insurance. Insurance companies require that each applicant be able to do this as a key part of functional independence.</p>

<p>Life insurance companies define eating as the process of putting food into the body which includes reaching for, picking up, and grasping a utensil or food container. Getting food on the utensil and bringing food from the utensil into the mouth and manipulating food on the plate and cleaning hands and face following meals is also included in ADLs.</p>

<p>If you need assistance from another person to eat, you are dependent on eating, and the only life insurance available for you is a guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p>Guaranteed Issue (GI) policies do not require you to answer any underwriting questions, meaning your health is not taken into account at all. The insurance company will accept your application without asking for any details about your health.</p>

<p>While it cost a bit more than insurance with underwriting, you will not have to worry about the possibility of being denied coverage because of not being able to perform eating or other activities of daily living on your own.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance has a death benefit limitation for a natural cause of death during the first two years of the policy. After the two years waiting period, you are fully protected for life. At this point, the insurance provider will pay 100% of the death benefit if you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>AGE REQUIREMENT</strong></p>

<p>Every final expense company has their own age availability, but most guaranteed issue burial insurance companies accept applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><br><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every insurance company have their products licensed in every state. Make sure the company has guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state before applying.</p>

<p><br><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must possess a mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, if you have advanced dementia or Alzheimer’s disease and you are not able to legally consent to an agreement, the company may reject your application.</p>

<h2 id="why-are-the-activities-of-daily-living-important-in-life-insurance"><br><strong>Why Are The Activities Of Daily Living Important In Life Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p><strong>If you cannot perform any of the activities of daily living, it will impact your life insurance eligibility and may result in:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>You may not be approved for a new policy</li>
<li>If you’ve already purchased insurance, you may now qualify for long-term care benefits.</li>
</ul>

<p>Insurance companies only ask if you need help with ADL’s because when a person cannot perform any of the ADL’s, they will usually need long-term care like in-home care, nursing, or assisted living facility care which is a “knock-out” for a first-day coverage insurance plan. </p>

<p>It’s critical that you work with an independent life insurance agency before it’s too late because when you need help with any of the activities of daily living, the best life insurance option for you is to take a guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Need Help With Eating, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help with eating.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple, and you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-people-needing-help-with-eating"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With Eating</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company that has health questions will ask if you need help with activities of daily living in their application.</p>

<p><strong>Common life insurance questions about eating and other ADL</strong>:</p>

<ul>
<li>Do you need help in performing activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting or continence?</li>
<li>Do you have any impairment, whether physical or mental, for which you need or receive assistance or supervision in performing normal activities of daily living such as dressing, eating, bathing, incontinence, toileting, or moving without any type of physical assistance?</li>
<li>Do you require a wheelchair due to chronic illness or disease, or do you require assistance from anyone with activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, or continence?</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer “yes” to any of these questions, your application will be automatically declined for traditional life or burial insurance. However, you have another option for coverage, and that is by getting a guaranteed issue burial insurance that does not have any health questions.</p>

<p>The inability to eat independently may be caused by physical and mental health. These conditions can cause the applicant to have a premature death. Anything that can cause premature death is considered high risk by life insurance companies.</p>

<p>Needing help with eating is one of the determining factors of the insurance company whether they will provide coverage or decline your application. Needing help with eating is generally a decline for first-day coverage life insurance and the only coverage you will qualify for is guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>
