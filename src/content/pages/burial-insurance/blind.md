<h1>Burial Insurance for the Blind</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• Blindness alone usually won’t disqualify you from burial insurance<br>• Your overall health matters more than your vision loss<br>• Some companies still charge higher rates depending on other conditions<br>• Guaranteed issue plans cost more and delay payouts for 2 years<br>• Not all companies handle blind applicants the same way<br>• Not comparing options can leave you overpaying for basic coverage</p>

<p>Burial insurance for the blind is more accessible than most people think. Blindness by itself usually isn’t considered a high-risk factor, so many people can still qualify for burial insurance, which is a type of whole life insurance. What matters is your overall health, any underlying conditions, and how stable things are. Some applicants can even qualify for first-day coverage with no waiting period, while others may be pushed into more expensive guaranteed issue plans. The biggest mistake is assuming blindness limits your options when the real decision comes down to everything else going on with your health.</p>

<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-are-blind"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I’m Blind?</strong></h2>

<p id="Factors-That-May-Affect-Insurance-Eligibility"><br><strong>BLINDNESS DUE TO AN ACCIDENT</strong></p>

<p>If you’re blind due to an accident, you are independent and can do activities of daily living on your own, you have better options, and you will qualify for a level death benefit plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<p>Level death benefit plans come with first-day coverage. It will be offered to you if <a href="https://www.healthline.com/health/blindness" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">blindness</a> is your only issue. You are independent and generally healthy or answer no to the health questions the insurance company asks. The insurance company will not care about your blindness.</p>

<p>You will be 100% covered from the first day and have no waiting period. Your beneficiary would receive your full death benefit payout when you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>BLINDNESS DUE TO AN ILLNESS</strong></p>

<p>Cause of blindness the Insurers are cautious of:</p>

<ul>
<li>Uncontrolled diabetes</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
<li>Head injuries</li>
<li>Glaucoma</li>
<li>Head trauma</li>
<li>Cranial hemorrhaging</li>
<li>Uncontrolled hypertension</li>
</ul>

<p>Depending on the cause of blindness, blind applicants may have some issues because blindness can be a complication of other serious diseases. </p>

<p>Insurance companies will often consider the cause of your blindness. If your cause of blindness will likely reduce your life expectancy, you will still have the option of purchasing guaranteed issue life insurance.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue whole life insurance doesn’t ask any health questions, and you will easily qualify regardless of your medical condition.</p>

<p>While it cost a little more than the first-day coverage plan, you don’t have to worry about the possibility of being denied insurance coverage due to health issues.</p>

<p>If you are 50 to 85 years old, a U.S. citizen who can enter into a legal contract, you will be eligible for this policy.</p>

<p>A guaranteed acceptance policy is graded for the first two years for natural causes of death. The insurance company will only pay the full death benefit if you die from an accident during the first two years.</p>

<p>If you pass away due to an illness during the two-year
waiting period, the insurance company will pay your beneficiary with all the
premiums you’ve paid plus 10% interest.</p>

<p>When the two-year waiting period is over, you are fully covered, and your beneficiaries will receive the full death benefit payout if you die for any reason.</p>

<p> <strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance<br><br><br><strong>ACTIVITIES OF DAILY LIVING</strong></p>

<p>Blindness can affect your mobility and may require you to need help with the <a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">activities of daily living</a>. </p>

<p>Almost all life insurance companies with underwriting (health questions) ask about activities of daily living on their life insurance application.</p>

<p>Life insurance companies use six ADLs to determine if you will qualify for the first-day coverage plan. </p>

<p>You must be able to do these activities on your own to be eligible for the best rate.</p>

<ol>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-help-with-eating-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank">Eating</a></li>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-bathing-disability-adl/" target="_blank">Bathing</a></li>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-need-help-with-dressing-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank">Dressing</a></li>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-help-with-toileting-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank">Toileting</a></li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-transferring/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Transferring</a></li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-continence-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Continence</a></li>
</ol>

<p>If you can do these activities independently, then your blindness will not affect your final expense insurance eligibility (and you will qualify for the lowest pricing). </p>

<p>Needing assistance from somebody else to do any activities of daily living will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the lowest-priced burial insurance policy. Understand that your options are limited if you need help with ADL’s. </p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance will be your best choice if you need help with ADLs. </p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>HOME HEALTH CARE</strong></p>

<p>It’s very common for blind people to require home health care. Every burial insurance company will specifically ask about home health care. Only a few companies don’t ask about home health care. </p>

<p>If you currently need home health care because of blindness, and you have some chronic illness, it would be best to apply for a guaranteed acceptance burial insurance policy.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I’m Blind, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance for the blind.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-the-blind"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Are Blind </strong></strong></h2>

<p>If you want the lowest-priced insurance coverage, go for a company that asks some health questions (underwriting). Underwriting is a process insurance companies use to evaluate your risk of death and insurability. </p>

<p>Simply put, burial insurance companies with underwriting ask a few health questions on the application. The insurer will also perform a prescription history check to verify your answer to the health questions.</p>

<p>Some insurance companies offer insurance policies with no underwriting. This plan should be your last option because it has a higher premium and a waiting period. </p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>If you choose a company that uses medical underwriting, you cannot avoid answering a few health questions. </p>

<p>Fortunately, none of the insurance companies we use ask about non-disease-related blindness in their health questionnaire. Yes, you read it right; they never ask about it.</p>

<p>If they don’t ask about it, they don’t care about it, and you will qualify for the lowest rates!</p>

<p>Blindness as a standalone condition is never an issue. It will only be an issue if blindness is paired with some chronic illnesses, you need home health care, or you need help with activities of daily living.</p>

<p>The first step to figuring out how much burial insurance you need is to know your end-of-life expenses. Your funeral cost is often the biggest single expense you need to pay. Other end-of-life expenses to consider are your outstanding medical bills, living expenses, credit card bills, and other debts.</p>
