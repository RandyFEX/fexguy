<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-need-help-with-toileting"><br><strong><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With Toileting?</strong></strong></h2>

<p>ADL’s are self-care activities such as toileting, eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, and continence that we must do to lead a normal and independent life. Inability to do these means you cannot take care of yourself and you may need long-term care.</p>

<p>ADLs are used by life insurance companies to measure an applicant’s function and independence. If you cannot perform toileting or any activities of daily living, your best insurance option is guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p>Guaranteed Issue (GI) policies do not require you to take a medical exam or pass any underwriting questions. The company will also not require you to submit your medical records, and your prescription history will not be checked either. Your health is not taken into account to qualify. </p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>AGE REQUIREMENT</strong></p>

<p>Every final expense company has its own age availability, but most guaranteed acceptance burial insurance companies accept applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every insurance company has its products licensed and available in every state. Make sure the company has a guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state before applying.</p>

<p><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must possess a mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, if you have severe cognitive impairment and you cannot legally consent to an agreement, the company may reject your application.</p>

<h2 id="why-you-need-guaranteed-issue-burial-insurance"><br><strong>Why Do You Need Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance If You Need Help With Toileting?</strong></h2>

<p>We recommend buying guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with toileting and other ADLs because it’s the only life insurance plan you will qualify for because of your condition. </p>

<p>If you are relatively healthy and only need help with toileting, you can have an average life expectancy and easily outlive the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>Postponing your application for life insurance coverage is not wise because your health may decline at any moment. Better apply now and start working on the waiting period.</p>

<p>Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance if you need help with toileting is a wise investment as you won’t lose money with this plan because your premiums will be returned plus interest if you die during the waiting period. Buying a guaranteed issue burial insurance policy will cost much less in the long run than having no insurance coverage at all.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Need Help With Toileting, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help with toileting.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple, and you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-people-needing-help-with-toileting"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With Toileting</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company that has underwriting (health questions) will ask if you need help with toileting and other daily living activities in their application.</p>

<p><strong>Common life insurance questions about toileting and other ADLs you will see include:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Do you need assistance in performing activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting or continence?</li>
<li>Do you currently use a wheelchair due to a disability or chronic illness?</li>
<li>Do you currently receive home health care services or, in the last two years, did you receive home health care for more than three separate periods of care?</li>
<li>Are you currently confined to a nursing or assisted living facility?</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer “yes” to any of these questions, your application will automatically decline for first-day coverage life insurance. However, your only option is getting guaranteed issue burial insurance that does not have any health questions.</p>

<h2 id="how-does-needing-help-with-continence-affect-life-insurance"><br><strong>How Does Needing Help With Toileting Affect Life Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>The insurance companies define toileting as the ability to go to the toilet and perform the associated personal hygiene task. Toileting is an important self-care activity that you must do on your own. But, as we age and our health deteriorates, we may need help with toileting and other basic activities of daily living.</p>

<p>Even if seniors are pretty independent, they may still need help when they go to the toilet. It’s easy for them to trip and fall when walking to the bathroom in a rush or when standing or sitting in the toilet due to blood pressure changes.</p>

<p>Being toilet dependent will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the best-priced insurance coverage. Anybody who is dependent on help with toileting and other ADLs often needs long-term care services. </p>

<p>Needing assistance to perform ADL raises a red flag to life insurance companies because the inability to go to the toilet independently is generally associated with several health problems, including:</p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (<a href="http://www.alsa.org/about-als/what-is-als.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ALS</a>)</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Cerebral palsy</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Heart attack</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Parkinson’s disease</li>
<li>Spina bifida</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
</ul>

<p>Any of these medical conditions, when severe enough, can cause you to prevent going to the toilet independently. These pre-existing conditions can also cause mobility issues that can lead to falls, accidents, and injuries that can cause premature death. Anything that can cause premature death is considered high risk by life insurance companies.</p>

<p>Applicants needing help with toileting and other activities of daily living are considered high-risk applicants. Therefore, they must pay more premiums and have a short waiting period. No life insurance company will offer you coverage during the first two years if you need help in performing ADL.</p>
