<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-use-cbd-oil"><br><strong><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I’m A CBD Oil User?</strong></strong></h2>

<p>It’s important to understand that CBD oil users may be approved for burial insurance depending on the medical condition the CBD oil is treating.</p>

<p>The medical condition it is treating is the bigger concern of insurance companies because some medical conditions can reduce life expectancy.&#160;The insurance company will consider the nature of your condition before deciding to approve your application.</p>

<p><br><strong>CBD OIL USE FOR MINOR HEALTH CONDITION</strong>S</p>

<p>It is important to note that whether or not you will qualify for a level death benefit if you’re a CBD oil user entirely depends on the individual company. Every life insurance company is different, and they have different underwriting requirements. It is best to seek professional advice from an independent life insurance agency like Final Expense Guy about your specific case.</p>

<p>If you are using CBD oil to treat a minor health condition and are otherwise healthy, you can qualify for a level death benefit.</p>

<p>A level death benefit plan is the best rate class for burial insurance. This is great because you will only pay the lowest possible premium on your life insurance, and your coverage starts on the first day. You will have no waiting period on your plan. Your beneficiaries will receive your full death benefit when you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>CBD OIL USE FOR SEVERE MEDICAL CONDITIONS</strong></p>

<p>If you use CBD oil to treat a severe medical condition like a recent cancer diagnosis or terminal illness, you will be declined for a first-day coverage plan, but there is another option. You can apply and receive approval for a guaranteed issue burial insurance even if you use CBD oil to treat a severe medical condition.</p>

<p>A guaranteed acceptance policy has no health questions and doesn’t ask about CBD oil use or your medical condition. Guaranteed issue burial insurance costs more than a&#160;level death benefit plan, but you can be approved for coverage as quickly as 15 minutes.</p>

<p>You can qualify for this plan if you are a U.S. citizen between 50 and 85 years old.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue policy has a two-year waiting period for a natural cause of death. If you pass away during the first two years due to any medical issues, your beneficiaries will receive all your premiums plus 10% interest. The policy will pay for accidental heath if you pass away during the waiting period. After the first two years, it will pay full death benefits for any cause of death.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance for CBD oil users.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-cbd-oil-user"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You’re A CBD Oil User</strong></strong></h2>

<p>Every final expense insurance company with underwriting will ask health questions and conduct a prescription history check to verify your health.</p>

<p><strong>Some insurance companies will ask about marijuana or CBD oil use this way:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Other than being prescribed by a physician, have you ever used marijuana, stimulants, narcotics, sedatives, hallucinogens, or any prescription drugs?</li>
</ul>

<p>You’ll notice that this question did not ask about CBD oil use. If you answer yes to any marijuana use, please provide us with the name, frequency, amount and length of use, and the date last used.</p>

<p id="DO-YOU-NEED-TO-DISCLOSE-YOUR-CBD-OIL-USE"><br><strong>DO YOU NEED TO DISCLOSE YOUR CBD OIL USE?</strong></p>

<p>You are not obliged to disclose your CBD oil use if the insurance company does not ask about it. Most companies never ask about CBD oil; they will only ask about your medical condition.</p>

<p>If the insurance company asks about CBD oil use, answer it honestly – do not lie. Tell them about your CBD oil use, including the amount you use and the frequency of usage. </p>

<p>There is no need to worry about negative repercussions for you. Your rights are protected under the HIPPA laws (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 1996) and will never be released to law enforcement or public officials.</p>

<p>HIPPA guarantees that the information you disclose on your application is strictly private and confidential.</p>

<p>Lying on an insurance application is FRAUD, and you risk losing your benefits.</p>
