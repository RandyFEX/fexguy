<h2 id="best-option-for-people-with-hepatitis-b"><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Hepatitis B?</strong></h2>

<p>Your ability to qualify for burial insurance with Hepatitis B depends on the following factors:</p>

<ul>
<li>Date of your Hepatitis B diagnosis</li>
<li>The time that passed since your Hepatitis B treatment</li>
<li>Prescription medications taken</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>TREATMENT STOPPED WITHIN 2 YEARS</strong></p>

<p>If your treatment stopped two years ago because you recovered and your body was able to fight off the infection, you would qualify for the best plan and the lowest rate</p>

<p>Most life insurance companies that only have a two-year lookback period on Hepatitis B will offer you a level death benefit with first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best option:</strong> Level Death Benefit Plan</p>

<p>This plan with first-day coverage has a level premium that remains the same your whole life. The level death benefit plan is a simplified issue policy with no medical exam and just health questions to answer. Your death benefit will never decrease, and your beneficiary will receive a 100% death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<p><br><strong>CURRENT DIAGNOSIS AND TREATMENT (Less than 2 years)</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company asks about Hepatitis B within a minimum period of two years. If you were diagnosed with Hepatitis B in the last two years and still undergoing any type of treatment, your best option is first-day benefits.</p>

<p>A first-day benefit plan will also cover you from the first day but your death benefit payout is phased in over time.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> First-day benefits</p>

<p>No, you are not required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with Hepatitis B.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-hepatitis-b"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Hepatitis B</strong></h2>

<p>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting.</p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They ask you a series of health questions. <strong>Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility. </strong></p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history. Your Hepatitis B medications will give them a clear picture of your current and past medical treatments.</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>Here are some examples of how burial companies ask about Hepatitis B in their health questions:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Have you ever had, or been diagnosed with, or received or been advised to receive treatment or medication for: liver disease or condition such as chronic hepatitis or cirrhosis of the liver?</li>
<li>During the past 24 months, have you been diagnosed or treated for liver disease?</li>
<li>Have you been diagnosed within the prior 2 years with or received, or been advised to receive treatment for Chronic hepatitis, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, or liver cirrhosis?</li>
</ul>

<p>You will also notice that some insurance companies do not specifically ask about Hepatitis B in the health questionnaire. However, they ask if you have liver disease. You need to answer “yes” to liver disease question since Hepatitis B is a form of liver disease.</p>

<p>If you have a history of Hepatitis B, inform us immediately so we can place you with an insurance carrier that never asks about Hepatitis B. An insurance carrier never asks about a specific medical condition will approve you for their best plan.</p>
