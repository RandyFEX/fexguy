Your life insurance options are limited, but you may still qualify for first-day benefits.</p>

<p><br><strong>What is a Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance?</strong></p>

<p><br><strong>OTHER FACTORS THAT MAY ONLY QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE:</strong></p>

<p><strong><a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Activities of Daily Living </a></strong></p>

<p>Every life insurance company will ask if you need help with any of the ADL’s in the knockout section of the application. Activities of daily living include:</p>

<ul>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-help-with-eating-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank">Eating</a></li>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-bathing-disability-adl/" target="_blank">Bathing</a></li>

<li><a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-transferring/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Transferring</a> </li>
</ul>

<p>If you are in the late stage of Prion disease, you will require help with activities of daily living because your condition is getting worse. Traditional life insurance declines the application of anyone who needs help with ADLs. Guaranteed issue no questions asked is your only option for coverage.</p>

<p>You can get approved for <a href="/guaranteed-issue-life-insurance-for-seniors/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Guaranteed issue</a> burial insurance without answering any health questions. This policy is great for those with cognition loss or difficulty answering application questions coherently. </p>

<p>The minimum age requirement to apply is 40 to 85 years old. If you are a U.S. citizen and reside in the state where this policy is sold, and you’re within the age requirement, you will be eligible for this policy.</p>

<p>The application process is quick and easy. It only takes several minutes over the phone or computer.  </p>

<p>Guaranteed issue policies pay 100% of the face amount from day one if you die from an accident but will not cover natural causes of death until the two-year waiting period is over.</p>

<p>If you pass away from an illness as a consequence of Prion disease, the insurance company will pay your beneficiary all the premiums you paid plus 10% interest. After the waiting period, the company will pay 100% of the death benefit for any reason for death.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>Home Health Care</strong></p>

<p>Aside from activities of daily living, the life insurance companies will also ask if you need home health care. Most Prion disease patients need permanent home health care during the late stage of Prion disease. If this is your condition or your loved ones, it would be best for you to take out a guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<h2 id="burial-insurance-underwriting"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Prion Disease</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>You will typically see Prion disease asked this way:</p>

<ul>
<li>Have you ever been diagnosed, treated, or taken medication for Alzheimer’s disease, dementia, or organic brain syndrome, or mental incapacity?</li>
<li>Have you been diagnosed or treated for neurodegenerative disease in the past 24 months?</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>WHY DO INSURANCE COMPANIES CARE IF YOU HAVE PRION DISEASE?</strong> </p>

<p>Prion disease is a degenerative brain disorder that leads to dementia and death. Symptoms can look like those of other dementia-like brain disorders. The patient will have a rapid cognitive decline. It progresses more rapidly and can be fatal within a few months, although some can last a few years.</p>

<p>As with other causes of dementia, Prion disease profoundly affects the brain and the body.  They may also forget to care for themselves and get into a coma. Prion disease is a terminal illness that will result in premature death. </p>

<p><strong>Types of Prion
Diseases</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (CJD),</li>
<li>Variant Creutzfeldt – Jakob disease (vCJD)</li>
<li>Fatal Familial Insomnia (FFI)</li>
<li>Gerstmann-Straussler-Scheinker syndrome (GSS)</li>
<li>Kuru</li>
</ol>

<p>Prion diseases are marked by rapid mental deterioration. As Prion disease progresses, the mental symptoms worsen. Most patients eventually lapse into a coma.&#160; Respiratory failure, heart failure, pneumonia, or other infections are the common cause of death. Death usually occurs within a year.</p>

<p>Because there’s still no cure for Prion disease, a complete breakdown of the motor system is certain. It is the reason why life insurance companies are cautious about offering first-day coverage with this condition. </p>

<p>Life insurance companies understand that Prion diseases are serious diseases that cause premature death. If you or your family member has Prion disease, we are confident that we can find you an affordable final expense insurance policy.</p>
