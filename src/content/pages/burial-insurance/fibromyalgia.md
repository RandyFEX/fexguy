<p>Getting burial insurance with fibromyalgia is usually easier than people expect, but it depends on how stable your condition is. Burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed for people with health conditions, and fibromyalgia often qualifies for standard approval if symptoms are controlled. Insurance companies focus more on how your condition affects your daily life and work, not just the diagnosis itself. If symptoms are severe or limit your activity, you may face higher costs or fewer options, but coverage is still available in most cases.</p>

<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-fibromyalgia"><strong><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Fibromyalgia?</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>MILD FIBROMYALGIA</strong></p>

<p>If you have mild fibromyalgia, then you can easily qualify for level death benefit with first-day coverage. You will not have a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>You will also be eligible for the lowest rate possible with mild fibromyalgia.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>MODERATE FIBROMYALGIA</strong></p>

<p>It is rare for burial insurance companies to decline someone in this category. People with moderate fibromyalgia can still qualify for a level death benefit plan.</p>

<p>Even if you have difficulty with your chronic pain, which occasionally impacts your work and lifestyle, you will still qualify for first-day coverage.</p>

<p>Your coverage will be effective from day one, and you will pay the lowest price offered by the burial insurance company.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>SEVERE FIBROMYALGIA</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company asks if you have issues with activities of daily living (ADLs). ADLs refer to your ability to perform activities of daily living such as bathing, dressing, eating, transferring, toileting, and continence.</p>

<p>If your fibromyalgia prevents you from performing these activities, you will be declined for a level death benefit plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance doesn’t ask any health questions, so if you struggle with ADLs, this will be the right plan. </p>

<p>Your approval is guaranteed with guaranteed issue life insurance because it does not turn people down due to their health conditions.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue policies have a two-year waiting period. If you pass away during the waiting period, your beneficiary will not receive the full death benefit. Instead, they will get a refund of all premiums paid plus 10% interest.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><strong>If I Have Fibromyalgia, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with fibromyalgia.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-fibromyalgia"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Fibromyalgia</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies will evaluate your level of risk in two ways:</strong></p>

<p><strong>First</strong> – they will ask you questions about your overall health.</p>

<p><strong>Second</strong> – they will conduct a prescription history check to validate your answer to the health questions.</p>

<p>Your answer to the health questions and your prescription history will help the insurance companies to determine your eligibility and the amount of premium they will charge.</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>Most burial insurance companies do not ask about fibromyalgia in their health questionnaire.</strong>&#160;This means you can easily qualify for the best plans and rates if you have a history of fibromyalgia. </p>

<p><br><strong>PRESCRIPTION HISTORY CHECK</strong></p>

<p>Burial insurance companies will always check your prescription history to identify your health issues based on the medications you are taking.</p>

<p>There are no specific medications for fibromyalgia. Drugs used to treat fibromyalgia include painkillers, anti-seizure, and antidepressants. The drug approved to treat fibromyalgia, such as Pregabalin (Lyrica) is also used to treat other medical conditions like neuropathy and epilepsy.</p>

<p>Some burial insurance companies have issues with depression and epilepsy medications. They will be able to see a red flag prescription and will use it to identify any other serious health issues you have.</p>

<p>Some prescription medication for fibromyalgia is also flagged for diabetic neuropathy, including Gabapentin, Lyrica, and Neurotin. These drugs may cause issues because neuropathy is a severe complication of diabetes that causes nerve damage.</p>

<p>Most burial insurance companies will charge you more in premiums and impose a waiting period if you have neuropathy related to diabetes.</p>
