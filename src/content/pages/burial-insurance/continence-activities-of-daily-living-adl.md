In most cases, that pushes you into guaranteed-issue burial insurance, a type of whole life insurance with higher costs and a 2-year waiting period before full benefits begin.</p>

<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-need-help-with-continence"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With Continence?</strong></h2>

<p>Activities of daily living (<a href="/burial-insurance/adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ADL)</a>  such as continence, eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, and transferring are used by life insurance companies to assess the applicants’ function and independence:</p>

<p>Life insurance companies define continence as maintaining bladder and bowel control and performing personal hygiene tasks. It includes managing and caring for a catheter, ostomy, or urinary catheter collection bag and applying diapers or disposable barrier pads.</p>

<p>Suppose you cannot retain control of your bladder or bowel function and need assistance from another person to do the associated personal hygiene tasks. In that case, you are dependent on help for continence.</p>

<p>If you need help with continence, your best insurance option is guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p>Guaranteed Issue (GI) policies don’t require you to pass a medical exam or underwriting questions because your health is not considered qualified.</p>

<p>You could even have a terminal illness, yet the company will approve your application!</p>

<p>These policies have a death limitation for a natural cause of death during the first two years of the policy. The company will also pay a full death benefit if you die from an accident during the two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>If you die from illness, the insurance company will return all your premiums to your beneficiaries plus 10% interest. After the two-year waiting period, you are fully protected for life. At this point, the insurance company will pay the full death benefit to your beneficiaries if you die for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p id="REQUIREMENTS-TO-QUALIFY-FOR-GUARANTEED-ISSUE-BURIAL-INSURANCE"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>AGE REQUIREMENT</strong></p>

<p>If you are 50 to 85 years old and a U.S. citizen, you will easily qualify for guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p><br><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every insurance company has its insurance products licensed in every state. Ensure the company has guaranteed issue whole life insurance policy available in your state before applying.</p>

<p><br><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must also possess the mental capacity to enter into a legal contract to qualify for burial insurance.</p>

<h2 id="how-does-needing-help-with-continence-affect-life-insurance"><br><strong>How Does Needing Help With Continence Affect Life Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>Needing help from another person with continence will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the best-priced insurance coverage, as continence is an important self-care activity you must do on your own.</p>

<p><strong>Inability to control the bladder and bowel is associated with several medical problems such as:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Cerebral palsy</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Heart attack</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Parkinson’s disease</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
</ul>

<p>Any of these medical conditions can cause incontinence and mobility limitations. In some cases, the inability to go to the toilet and do the necessary personal hygiene tasks may be due to forgetting the process because of cognitive impairment.</p>

<p>These medical conditions may cause an applicant to have incontinence and lose control of their bladder or bowel are the primary concern of the life insurance companies because they may cause premature death. Anything that causes premature death is considered high risk by insurance companies.</p>

<p>Therefore, they will need to pay more premiums and endure a waiting period.&#160; No life insurance company will offer you a first-day coverage plan if you need help in performing ADL.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Need Help With Continence, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help with continence.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-people-needing-help-with-continence"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With Continence</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>Common life insurance questions about continence and other ADL</strong>:</p>

<ul>
<li>Do you need help performing daily living activities such as eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting or continence?</li>
<li>Do you have any impairment, whether physical or mental, for which you need or receive assistance or supervision in performing normal activities of daily living such as dressing, eating, bathing, incontinence, toileting, or moving without any type of physical assistance?</li>
<li>Do you require a wheelchair due to chronic illness or disease, or do you require assistance from anyone with activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, or continence?</li>
</ul>

<p>People with cognitive impairment can experience difficulties with continence. This condition can directly affect the bladder and bowel function and make the individual less aware of the need to go to the toilet.</p>

<p>Incontinence is the involuntary leakage of urine or feces or both. Older people have a higher risk of <a href="https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/urinary-incontinence/symptoms-causes/syc-20352808" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">incontinence</a>.</p>

<p><strong>In some cases, some medical conditions may cause incontinence in older people including:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Urinary Tract Infection</li>
<li>Constipation</li>
<li>Prostate Gland Trouble</li>
<li>Irritable bowel syndrome</li>
<li>Side effects of medications</li>
</ul>

<p>Some people develop incontinence because messages between the brain and the bowel or bladder don’t work properly. They don’t recognize that they have a full bladder or bowel and have no control over the need to empty their bladder or bowel.</p>

<p>Because of old age, and physical or mental illness, some people need help with continence. People needing continence help may need long-term care at a nursing home or an assisted living facility.</p>

<p>Every life insurance company will ask if you need help with continence and activities of daily living. Suppose you answer “yes” to the ADL question. In that case, you will automatically be declined for life insurance except for guaranteed issue burial insurance, which does not ask any health questions and will take you in regardless of your current situation.</p>
