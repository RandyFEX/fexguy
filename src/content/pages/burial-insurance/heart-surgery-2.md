<p>Had heart surgery a year back? No worries, you’re still in the game for burial insurance. Your best bet is a modified benefit plan. </p>

<p>This plan has got you covered from day one, with your death benefit gradually kicking in over time.</p>

<p>Remember, everyone’s situation is unique when hunting for funeral or burial insurance post-heart surgery. Other health issues might come into play, and some insurers are more lenient based on your age and current health conditions.</p>

<h2 id="underwriting-heart-surgery"><br><strong><strong>Burial insurance underwriting if you have heart surgery</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Insurance companies have two ways to do their underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They’ll ask you some health questions. Your answers will help determine if you’re eligible.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They’ll do a quick electronic review of your prescription history to confirm your health status.</p>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p>Every life insurance company wants the scoop on your heart surgery history.</p>

<p><strong>Here’s the rundown on what qualifies as heart surgery:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Heart transplant</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance/heart-surgery/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Heart valv</a>e repair or replacement</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance/heart-bypass-surgery/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Coronary artery bypass</a> graft surgery</li>
<li>Transmyocardial laser revascularization</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance/arrhythmia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Arrhythmia</a> treatment</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance/aneurysm/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Aneurysm</a> repair</li>
<li>Myectomy/myotomy</li>
<li>Cardiomyoplasty</li>
<li>Minimally invasive surgical <a href="https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/heart-attack/treatment-of-a-heart-attack/cardiac-procedures-and-surgeries" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">procedures</a></li>
</ul>

<p>Your answers to these questions will decide your eligibility and coverage amount. Given your heart condition, most insurance companies are itching to know when your last heart surgery was.</p>

<p>For most burial insurance companies, why you had heart surgery isn’t a big deal. What they really want to know is how long it’s been since your last surgery.</p>

<p><strong>Some heart health questions they might throw your way:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>During the past 12 months, have you had a heart attack or failure or heart or circulatory surgery?</li>
<li>Within the past 24 months, have you been medically diagnosed as having or been treated for heart attack, heart disease, or heart or circulatory surgery to improve circulation to the heart?</li>
<li>Within the past 2 years, have you had or been diagnosed with angina, heart attack, cardiomyopathy, or any type of heart surgery?</li>
</ul>

<p>Your answers will determine if you qualify for first-day coverage or first-day benefits…and what your pricing will be.</p>
