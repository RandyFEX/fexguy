<h2 id="understanding-guaranteed-issue-insurance"><br><br><strong>What You Must Understand About Guaranteed Issue Burial Insurance  </strong></h2>

<p>Most guaranteed acceptance life insurance companies will
only require your age, gender, resident state information, and whether or not
you use tobacco products. The application process is simple and
straightforward.</p>

<p>A standard part of every guaranteed issue final expense life insurance no exam policy is a graded death benefit or limited benefit period. </p>

<p>Most companies will have a two-year waiting period for natural causes of death before the 100% death benefit will be available to your beneficiaries. </p>

<p><br><strong>WHAT IS A GRADED DEATH BENEFIT?</strong></p>

<p>A graded death benefit or limited benefit period is something every company includes in their plans to protect the company from having to pay large sums of money to people on their deathbed (those who will not be paying much into their policy). </p>

<p>A graded death benefit means there is a two to a four-year waiting period before they will give the full death benefit payout to the beneficiary. </p>

<p>If death occurs during the waiting period, the beneficiary will only receive a return of premium plus 7% to 10% interest. If death occurs from accidental causes during the waiting period, 100% death benefit will be paid out.</p>

<p><br><strong>HOW DOES A GRADED DEATH BENEFIT WORK?</strong></p>

<p>Death from accidental causes will not have a waiting period. The full death benefit amount will be paid to your beneficiaries. </p>

<p>If you die from natural causes or any reason other than an accident during the waiting period, your beneficiary will receive all premiums paid plus interest. </p>

<p>For example, you’re a 60-year-old female and pay $1,000 annually for your $25,000 guaranteed issue policy. If you are hospitalized for breast cancer and died one year after taking your policy, your beneficiaries will get $1,100. </p>

<p>If you pass away after waiting, your family will get the full $25,000.</p>

<p id="WHAT-IF-I-OUTLIVE-THE-GRADED-DEATH-BENEFIT-PERIOD"><br><strong>WHAT IF I OUTLIVE THE GRADED DEATH BENEFIT PERIOD?</strong></p>

<p>If you outlive the waiting period even for a day, your beneficiary will receive the 100% death benefit that you signed up for.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam"><br><strong>Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you are hospitalized.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<p>Here are some common situations where guaranteed issue life insurance may be appropriate:</p>

<ul>
<li>Terminal illness (your life expectancy is 24 months or less)</li>
<li>You have cancer within the last 24 months</li>
<li>End-stage kidney disease requiring dialysis</li>
<li>Diagnosed with AIDS or HIV</li>
<li>Alzheimer’s or dementia</li>
<li>Congestive heart failure (CHF)</li>
<li>Heart attack within the previous 12 months</li>
<li>A circulatory surgery within the previous 12 months</li>
<li>Full blown stroke (TIA mini-strokes do not count) within the previous 12 months</li>
<li>Diabetic coma or insulin shock&#160; within the last 24 months</li>
<li>Amputation due to diabetes</li>
<li>Angina (chest pain) within the last 12 months</li>
<li>Had a heart surgery within the previous 12 months</li>
<li>Been recommended to have or had an organ transplant</li>
<li>Oxygen use (only OK for sleep apnea)</li>
<li>Currently in the hospital, skilled nursing facility, or hospice care</li>
<li>Confined to a wheelchair or bed because of chronic illness</li>
<li>Needing assistance with any activities of daily living such as eating, bathing, dressing, transferring and continence</li>
