<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-a-disability"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A Disability?</strong></h2>

<p>The cause of your disability will play a major role in your life insurance eligibility.</p>

<p id="THE-CAUSE-OF-YOUR-DISABILITY-WILL-PLAY-A-MAJOR-ROLE-IN-LIFE-INSURANCE-APPROVAL"><strong>Here are the most common causes of disability:</strong></p>

<p><strong>CHRONIC PAIN</strong></p>

<p>Chronic pain such as the knee, back or arthritis can cause disability.</p>

<p>If this is your case, you can qualify for the level death benefit. However, insurance carriers will look into the types of medications you use to treat your pain which may have a significant effect on your life insurance eligibility.</p>

<p>If you are taking a prescription medication like Tramadol, Vicodin, Percocet or Hydrocodone, some insurance companies may charge a higher premium because these drugs have the potential for abuse.</p>

<p>If you are taking narcotic drugs, the insurance companies will want to confirm that you have no history of substance abuse. Anti-inflammatory and non-narcotic over-the-counter pain medications have less severe side effects which will make it easier to qualify for a level death benefit.</p>

<p>You will be fully covered immediately and your beneficiary will receive 100% of the death benefit when you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>ACTIVITIES OF DAILY LIVING</strong></p>

<p>Chronic pain is absolutely a non-issue with every burial insurance company.It will only pose a problem if your chronic pain causes you to need home health care or if you need help with activities of daily living. If this is your case, your best insurance option is guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p>You will qualify for this plan regardless of your disability. You don’t need to answer any health questions or take a medical exam to qualify for coverage.&#160;A guaranteed issue policy does not even ask how mild or severe your disability is because you will be covered with no health questions.</p>

<p>If you die from illness as a result of your other medical condition, the insurance company will pay your beneficiary all premiums you paid plus 10% interest.</p>

<p>After the two-year waiting period, your policy will pay 100% of the death benefit if you die for any reason.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>HEART DISEASE</strong></p>

<p>Heart disease includes many different heart conditions, such as heart attack, atrial fibrillation, arrhythmias, and even hypertension.</p>

<p>Some heart diseases, like congestive heart failure, may make applying for life insurance a challenge, but we know some life insurance carriers that will accept this condition.</p>

<p>The majority of heart diseases are non-issue, with most burial insurance companies. If you have heart disease, we can get you an affordable burial insurance plan. If your heart disease has been treated for more than two years. You will still qualify for a level death benefit plan.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>MENTAL HEALTH CONDITIONS</strong></p>

<p>Many people with a disability may suffer from different mental health conditions such as anxiety, depression, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, or post-traumatic stress disorder.</p>

<p>If you have mental health issues coupled with disability many burial life insurance companies will accept your application for a first-day coverage plan.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>CANCER 2+ YEARS AGO</strong></p>

<p>Cancer can cause a disability. Whatever type of cancer you have, and when you had it, you still qualify for a burial life insurance plan.</p>

<p>Most cancers that occurred longer than 2 years are a non-issue with most insurance companies. You will qualify for a level death benefit plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>CANCER LESS THAN 2 YEARS AGO</strong></p>

<p>If your cancer occurred within the last 2 years, you will only pay a little more for your policy and have a short waiting period.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>DIABETES</strong></p>

<p>Diabetes can cause disability, but most burial insurance companies will readily accept you with diabetes.</p>

<p>Even if you have diabetic complications like nephropathy, neuropathy, or retinopathy, it will may not even affect your application. You may still be able to get a level death benefit and immediate coverage with your diabetes.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p id="POLICY-OPTIONS-IF-YOU-HAVE-A-DISABILITY"><br><strong>HOW DO PEOPLE WITH SOCIAL SECURITY DISABILITY INSURANCE (SSDI) GET BURIAL INSURANCE?</strong></p>

<p>Most traditional life insurance carriers will flat out decline any applicant for coverage if they are currently receiving disability benefits.</p>

<p>Burial insurance is different than traditional life insurance. We work with companies that will approve people with a disability if the cause of the disability is not life-threatening and you have no other urgent medical issues.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>VA DISABILITY AND BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>If you’re a <a href="https://lifewealthwin.com/best-life-insurance-for-disabled-veterans/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">disabled veteran</a> who doesn’t collect SSDI, the approval process is often easier for burial insurance. Many veterans who collect VA disability benefits still work or own a business. Most of the time, they would pay the same premium as people who are not on disability.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Level death benefit with first-day coverage</p>

<p>None of the burial insurance companies care if you are receiving SSDI or veteran benefits. Only a few companies will ask if you are working, retired, or disabled. Being on disability is a non-issue to most life insurance companies.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Have A Disability, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with a disability.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple, and you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-disability"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have A Disability</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p>To get the lowest rates and level death benefit that protects you immediately from day one, you need to apply for burial insurance that has underwriting (asks a few health questions).</p>

<p>The first step to figuring out how much burial insurance you need is to know your end-of-life expenses. Your funeral cost is often the biggest single expense you need to pay. Other end-of-life expenses to consider are your outstanding medical bills, living expenses, credit card bills, and other debts.</p>
