<h1>Burial Insurance if you Need Help with ADL’s (Activities of Daily Living)</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• Needing help with ADLs signals high risk to insurance companies<br>• Most policies decline you or restrict coverage if you need assistance<br>• Guaranteed issue plans cost more and delay payouts for 2 years<br>• You’ll pay higher premiums for smaller, limited coverage amounts<br>• Waiting to apply only makes your options worse and more expensive<br>• Not understanding ADLs can lead to buying the wrong policy</p>

<p>Burial insurance with ADL (Activities of Daily Living) limitations is one of the biggest red flags in underwriting. ADLs are basic tasks like eating, bathing, dressing, and using the bathroom that people need to live independently. If you need help with even 1 or 2 of these, most traditional life insurance policies will decline you. That leaves burial insurance, a type of whole life insurance, usually in guaranteed-issue form. These plans are easier to get, but they cost more and delay full benefits. This is where most people unknowingly trade value for approval.</p>

<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-need-help-with-adl"><br><strong><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Need Help With ADL?</strong></strong></h2>

<p>The number of ADLs an applicant needs help with determines whether an insurance provider will approve your application. You need to perform activities of daily living (eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, toileting, continence, and transferring on your own. Needing help with a single ADL is an automatic decline for most traditional life insurance, and the only plan you will qualify for is guaranteed issue burial insurance.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option: </strong>Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<p><br><strong>GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>Guaranteed-issue policies do not require you to take a medical exam or pass any underwriting questions. The insurance company will accept your application without asking for any details about your health.</p>

<p>They do not ask any health questions, and they do not check your medical or prescription history. Even if you have a terminal illness, it will not matter, and you will be approved.</p>

<p>While it will cost a bit more than insurance with health questions and underwriting, you will not have to worry about the possibility of being denied coverage because of not being able to perform the activities of daily living.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance has a death benefit limitation for a natural cause of death during the first two years of the policy. After the two years waiting period, you are fully protected for life. At this point, the insurance company will pay 100% of the death benefit if you pass away for any reason.</p>

<p id="Requirements-to-Qualify-for-Guaranteed-Issue-Burial-Insurance"><br><strong>REQUIREMENTS TO QUALIFY FOR GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p><strong>AGE LIMITATIONS</strong></p>

<p>Every final expense company has its own age availability, but most guaranteed issue life insurance companies accept applicants who are 50 to 85 years old.</p>

<p><br><strong>STATE AVAILABILITY</strong></p>

<p>Not every insurance company has its products licensed in every state. Make sure the company has guaranteed acceptance life insurance policy available in your state.</p>

<p><br><strong>ABILITY TO ENTER A LEGAL CONTRACT</strong></p>

<p>You must have the mental capacity to enter into a legal contract. For example, the company may reject your application if you have advanced Alzheimer’s disease or dementia and cannot legally consent to an agreement.</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Need Help With Adl, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance if you need help with ADL.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-people-needing-help-with-adl"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Need Help With ADL</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p>Every burial insurance company with medical underwriting (they ask health questions) will ask about ADL’s in their life insurance application.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS:</strong></p>

<p><strong>Common life insurance questions about ADL:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Is the Proposed Insured currently receiving home health care or requires help with activities of daily living (ADL) such as eating,  dressing, toileting, or transferring?</li>
<li>Are you bedridden? Do you help with bathing, transferring, eating, toileting, or dressing?</li>
<li>Are you scheduled for admission or currently living in a nursing home or assisted living facility?</li>
<li>Do you need home health care, or, during the previous 24 months, have you received home health care for three or more separate periods of time?</li>
<li>Are you wheelchair-bound due to a chronic illness, or do you need help with activities of daily living such as bathing, dressing, eating, or toileting?</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer “yes” to any of these questions, your application will be automatically declined for traditional life insurance. However, you got other options for coverage, and that is by getting a guaranteed acceptance burial insurance that does not have any health questions.</p>

<p id="How-Does-Needing-Help-with-ADL-Affect-Life-Insurance?"><br><strong>HOW DOES NEEDING HELP WITH ADL AFFECT LIFE INSURANCE?</strong></p>

<p>Needing assistance from somebody else to perform activities of daily living will significantly affect your ability to qualify for the best-priced burial insurance policy.</p>

<p><strong>Problems in doing activities of daily living arise for many reasons and are closely linked to other health problems such as:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s disease</li>
<li>Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Cerebral palsy</li>
<li>Cystic Fibrosis</li>
<li>Dementia</li>
<li>Heart attack</li>
<li>Injury</li>
<li>Kidney failure</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Paralysis</li>
<li>Parkinson’s disease</li>
<li>Spina bifida</li>
<li>Stroke</li>
</ul>

<p>Almost any of these medical conditions, when serious enough, will stop you from performing one or more of the ADL’s. In some cases, the inability to do activities of everyday life may be due more to forgetting to perform a task or how to complete it correctly rather than doing the task physically.</p>

<p>Problems with activities of daily living and instrumental activities of daily living reflect problems with physical and mental health. Functional difficulties mean health problems that may cause premature death.</p>

<p>Any conditions that may cause premature death are considered high risk by life insurance companies.</p>
