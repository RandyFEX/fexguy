<h1>Senior Dollar</h1>

<p><strong>Welcome to Senior Dollar</strong>! (a fexguy.com resource)</p>

<p><strong>Your dollars should work as hard as you do!</strong></p>

<p>Seniors have spent a lifetime earning, saving, and planning. Senior Dollar exists to make sure that money goes as far as possible so you stay in control of your finances and your future.</p>

<p><strong>Our goal is simple.</strong></p>

<p>We give seniors the information and tools to manage their money wisely. Stretching retirement income, planning ahead for end-of-life costs, making smart decisions that keep more money in your pocket. </p>

<p>When your finances are in good shape, final expense life insurance is something you will likely never need. That is exactly where we want you to be.</p>

<p><strong>But life does not always go to plan.</strong></p>

<p>If you find yourself in a spot where covering final expenses through a policy makes sense, we can help with that too. Honest guidance on whether it is the right move and how to find the right coverage at a fair price.</p>

<p><strong>What we cover</strong></p>

<p>Stretching your retirement income. Planning ahead for end-of-life costs. Understanding your options, including final expense coverage if it comes to that</p>
