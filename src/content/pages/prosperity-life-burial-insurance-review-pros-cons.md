<p>Prosperity Life burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed for final expenses, and it stands out for offering first-day coverage to more people than many companies. But not everyone qualifies for that top plan. If your health is worse, you may be placed into a graded or modified policy where your family only gets partial payouts in the first 2 years or just a refund of premiums early on. Coverage is usually between $1,500 and $35,000, which is meant for funeral costs, not long-term financial protection. If you don’t understand which plan you’re getting, you can pay full price for limited early coverage.</p>

<h2 id="does-prosperity-offer-first-day-coverage"><br><strong><strong>Does Prosperity Life Offer First-day Coverage?</strong></strong></h2>

<p> <strong>YES</strong>, Prosperity Life Insurance offers a first-day coverage plan, but they tend to be more expensive than other companies.    </p>

<p> If you qualify, their New Vista Final Expense no medical exam insurance policy gives you full coverage from the first day. Your beneficiary will receive your 100% death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<h2 id="pros-of-prosperity-burial-insurance"><strong>Pros Of Prosperity Life Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Average pricing</strong> – the Level benefit plan pricing has average pricing that is in the same range as other big insurance companies offering first-day coverage plan.</p>

<p><strong>Non-smoker rates</strong> – smoker rate only applies to cigarette smoking. Tobacco and nicotine users can get non-tobacco rates equal to 30-40% savings on their premium.</p>

<p><strong>Semi-lenient underwriting</strong> – some medical conditions will qualify for first-day coverage.</p>

<h2 id="cons-of-prosperity-burial-insurance"><strong>Cons Of Prosperity Life Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>After approval policy support</strong> – getting policy support to get updates or changes to your policy is below average </p>

<p><strong>3-year lookback period</strong> – on cancer diagnosis and treatment</p>

<p><strong>Restrictive underwriting for blood thinner use</strong> – if you are using Coumadin, Warfarin, and other blood thinners for angina and heart attack prevention, you may be disqualified for coverage.</p>

<h2 id="prosperity-burial-insurance-products"><br><strong>Prosperity Life Burial Insurance Product</strong>s</h2>

<p>Prosperity Life Group offers their “New Vista” policy in 49 States and their “Golden Promise” in New York only. </p>

<p>Burial insurance policies from Prosperity Life are all simplified issue whole life insurance designed to provide cash benefits to cover end-of-life expenses. </p>

<p>There are three different eligibility ratings for their burial insurance program. Your health will determine which one you will qualify for. </p>

<p><br><strong>1. LEVEL BENEFIT PLAN</strong></p>

<p>This plan is best for three reasons. First, it has immediate coverage. You will get first-day coverage. Second, it has no waiting period. Third, this plan has the lowest-priced premium they offer.</p>

<p><strong>Face amounts:</strong> Between $1,500 and $35,000</p>

<p><strong>Issue ages:</strong> 50-80</p>

<p>Prosperity has a more lenient underwriting compared with
other life insurance companies. Many medical conditions qualify for first-day
coverage.</p>

<p><br><strong>2. GRADED BENEFIT PLAN</strong></p>

<p>Prosperity’s graded benefit plan is called The Golden Promise. This plan is offered to applicants who are not healthy enough to qualify for a level benefit plan. It also costs more than the level benefit plan.</p>

<p><strong>Face amounts:</strong> Between $1,500 and $35,000</p>

<p><strong>Issue ages:</strong> 50-75 Golden Promise                    </p>

<p><strong>Limited payout</strong> – The Golden Promise has a <strong>limited payout</strong> of benefits for the natural cause of death during the first two years. A graded benefit plan works this way:</p>

<p><strong>If the insured dies due to natural causes, the beneficiary will receive:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li><strong>1st year: 30% of the face amount </strong></li>
<li><strong>2nd year: 70% of the face amount</strong></li>
<li><strong>3rd year and beyond: 100% of the face amount</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>For accidental death from the first day of coverage – 100% of the face amount will be given to the beneficiary.<br></p>

<p>Their graded benefit plan is only suitable in a few rare situations. Some medical conditions on the graded plan will qualify for first-day coverage in other companies.</p>

<p><br><strong>3. MODIFIED BENEFIT PLAN</strong></p>

<p>A modified plan is the highest risk plan offered by the company. It is the most expensive of the three plans. This plan comes with a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>The modified benefit plan is designed for applicants with multiple or severe medical conditions who did qualify for the level benefit and graded benefit plan.</p>

<p><strong>Face amounts:</strong> Between $1,500 and $35,000</p>

<p><strong>Issue ages:</strong> 50-80</p>

<p><strong>2-year waiting period</strong> – The modified plan will payout 100% accidental death benefit from the first day of coverage.</p>

<p>If you pass away due to natural causes during the first year, the company will return 110% of the annual premium (excluding the policy fee).</p>

<p>On the 12-24 months of the policy, they would return 231% of the annual premium (excluding the policy fee). After the two-year waiting period, the death benefit is equal to the face amount of the plan.</p>

<p>Prosperity’s modified plan is not the best option because it is the most expensive plan with a two-year waiting period. </p>

<p>Overall, Prosperity Life offers average rates with semi-lenient underwriting. Prosperity may be your best option if you qualify for their level benefit plan. But, they may not be the best if you only qualify for their Graded or Modified plans.</p>

<h2 id="prosperity-burial-insurance-riders"><br><strong>Prosperity Life Burial Insurance Riders</strong></h2>

<p>Prosperity Life has no optional riders you can add to your policy, unlike other insurance companies. However, they include Accelerated Death Benefit Rider at no cost.</p>

<p>If you’re diagnosed with a terminal illness, you can access your death benefit while you are still alive. You can use between 25-50% of your face amount. If you decide to access this, the accelerated amount will be considered a policy loan and deducted from your total death benefit when you die.</p>

<h2 id="when-does-prosperity-make-the-most-sense"><br><strong>When Does Prosperity Life Burial Insurance Make The Most Sense?</strong></h2>

<p>Prosperity Life has a liberal underwriting. They accept all minor health issues. They also absorb higher-risk conditions. If you have any medical conditions, you will still qualify for a level benefit plan with first-day coverage.</p>

<ul>
<li>Asthma</li>
<li>Non-cigarette tobacco product users get non-tobacco rates </li>
<li>Blood clots</li>
<li>Depression (not bi-polar)</li>
<li>Non-cancerous tumors</li>
<li>Type 1 or 2 diabetics</li>
<li>Insulin injections for diabetics</li>
<li>Insulin shock after two years or more</li>
<li>Diabetic coma after two years or more</li>
<li>Diabetic amputations after two years or more</li>
<li>Pacemaker or defibrillator (installed for at least two years)</li>
<li>Irregular heartbeat issues such as Afib</li>
<li>Sleep Apnea</li>
<li>Seizure disorders like epilepsy</li>
<li>Multiple Sclerosis</li>
<li>Wheelchair-bound because of accident</li>
</ul>

<p>Purchasing Prosperity Life burial insurance with first-day coverage makes the most sense.</p>

<p>However, if you cannot qualify for this plan, we will help you to get approved by another company. Stay away from their graded and modified plans because they are expensive and have limited death benefit schedules.</p>

<h2 id="prosperity-underwriting-guidelines"><br><strong><strong>Prosperity Life Underwriting Guidelines</strong></strong></h2>

<p>You don’t have to take a medical exam to qualify for Prosperity Life burial insurance. You are only required to answer a set of health questions.</p>

<p><strong>The company’s decision if you qualify for coverage will depend on:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Health questions on the application</li>
<li>Prescription history</li>
<li>Medical Information Bureau (MIB) file</li>
<li>Height &amp; weight chart</li>
</ul>

<p>Your answers to the health questionnaire will carry the most
weight in determining what plan you will qualify for.</p>

<p>During your tele-application, the underwriter will electronically check your medication history to verify your health. Some medications are a red flag for specific conditions. If you are taking those meds, it may affect your eligibility for the best rates.</p>

<p>They will check your MIB file to look for the health conditions you have admitted to other companies you applied to in the past.</p>

<p>Prosperity uses a build chart for your height and weight to determine if you are eligible. If you are severely under or overweight, it may impact what plan you will qualify for.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONNAIRE</strong> </p>

<p>Answering health questions is part of the application
process. There are three parts of the health questions that you must answer
“no” to qualify for a level benefit plan.</p>

<p>Here are the health questions on the Prosperity Life Burial Insurance Application:</p>

<p><br><strong>PART 1: KNOCKOUT QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p><strong>Here are the knockout questions on the application. If you have any of the following medical conditions, you will not qualify for any of Prosperity’s burial insurance plans. They will not issue you any coverage.</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Is the Proposed Insured currently or in the last 30 days been: hospitalized, committed to a psychiatric facility, bedridden, confined to a nursing home, receiving hospice or home health care,&#160; wheelchair-bound due to a chronic illness or disease, or waiting for an organ transplant? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Does the Proposed insured currently require assistance or supervision with <a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">activities of daily living</a> such as taking medications, eating, bathing, dressing, toileting, continence, or transferring? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Within the past 12 months has the Proposed Insured:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Been advised by a member of the medical profession to have a diagnostic test (other than an HIV test), surgery, home health care or hospitalization which has not yet started, been completed or for which results are not known? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Used or been advised by a member of the medical profession to use oxygen equipment for assistance in Breathing (excluding CPAP or nebulizer)? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Had or been advised by a doctor to have <a href="/life-insurance-for-dialysis-patients/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Kidney Dialysis</a>? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Insured ever been diagnosed or treated for Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) and or Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infection by a licensed member of the medical profession? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Insured ever been diagnosed or received treatment for Alzheimer’s disease, dementia, Lou Gehrig’s/Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (<a href="/burial-insurance-als-lou-gehrigs-disease/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ALS</a>), <a href="/burial-insurance-cirrhosis/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Liver Cirrhosis</a> (Stage C) or been diagnosed as having a terminal illness that is expected to result in death within the next 24 months? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Insured ever been diagnosed with more than one occurrence of the same or different type of <a href="/life-insurance-for-cancer-patients/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">cancer</a> or is the Proposed Insured currently receiving treatment (including taking medication) for any form of cancer (excluding basal cell skin cancer)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answered “Yes” to any of the above medical conditions, we work with different A-rated life insurance companies, and we can often get you insurance coverage even with these health issues.</p>

<p>If you answer “No” to the knockout questions in Part 1, proceed to Part 2<br></p>

<p><strong>PART 2: MODIFIED BENEFIT QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p><strong>In the past two years, has the Proposed Insured been diagnosed or received treatment from a member of the medical profession, or other practitioners, or been hospitalized for any of the following:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>The use of alcohol or drugs; or been advised by a physician, practitioner,      health facility, or counselor to restrict the use of alcohol or drugs? (Yes – No)</li>
<li><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-diabetes-diabetic-complications/" target="_blank">Complications of diabetes</a>, such as diabetic coma or insulin shock or had an amputation due to complications of any disease?  (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Heart attack, angina (chest pain), congestive heart failure, cardiomyopathy stroke, transient ischemic attack (TIA), or aneurysm or had heart or <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="/burial-insurance-circulatory-surgery/" target="_blank">circulatory surgery</a>? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Insured had more than one conviction for reckless driving or driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs (DUI or DWI)? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the past three years, has the Proposed Insured been diagnosed, treated, or prescribed medication for internal cancer, including but not limited to,      malignant brain tumor, malignant melanoma (but excluding basal/squamous cell skin cancer), leukemia, or multiple myeloma? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the past 2 years, has the Proposed Insured had more than 1 conviction for reckless driving or for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs (DUI or DWI)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer “Yes” to any of the modified benefit questions, Prosperity will offer you their Modified Death Benefit plan.</p>

<p>If you answer “No” to the modified benefit questions, proceed on.<br></p>

<p><strong>PART 3: GRADED BENEFIT QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p><strong>Has the Proposed Insured ever been diagnosed, treated, or prescribed medication by a member of the medical profession for:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Parkinson’s disease, Systemic Lupus (SLE), or sickle cell disease? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Bipolar Disorder or Schizophrenia or been hospitalized in the past two years for any mental or nervous disorder? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Cirrhosis (Stage A or Stage B) of the liver, chronic hepatitis or other liver disorder, kidney failure, or other chronic kidney diseases? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), which includes emphysema, black lung disease, or tuberculosis? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>If you answer “YES” to any of the graded benefit
questions, Prosperity will offer you their Graded Benefit Plan.</p>

<p>If you answer “NO” to all the health questions in Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3, and your prescription history aligns with your health question answers, Prosperity will offer you Level Benefit with first-day coverage and their most affordable premium.</p>
