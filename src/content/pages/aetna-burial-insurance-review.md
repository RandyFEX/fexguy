<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>What to ask before buying Aetna life insurance</strong></h2>

<h3><strong>Does Aetna offer first-day coverage?</strong></h3>

<p>Aetna has a burial insurance plan that offers first-day coverage.</p>

<p>If you qualify, their Whole Life Insurance Level Benefit Plan comes with immediate first-day coverage. You will be 100% covered from the first day, and your beneficiary will receive your full death benefit when you pass away.</p>

<h3 id="h-what-are-my-policy-options-with-aetna"><strong>What are my policy options with Aetna?</strong></h3>

<p>Aetna, through its affiliated insurance companies, offers three types of burial insurance policy options:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Level benefit</strong>&#160;– first-day immediate coverage</li>
<li><strong>Graded benefit</strong>&#160;– limited benefits during the first two years</li>
<li><strong>Modified benefit</strong>&#160;– two-year waiting period</li>
</ol>

<p>Your age and current health status will determine which plan you will be eligible for and the amount you will pay.</p>

<p><strong>#1</strong> – <strong>LEVEL BENEFIT PLAN:</strong></p>

<p>Issue ages: 40 – 89, age last birthday</p>

<ul>
<li>40-55 death benefit: $2,000-$50,000</li>
<li>56-65 death benefit: $2,000-$40,000</li>
<li>66-75 death benefit: $2,000-$30,000</li>
<li>76-89 death benefit: $2,000-$25,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>The level benefit plan is designed for healthier folks, but it accepts these health issues:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>High blood pressure</li>
<li>High cholesterol</li>
<li>Controlled diabetes</li>
<li>Arthritis</li>
<li>Mental health issues (bipolar, anxiety disorder, depression)</li>
<li>And they have a two-year lookback period on full stroke, TIA, heart attack, and circulatory surgeries.</li>
</ul>

<p>Aetna’s level benefit plans are generally competitively priced.</p>

<p><strong>#2 – GRADED BENEFIT</strong></p>

<p>This graded plan is from an older version of Aetna’s final expense product. Current carrier materials we found list only level and modified benefits, so ask whether a graded plan is available in your state.</p>

<p>Issue ages: 40 – 75, age last birthday</p>

<ul>
<li>Minimum death benefit amount: $2,000</li>
<li>Maximum death benefit: $25,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Death Benefits Schedule:</strong></p>

<p>Accidental death: full benefit immediately</p>

<p><strong>Non-accidental death:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First 12 Months: 40% of the benefit amount</li>
<li>12 – 24 Months: 75% of the benefit amount</li>
<li>After 24 Months: full death benefit</li>
</ul>

<p>Aetna takes a higher risk for insuring you that’s why your beneficiary will receive 40% if you pass away in the first year of the policy. 75% in the second year, and only in the third year will they receive 100% of your benefit.</p>

<p><strong>Health conditions that will qualify for this plan:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Congestive heart failure</li>
<li>Cardiomyopathy</li>
<li>COPD</li>
<li>Emphysema</li>
<li>Cancer diagnosis in the last 12-24 months</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>#3</strong> – <strong>MODIFIED BENEFIT PLAN </strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Issue ages: 40 – 75, age last birthday</li>
<li>Minimum death benefit amount: $2,000</li>
<li>Maximum death benefit amount: $25,000</li>
<li>Death Benefits Schedule:</li>
</ul>

<p>Accidental death – 100% benefit immediately</p>

<p><strong>Non-accidental death:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First 24 Months: 110% of paid premium</li>
<li>After 24 Months: Full death benefit</li>
</ul>

<p>The modified benefit plan has a full two-year waiting period. If you pass away during the first two years, they will pay your beneficiary the premium you paid plus 10% interest. Only in the third year will Aetna pay your full death benefit amount.</p>

<p>Aetna’s modified benefit plan is expensive compared to a guaranteed issue, no health questions policy. GI policy costs less, and it will approve your application regardless of the severity of your medical condition.</p>

<h3><strong>How is Aetna final expense whole life insurance good for seniors?</strong></h3>

<p>Aetna’s three final expense whole life insurance plans help ease the financial burden. However, among the three plans, the most beneficial is the level-benefit plan. This plan comes with first-day coverage, and the pricing is competitive. The graded plan is best for some health issues. </p>

<p>We do not recommend the modified plan for seniors because it has a two-year waiting period. Seniors will be better off with a guaranteed issue life insurance compared with this plan. A guaranteed issue policy will accept all applicants regardless of their medical condition.</p>

<h3><strong>Does Aetna have any hidden “Fine Print” in their policy?</strong></h3>

<p>You must be aware of the death benefit schedule when considering Aetna’s graded and modified benefit plans. I don’t recommed these plans, as most people will qualify for AETNA’s 1st-day coverage plan.</p>

<p><strong>Graded Death Benefit</strong><br>Non-accidental death </p>

<ul>
<li>Policy year 1: 40%of benefit amount </li>
<li>Policy year 2: 75% of benefit amount </li>
<li>Policy year 3: Full death benefit</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Modified Death Benefit</strong></p>

<p>Non-accidental death</p>

<ul>
<li>Policy years 1 and 2: ROP plus 10% </li>
<li>Policy year 3: full death benefit</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="getting-approved"><strong>How do I get approved by Aetna?</strong></h2>

<p><strong>To qualify for burial insurance, Aetna will check your health in three ways:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>They will ask health questions in the application</li>
<li>They will electronically check your prescription history</li>
<li>They will perform a telephone interview</li>
</ul>

<p>They do this to verify your health and assess your level of risk.</p>
