<h1>CICA Life Burial Insurance Review</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• CICA Life is weaker financially than the top life insurance companies<br>• Guaranteed issue plans have a 2-year delay before full payout<br>• Coverage amounts are small and may not cover the full final expenses<br>• Customer complaints include slow claims and hard-to-reach service<br>• It works for serious health issues when other companies decline you<br>• Better carriers often offer stronger coverage at similar or lower cost</p>

<p>CICA Life burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed for final expenses, with easy approval and no medical exam. The appeal is simple. You can qualify even with serious health problems, and some plans offer first-day coverage. The problem is tradeoffs. The company has a lower financial strength rating than top competitors, and guaranteed issue plans come with a 2-year waiting period before full benefits pay. This makes it a backup option, not a first choice, unless your health limits everything else.</p>

<h3 id="h-what-are-my-policy-options-with-cica-life"><strong>What are my policy options with CICA Life?</strong></h3>

<p><strong>CICA Life offers two final expense insurance products:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Superior Choice Standard Plan</li>
<li>Superior Choice Guaranteed Issue policy</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>CICA Life Superior Choice Standard</strong> – CICA Life’s first-day coverage plan. It does not require a medical exam, you only need to answer basic health questions.</p>

<p>We highly recommend this plan for people with significant health issues like uncontrolled high blood pressure, stroke, TIA, uncontrolled diabetes and diabetic complications, congestive heart failure, heart disease, cardiomyopathy, lung diseases including COPD and emphysema, liver cirrhosis, liver failure, kidney failure, kidney insufficiency, kidney disease even kidney dialysis.</p>

<p><strong>CICA Life Superior Choice Guaranteed Issue</strong> – CICA Life’s burial insurance product that accepts all health issues. The only downside to this plan is the two-year waiting period. If you die during the waiting period, CICA Life will pay 110% of the premiums you paid.</p>

<h2 id="How-Do-I-Get-Approved"><strong>How do I get approved by CICA Life?</strong></h2>

<p>CICA Life’s standard plan will ask if you’ve had health issues in the last 12, 24 months, 5 years, and 10 years.</p>

<h3 id="h-cica-life-application-questions"><strong>CICA Life application questions</strong></h3>

<p><strong>A)</strong> Are you <strong>CURRENTLY</strong> hospitalized, confined to a bed or nursing facility, residing in an assisted living facility, receiving hospice care, or do you have any physical or mental impairment for which you need or receive assistance or supervision in performing normal activities of daily living, unable to care for yourself, or terminally ill? (Terminally ill means an illness or physical condition, including a physical injury, that can reasonably be expected to result in death within 12 months. A Terminal illness is based on diagnosis by a member of the medical profession.)</p>

<p><strong>B)</strong> Have you <strong>EVER</strong> been diagnosed by a member of the medical profession or tested positive for Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV), Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS)?</p>

<p><strong>C)</strong> Have you <strong>BEEN DIAGNOSED</strong> by a member of the medical profession with MORE THAN ONE occurrence of <strong>ANY</strong> cancer, a recurrence of any cancer, metastasis of any cancer, or currently being treated for cancer (excluding basal cell or squamous cell skin cancer)?</p>

<p><strong>D)</strong> In the <strong>PAST 10 YEARS</strong>, have you been medically diagnosed, for which you have NOT been treated by a member of the medical profession, or have NOT taken medication for the following: uncontrolled diabetes, uncontrolled high blood pressure, stroke/TIA, paralysis, Congestive Heart Failure, heart disease, cardiomyopathy, lung disease (including COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease)/emphysema), liver cirrhosis or failure, kidney (renal) failure/insufficiency, or chronic/end-stage kidney disease (including dialysis)?</p>

<p><strong>E)</strong> Have you <strong>EVER</strong> been medically diagnosed, treated by a member of the medical profession, or prescribed medication for mental disorder, disorder of the brain or nervous system, Systemic Lupus (SLE), Alzheimer’s disease, dementia, brain disease, organic brain syndrome, Lou Gehrig’s disease (ALS), Huntington’s disease, Muscular Dystrophy, Cystic Fibrosis, Pulmonary Fibrosis, or Multiple Myeloma?</p>

<p><strong>F)</strong> In the <strong>PAST 2 YEARS</strong>, have you been hospitalized 2 or more times, or have you been advised or recommended by a member of the medical profession to have any tests (except for tests relating to Human Immunodeficiency Virus (AIDS virus)), treatment, surgery, or hospitalization which has not been received or completed?</p>

<p><strong>G)</strong> Within the <strong>PAST 2 YEARS</strong>, have you been treated for or been advised by a medical professional to have treatment for alcohol, drug, opioid, or controlled substance abuse, plead guilty or been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor for any reason?</p>

<p><strong>H)</strong> Within the <strong>LAST 5 YEARS</strong> have you been advised to by a member of the medical profession to have an organ transplant?</p>

<p><strong>I) </strong>Within the <strong>LAST 2 YEARS</strong>, have you received medical advice or treatment following an attempted suicide?</p>
