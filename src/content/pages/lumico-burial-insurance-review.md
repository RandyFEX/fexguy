<h2 id="does-lumico-offer-first-day-coverage"><br><strong><strong>Does Lumico Offer First-day Coverage?</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>YES</strong>, Lumico Life has a burial insurance plan that offers first-day coverage, but they tend to be more expensive than other companies.  </p>

<p>If you qualify, their Preferred whole life plan will cover you from day one. Once approved, the company will pay a 100%  death benefit when you pass away. </p>

<h2 id="pros-of-lumico-burial-insurance"><strong>Pros Of Lumico Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Diabetes-friendly</strong> – accept insulin use if you use less than 40 units daily.</p>

<p><strong>Seizure friendly</strong> – seizures qualify for the preferred plan</p>

<p><strong>Accepts mild mental health disorders</strong> – depression and anxiety qualify for the preferred plan</p>

<h2 id="cons-of-lumico-burial-insurance"><strong>Cons Of Lumico Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Strict underwriting</strong> – most health issues will not be eligible for the preferred plan</p>

<p><strong>3-year waiting period</strong> – standard and modified plans have a 3-year waiting period</p>

<p><strong>Blood thinners are a red-flag</strong> – If they found blood thinners on your prescription history, you will be offered a standard plan with a 3-year waiting period</p>

<h2 id="lumico-burial-insurance-products"><br><strong>Lumico Burial Insurance Products</strong></h2>

<p>Lumico Life has three burial insurance policies you can be eligible for depending on your health and age (ages 50-85 only). </p>

<p>Their final expense life insurance is a “simplified issue” and does not require applicants to undergo a medical exam. You only need to answer some simple health questions. </p>

<p>Your answer to the health questions will determine what type of policy you can buy. Every plan has its own age availability, pricing, face amount, and payout schedules.</p>

<p id="LUMICO-BURIAL-INSURANCE-REVIEW-POLICY-FEATURES"><strong>LUMICO BURIAL INSURANCE FEATURES</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Phone application process with an agent</li>
<li>Lasts until the end of life</li>
<li>Premium payment is fixed, and monthly payments won’t increase</li>
<li>The policy won’t expire because of age or health condition</li>
<li>Death benefits won’t decrease</li>
<li>Tax-free cash benefit payout</li>
<li>Coverage amount up to $30,000 </li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE</strong> <strong>PREFERRED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-85</li>
<li>Face amount you can buy:&#160; $5,000-$30,000</li>
<li>Death benefit schedule: Instant, no waiting period effective on day one.</li>
</ul>

<p>Applicants who qualify for preferred burial insurance can
expect a level death benefit with no waiting period. The minimum coverage
amount is $5,000 and goes as high as $30,000.</p>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE STANDARD PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-85</li>
<li>Face amount you can buy: $5,000-$30,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Death benefit schedule: </strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-year – 25%</li>
<li>Second-year – 50%</li>
<li><strong>Third-year – 75%</strong></li>
<li>Fourth-year – 100%</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Can we be honest here….waiting three years for full coverage is a terrible idea! </strong></p>

<p>Especially since almost every insurance company offers the same plan with only a 2-year wait if you have significant health problems.</p>

<p>Applicants who will qualify for standard burial insurance will have a three-year modified death benefit or a three-year waiting period…ouch!</p>

<p>For example, if the insured dies in the first year of the policy, beneficiaries will receive 25% of the death benefit. If death occurs in the second year, 50% will be given, and the beneficiaries will receive 75% in the third year of the policy. After the third year and any time after, they will receive 100% of the death benefit.</p>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE MODIFIED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-80</li>
<li>Face amount options: $5,000-$15,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Payout schedule:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-year – 10%</li>
<li>Second-year – 25%</li>
<li><strong>Third-year – 50%</strong></li>
<li>Fourth-year – 100%</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Holy cow…there is that three-year waiting period again!</strong></p>

<p>If you qualify for a modified benefit plan, the maximum coverage amount is reduced to $15,000. This plan is also available up to 80 years old only. The modified death benefit plan also has a graded death benefit with a three-year waiting period. </p>

<p><strong>The death benefit will be paid as follows:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>If death occurs in the first year of the policy, the payout will be 10% of the actual death benefit.</li>
<li>In the second year, 25% will be paid to the beneficiaries, and in the third year, 50%. </li>
<li>After the third year and any time after, a full death benefit will be given to the beneficiaries.</li>
</ul>

<p>Lumico’s modified plan is unsuitable for everybody because it has a waiting period. This is only suitable for those people with certain health conditions that the modified policy accepts. </p>

<p>If you are healthy, this plan will cost you an arm and a leg. If your health only qualifies you for this plan, we have other companies that offer a better price.</p>

<h2 id="lumico-burial-insurance-riders"><br><strong>Lumico Burial Insurance Riders</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Accidental Death Rider</strong> – pays out an additional lump sum if you die from an accident.</p>

<p><strong>Accelerated Death Benefit Rider</strong> – accelerates a portion of the death benefit if you’re diagnosed with a terminal condition with a life expectancy of 12 months or less.</p>

<h2 id="when-does-lumico-make-the-most-sense"><br><strong>When Does Lumico Burial Insurance Make The Most Sense?</strong></h2>

<p>If you are healthy, Lumico’s preferred plan may make sense for some people, but better-priced plans are certainly available.</p>

<p>If you have a medical condition resulting in a standard or modified plan, think twice before purchasing a Lumico policy. They have a three-year waiting period that stinks compared to other companies that only have a two-year waiting period. </p>

<p>Again, there are lots of insurance companies that are accepting of most health issues and only have a two-year waiting period. </p>

<h2 id="lumico-underwriting-guidelines"><br><strong><strong>Lumico Underwriting Guidelines</strong></strong></h2>

<p>A medical exam is not required to qualify with Lumico Life burial insurance coverage. </p>

<p><strong>They will only check:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Your answers in the application</li>
<li>Your Medical Information Bureau file</li>
<li>Your prescription history but the answer to the health questionnaire is the most important factor that will decide your approval.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>Lumico will ask about different medical conditions as part
of their underwriting process.</p>

<p><br><strong>PREFERRED PLAN:</strong></p>

<p>The following questions are the knock-out questions. Answering “no” to these two questions on the health questionnaire and a clear prescription history check from all these medical conditions means you are eligible for Lumico’s <strong>preferred plan</strong>.</p>

<ul>
<li>Are you currently hospitalized, requiring nursing home care, confined to a bed, or receiving hospice care? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have a terminal condition (terminal condition means a medical condition resulting from injury or illness that is reasonably expected to result in the Insured’s death within 12 months or less)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>STANDARD PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Were you diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease? Are you suffering from permanent paralysis from any disease? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the previous 24 months, did you have a diagnosis for any circulatory disorder such as aneurysm, heart attack, TIA attack, stroke, internal cancer, leukemia, melanoma, kidney disease, connective tissue disorder, a neuromuscular disorder such as muscular dystrophy, multiple sclerosis, cerebral palsy, etc),   Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis, any psychiatric issues that need treatment? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the past 24 months, did you have more than 4 moving violations? Did you have a DUI conviction more than once? Have you been advised to seek alcohol or drug abuse treatment? Do you have a felony conviction, incarceration, probation, or parole? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>You will be offered the standard plan if you say no to the knock-out question and yes to any of these questions.</p>

<p><br><strong>MODIFIED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Do you use supplementary oxygen for lung and respiratory issues? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you need hospitalizations or surgeries? Do you have diagnostic tests that haven’t been completed yet, and the results are unknown? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you require help performing daily living activities (eating,  bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, and continence)? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have an organ or bone marrow transplant, cardiomyopathy or congestive heart failure, mental incapacity, dementia, Alzheimer’s disease, ALS, dialysis, HIV, or AIDS? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have a diabetes diagnosis before age 40? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Any diabetic complications resulting in heart or circulatory conditions? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the last 12 months, have you been treated for a TIA attack, stroke,  heart valve disorder, heart attack or heart blockage,    any form of cancer, melanoma, lymphoma, leukemia, systemic lupus, or lung disease (does not include mild asthma)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>Any yes answer to the following questions will qualify you for the <strong>modified plan</strong>.</p>

<p>The type of plan (preferred, standard, or modified) you will qualify for will depend on your answers to these health questions. If you say no to all of these, you will be eligible for the best plan, which is preferred.</p>

<p><br><strong>PRESCRIPTION HISTORY CHECK</strong></p>

<p>Lumico will electronically review your prescription history to check what medications you are taking to treat any health issues. A prescription history check will be used to validate what medical condition you’ve been treating in the past. </p>

<p>Some prescription medications are a red flag and can cause you to not qualify for the preferred plan. If you are taking any prescription medications for health issues they care about, they may respond negatively. </p>

<p>For example, suppose you say no to kidney failure but are taking medication to control a severe kidney condition. In that case, Lumico will offer you a plan consistent with someone with kidney failure.</p>

<p><br><strong>MEDICAL INFORMATION BUREAU (MIB) REPORT</strong></p>

<p>MIB provides information about previous life insurance application results. </p>

<p>This report is shared among insurance companies for review. If you applied for life insurance in the last six months and were denied coverage, this information will be written in your MIB file, which will be available to Lumico Life should you decide to apply.</p>

<p>For example, if you inform the other insurance company you applied to before that you have cancer, this information will be on your MIB report. Depending on when you last used it, that could affect the kind of plan you qualify for.</p>

<h2 id="lumico-company-overview"><br><strong>Lumico Company Overview</strong></h2>

<p>Burial insurance from Lumico is underwritten by Lumico Life Insurance Company, owned by <a href="https://www.iptiq.com/choose-region/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow">iptiQ</a> (formerly known as Generation Life Insurance Company). The parent company is iptiQ Americas, backed by Swiss Re (Swiss Reinsurance Company), a large global financial services company founded in 1865 and headquartered in Lincoln, NE.</p>

<p>Swiss Reinsurance is an old reinsurance company based out of Zurich, Switzerland. They are a financially stable company. Lumico Life Insurance Company was acquired through Swiss Reinsurer’s new iptiQ Americas, Inc. unit at the end of 2016 and re-named in 2017 Lumico Life.
