<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• Lumico stopped accepting new applications (its Help Center gives January 8, 2025); existing policies are not affected<br>• Lumico offered easy approval, but strict health rules limited its best plans<br>• Most people didn’t qualify for first-day coverage and got worse options<br>• Standard and modified plans come with a long 3-year waiting period<br>• Some medications like blood thinners triggered reduced benefit policies<br>• Coverage amounts are smaller and focused only on final expenses</p>

<p>Lumico burial insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed to cover funeral costs, but your results depended heavily on your health. Lumico burial insurance used simplified underwriting with no medical exam, which made approval easier, but also created strict cutoffs for better plans. If you didn’t qualify for their top tier, you were likely placed into a policy with a 3-year waiting period where full benefits don’t pay right away. That’s longer than most competitors, which is a major drawback if your health isn’t perfect.</p>

<p><strong>IMPORTANT: Lumico stopped accepting new applications in late 2024 to early 2025. Its Help Center says that as of January 8, 2025, Lumico is no longer accepting new applications, and that existing customers are not affected; an earlier notice to agents gave October 1, 2024 as the end of new life insurance business. The plan details below describe Lumico’s final expense plans as they were sold. They can help you understand a Lumico policy you already own, but you can’t buy a new one.</strong></p>

<h2 id="does-lumico-offer-first-day-coverage"><br><strong><strong>Does Lumico offer first-day coverage?</strong></strong></h2>

<p><strong>YES</strong>, while Lumico was accepting applications, its Preferred plan offered first-day coverage. Lumico no longer accepts new applications.  </p>

<p>If you qualified, their Preferred whole life plan covered you from day one, with the full death benefit payable from the start. </p>

<h2 id="pros-of-lumico-burial-insurance"><strong>Pros of Lumico burial insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Diabetes-friendly</strong> – accepted insulin use if you used less than 40 units daily.</p>

<p><strong>Seizure friendly</strong> – seizures qualified for the preferred plan</p>

<p><strong>Accepted mild mental health disorders</strong> – depression and anxiety qualified for the preferred plan</p>

<h2 id="cons-of-lumico-burial-insurance"><strong>Cons of Lumico burial insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Strict underwriting</strong> – most health issues were not eligible for the preferred plan</p>

<p><strong>3-year waiting period</strong> – standard and modified plans have a 3-year waiting period</p>

<p><strong>Blood thinners were a red flag</strong> – If they found blood thinners on your prescription history, you were offered a standard plan with a 3-year waiting period</p>

<h2 id="lumico-burial-insurance-products"><br><strong>Lumico burial insurance products</strong></h2>

<p>Lumico Life had three burial insurance policies you could be eligible for depending on your health and age (ages 50-85; 50-80 for the Modified plan). </p>

<p>Their final expense life insurance is a “simplified issue” and does not require applicants to undergo a medical exam. You only need to answer some simple health questions. </p>

<p>Your answers to the health questions determined what type of policy you could buy. Every plan had its own age availability, pricing, face amount, and payout schedule.</p>

<p id="LUMICO-BURIAL-INSURANCE-REVIEW-POLICY-FEATURES"><strong>LUMICO BURIAL INSURANCE FEATURES</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Phone application process with an agent</li>
<li>Lasts until the end of life</li>
<li>Premium payment is fixed, and monthly payments won’t increase</li>
<li>The policy won’t expire because of age or health condition</li>
<li>Death benefits won’t decrease</li>
<li>Tax-free cash benefit payout</li>
<li>Coverage amount up to $30,000 </li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE</strong> <strong>PREFERRED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-85</li>
<li>Face amounts sold:&#160; $5,000-$30,000</li>
<li>Death benefit schedule: Instant, no waiting period effective on day one.</li>
</ul>

<p>Applicants who qualified for preferred burial insurance got
a level death benefit with no waiting period. The minimum coverage
amount was $5,000 and went as high as $30,000.</p>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE STANDARD PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-85</li>
<li>Face amounts sold: $5,000-$30,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Death benefit schedule: </strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-year – 25%</li>
<li>Second-year – 50%</li>
<li><strong>Third-year – 75%</strong></li>
<li>Fourth-year – 100%</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Can we be honest here….waiting three years for full coverage is a terrible idea! </strong></p>

<p>Especially since almost every insurance company offers the same plan with only a 2-year wait if you have significant health problems.</p>

<p>Applicants who qualified for standard burial insurance got a three-year modified death benefit or a three-year waiting period…ouch!</p>

<p>For example, if the insured dies in the first year of the policy, beneficiaries will receive 25% of the death benefit. If death occurs in the second year, 50% will be given, and the beneficiaries will receive 75% in the third year of the policy. After the third year and any time after, they will receive 100% of the death benefit.</p>

<p><br><strong>LUMICO LIFE MODIFIED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age availability: 50-80</li>
<li>Face amount options: $5,000-$15,000</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Payout schedule:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-year – 10%</li>
<li>Second-year – 25%</li>
<li><strong>Third-year – 50%</strong></li>
<li>Fourth-year – 100%</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Holy cow…there is that three-year waiting period again!</strong></p>

<p>If you qualified for a modified benefit plan, the maximum coverage amount was reduced to $15,000. This plan was also available only up to age 80. The modified death benefit plan also has a graded death benefit with a three-year waiting period. </p>

<p><strong>The death benefit will be paid as follows:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>If death occurs in the first year of the policy, the payout will be 10% of the actual death benefit.</li>
<li>In the second year, 25% will be paid to the beneficiaries, and in the third year, 50%. </li>
<li>After the third year and any time after, a full death benefit will be given to the beneficiaries.</li>
</ul>

<p>Lumico’s modified plan was not suitable for everybody because it has a waiting period. It was only meant for people with certain health conditions that the modified policy accepted. </p>

<p>If you were healthy, this plan would cost you an arm and a leg. If your health only qualifies you for a reduced-benefit plan today, other companies may offer plans with a shorter, two-year waiting period.</p>

<h2 id="lumico-burial-insurance-riders"><br><strong>Lumico burial insurance riders</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Accidental Death Rider</strong> – pays out an additional lump sum if you die from an accident.</p>

<p><strong>Accelerated Death Benefit Rider</strong> – accelerates a portion of the death benefit if you’re diagnosed with a terminal condition with a life expectancy of 12 months or less.</p>

<h2 id="when-does-lumico-make-the-most-sense"><br><strong>When does Lumico burial insurance make the most sense?</strong></h2>

<p>Lumico no longer accepts new applications, so it isn’t an option for new coverage. If you already own a Lumico Preferred plan, it gives you first-day coverage, so keep it in force.</p>

<p>If you own a Standard or Modified plan, remember its three-year reduced-benefit period, which is longer than the two-year waiting period many other companies use. Compare your options before making a change, and never cancel your Lumico policy until a replacement is approved and active. </p>

<p>Again, there are lots of insurance companies that are accepting of most health issues and only have a two-year waiting period. </p>

<h2 id="lumico-underwriting-guidelines"><br><strong><strong>Lumico underwriting guidelines</strong></strong></h2>

<p>These are the underwriting rules Lumico used while it accepted applications. A medical exam was not required to qualify for Lumico Life burial insurance coverage. </p>

<p><strong>They only checked:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Your answers in the application</li>
<li>Your Medical Information Bureau file</li>
<li>Your prescription history, but your answers to the health questionnaire were the most important factor in deciding your approval.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>Lumico asked about different medical conditions as part
of their underwriting process.</p>

<p><br><strong>PREFERRED PLAN:</strong></p>

<p>The following questions are the knock-out questions. Answering “no” to these two questions on the health questionnaire and a clear prescription history check from all these medical conditions meant you were eligible for Lumico’s <strong>preferred plan</strong>.</p>

<ul>
<li>Are you currently hospitalized, requiring nursing home care, confined to a bed, or receiving hospice care? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have a terminal condition (terminal condition means a medical condition resulting from injury or illness that is reasonably expected to result in the Insured’s death within 12 months or less)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>STANDARD PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Were you diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease? Are you suffering from permanent paralysis from any disease? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the previous 24 months, did you have a diagnosis for any circulatory disorder such as aneurysm, heart attack, TIA attack, stroke, internal cancer, leukemia, melanoma, kidney disease, connective tissue disorder, a neuromuscular disorder such as muscular dystrophy, multiple sclerosis, cerebral palsy, etc),   Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis, any psychiatric issues that need treatment? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the past 24 months, did you have more than 4 moving violations? Did you have a DUI conviction more than once? Have you been advised to seek alcohol or drug abuse treatment? Do you have a felony conviction, incarceration, probation, or parole? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>You were offered the standard plan if you said no to the knock-out questions and yes to any of these questions.</p>

<p><br><strong>MODIFIED PLAN:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Do you use supplementary oxygen for lung and respiratory issues? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you need hospitalizations or surgeries? Do you have diagnostic tests that haven’t been completed yet, and the results are unknown? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you require help performing daily living activities (eating,  bathing, dressing, toileting, transferring, and continence)? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have an organ or bone marrow transplant, cardiomyopathy or congestive heart failure, mental incapacity, dementia, Alzheimer’s disease, ALS, dialysis, HIV, or AIDS? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Do you have a diabetes diagnosis before age 40? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>Any diabetic complications resulting in heart or circulatory conditions? (Yes – No)</li>
<li>In the last 12 months, have you been treated for a TIA attack, stroke,  heart valve disorder, heart attack or heart blockage,    any form of cancer, melanoma, lymphoma, leukemia, systemic lupus, or lung disease (does not include mild asthma)? (Yes – No)</li>
</ul>

<p>Any yes answer to these questions qualified you for the <strong>modified plan</strong>.</p>

<p>The type of plan (preferred, standard, or modified) you qualified for depended on your answers to these health questions. If you said no to all of these, you were eligible for the best plan, which was preferred.</p>

<p><br><strong>PRESCRIPTION HISTORY CHECK</strong></p>

<p>Lumico electronically reviewed your prescription history to check what medications you were taking to treat any health issues. The prescription history check was used to validate what medical conditions you had been treating. </p>

<p>Some prescription medications were a red flag and could keep you from qualifying for the preferred plan. If you were taking prescription medications for health issues they cared about, they could respond negatively. </p>

<p>For example, suppose you said no to kidney failure but were taking medication to control a severe kidney condition. In that case, Lumico would offer you a plan consistent with someone with kidney failure.</p>

<p><br><strong>MEDICAL INFORMATION BUREAU (MIB) REPORT</strong></p>

<p>MIB provides information about previous life insurance application results. </p>

<p>This report is shared among insurance companies for review. If you applied for life insurance in the last six months and were denied coverage, this information will be written in your MIB file, which was available to Lumico Life when you applied.</p>

<p>For example, if you inform the other insurance company you applied to before that you have cancer, this information will be on your MIB report. Depending on when you last applied, that could affect the kind of plan you qualified for.</p>
