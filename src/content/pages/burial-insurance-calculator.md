<h2 id="what-is-burial-insurance"><br><strong>What Is Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>Burial insurance is also called funeral, cremation, or final expense insurance. It is intended to cover the cost of a funeral and other final expenses. </p>

<p>Purchasing a burial insurance policy in advance will ensure that your loved ones are not left with a mountain of debt after dying.</p>

<p>Most families don’t have the funds to pay off the debt incurred from the funeral. A study shows that approximately 62% of Americans only have less than $1,000 in their savings account. If they only have less than $1,000 in savings, how can their family afford to pay for a funeral that could cost more than $10,000?</p>

<p>That’s the reason why buying burial insurance is important. Final expense life insurance can protect your family from the financial burden that could be incurred when you die.</p>

<p>Burial insurance can be an excellent alternative for people who won’t qualify for traditional life insurance because of a medical condition (or multiple health conditions). </p>

<p>Many health issues can disqualify you from traditional life insurance and can cause your monthly premiums to be unaffordable. The older you are when you apply for traditional life insurance, the higher your cost will be. This is why burial insurance is an affordable alternative for so many families.</p>

<p>You can qualify for burial insurance easily because it has “relaxed” underwriting. </p>

<p>Most people qualify because there is no medical exam, and you only need to answer a few questions about your health (some even ask no health questions). </p>

<p>We can help you submit your application over the internet, and you can get approved without needing to wait weeks or months to know if you are approved for coverage.</p>

<p><a href="/the-importance-of-burial-insurance/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Burial insurance</a> can range in coverage anywhere from $2,000 up to $50,000, depending on the type of coverage you need.</p>

<p><strong>Final expense insurance is whole life insurance that works this way:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Fixed monthly payments for life (pricing never increases)</li>
<li>The death benefit amount is guaranteed never to decrease</li>
<li>The policy will never expire (it will last until you are 121 years old)</li>
<li>Accumulates cash value</li>
</ul>

You are covered&#160;for your whole life, and the premiums will never increase in the future (beware of some policies advertised on TV that expire after you pass 80 years of age).</li>

<h2 id="when-to-buy-burial-insurance"><br><strong>When Do You Need To Buy Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>Your burial insurance purchase depends on your financial situation.</p>

<p><strong>Burial insurance is a wise decision if:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>You have no life insurance coverage</li>
<li>Your savings don’t have enough to cover the cost of a funeral</li>
<li>You have any outstanding debt</li>
<li>Your family and friends do not have the financial capacity to cover the cost of our funeral and burial expenses.</li>
<li>You don’t want your loved ones to be burdened when you pass away.</li>
<li>You want a separate insurance policy to cover your final expenses.</li>
</ol>

<p>Burial insurance is ideal for people who have no life insurance coverage. If you cannot afford traditional life insurance due to the monthly premiums, then burial insurance’s lower premium is right for you. </p>

<p>Additionally, burial insurance will be your best option if you’ve been declined life insurance coverage.</p>

<h2 id="burial-insurance-costs"><br><strong>Burial Insurance Costs</strong></h2>

<p>The average burial insurance cost is typically&#160;less than $50 per month, and the most common coverage amount is $10,000.</p>

<p>You can pay your premium weekly, monthly, semi-annually, or even annually.</p>

<p><strong>Your specific monthly premium might be higher or lower depending on different factors such as:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Age</li>
<li>Gender</li>
<li>Face Amount</li>
<li>Tobacco usage</li>
<li>Health condition</li>
<li>Pre-existing condition</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>AGE</strong></p>

<p>Every burial insurance company has a set price for each specific age. </p>

<p>Some will have a price for age 50, then age 51, then age 52, and so on. At the same time, some companies have set pricing according to age bands. They have one price for ages 50-55 or 70-75, and so on (5-year age band policies should be avoided).</p>

<p>If you buy burial insurance when you are 50, you’ll pay less than if you wait to purchase until you’re 80. The older you are, the higher the premium because insurance companies take more risks, ensuring that older folks are statistically closer to death.</p>

<p>When you purchase your policy, it will lock in at the age when you bought it, and it will remain that for life.</p>

<p><strong>GENDER</strong></p>

<p>Being male or female is one important factor determining your coverage’s cost. Men generally pay more than women. The reason is simple if you’re wondering why men need to pay more than women.</p>

<p>Women, on average, live much longer than men do. Therefore, women will pay longer than men, and why do they pay less on life insurance.</p>

<p><strong>FACE AMOUNT</strong></p>

<p>Your coverage will also help determine your burial insurance cost or the amount you need to pay. For example, your cost per $1,000 comes out to be $5.53 per month. If you want $10,000, in this example, your total monthly premium may be around $50.00 per month.</p>

<p>The more coverage you need, the higher the price will be.</p>

<p><strong>TOBACCO USAGE</strong></p>

<p>Each life insurance company has a set price for tobacco and non-tobacco users. They have different rates for each and charge more to applicants who use tobacco.</p>

<p>Cigarette smoking is a major health risk that could significantly reduce life expectancy. This is the reason why smokers pay more for life insurance.</p>

<p>It is important to understand that some insurance providers give a non-tobacco rating if you chew, smoke a pipe, smoke cigars, or smoke e-cigarettes. Cigarette smoking has a tobacco rating.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue life insurance policies don’t even ask if you are a smoker or non-smoker.</p>

<p><br><strong>YOUR HEALTH OR ANY PRE-EXISTING CONDITION</strong></p>

<p>Your overall health also factors in the cost of burial insurance. Your current and past health determines your risk classification and rates.</p>

<p>A pre-existing medical condition can cost you more because medical conditions can significantly affect your life expectancy. Some pre-existing conditions can be so high-risk that purchasing a guaranteed issue life insurance policy is your best option.</p>

<p>The best part about getting a burial insurance calculator and buying burial insurance in your 50s or younger is that you lock in a lower premium for the rest of your life. Being younger means, you are less likely to have any serious illness that could prevent you from qualifying for immediate coverage with no waiting period.</p>

<p>Serious illnesses usually occur as we grow older. It is wise to purchase final life insurance protection to cover your final expenses. If you want a quote for this coverage, click on the instant quote tool on this page.</p>

<h2 id="types-of-burial-insurance"><strong>Types Of Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<p><strong>SIMPLIFIED ISSUE WHOLE LIFE INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>Simplified issue whole life insurance has immediate coverage effective on the first day. This is burial insurance with no waiting period.</p>

<p>Since final expense life insurance, no exam face amount is lower than traditional life insurance; the medical questions are easy.</p>

<p><strong>Potential questions life insurance companies would ask in a simplified issue life insurance application:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Do you have <a href="/burial-insurance-valvular-heart-disease/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">heart disease</a>?</li>
<li>Have you ever had a <a href="/burial-insurance-stroke/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">stroke</a>?</li>
<li>Do you have kidney disease?</li>
<li>Have you been diagnosed with terminal illnesses like cancer in the last two years?</li>
<li>Do you have <a href="/life-insurance-for-hiv-positive/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">HIV</a> or <a href="/burial-insurance-aids-hiv/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">AIDS</a>?</li>
<li>Do you have any cognitive impairment like <a href="/burial-insurance-dementia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">dementia</a> or <a href="/burial-insurance-alzheimers-disease/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Alzheimer’s disease</a>?</li>
<li>Do you have a <a href="/burial-insurance-disability/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">disability</a> that needs a wheelchair or confinement to a nursing home?</li>
</ol>

<p>Minor health problems like high cholesterol or high blood pressure are considered normal. These health questions mainly aim to rule out potentially high-risk applicants, such as those with a terminal illness.</p>

<p>Simplified issue whole life insurance is the best option for people over 50 years old who may have health issues that could prevent them from getting coverage through the traditional underwriting process. </p>

<p>This is an excellent option if you’re not in perfect health, too busy to take a medical exam, or simply don’t want to be poked with a needle.</p>

<p><br><strong>GUARANTEED ISSUE BURIAL INSURANCE</strong></p>

<p>GI policy provides coverage regardless of your health, age, or any other risk factors. This is designed for people who cannot qualify for traditional life insurance due to high-risk factors; if you are between 50-85 and enter into a legal contract, you can qualify.</p>

<p>Guaranteed whole life final expense insurance will provide your family with up to $25,000 of coverage without a medical exam or health questions. Your approval is guaranteed!</p>

<p><strong>Here are some examples of the most common medical conditions where a guaranteed issue burial life expense policy is your only option:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Alzheimer’s or dementia</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance-kidney-dialysis/">Dialysis</a></li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance-organ-transplant/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Organ transplants</a></li>
<li>Oxygen use</li>
<li>Terminal illness</li>
<li>AIDS or HIV</li>
<li><a href="/burial-insurance-wheelchair-users/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Wheelchair</a>-bound because of chronic illness or disease</li>
<li>Mobility issue, needing help with <a href="/burial-insurance-adl-activities-of-daily-living/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">activities of daily living</a> such as <a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-eating-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">eating</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance-bathing-disability-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">bathing</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance-need-help-with-dressing-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">dressing</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-toileting-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">toileting</a>, <a href="/burial-insurance-help-with-continence-activities-of-daily-living-adl/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">continence</a>, transferring</li>
</ul>

<p>Guaranteed issue policies will accept applicants with these medical conditions. Since the insurance companies assume higher risk, the cost of GI policies is generally higher than other types of policies.</p>

<p>Guaranteed issue policies have graded benefits for the first two years. </p>

<p>They have a waiting period that applies to all-natural causes of death. If you die during the two-year waiting period, your beneficiary will receive all the premiums you paid plus 7-10% interest.</p>

<p>The life insurance company will pay 100% of the death benefit if you die from an accident. After the waiting period, you are covered for life.</p>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions"><strong>Frequently Asked Questions </strong></h2>

<p><strong>Is burial insurance the same as life insurance?</strong></p>

<p>Burial insurance is a type of life insurance designed to cover funeral and other end-of-life expenses. While life insurance can be used for this purpose, burial insurance typically has lower death benefits and pays out faster than traditional life insurance policies.</p>
