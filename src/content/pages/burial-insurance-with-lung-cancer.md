<h2 id="best-insurance-option-if-you-have-lung-cancer"><br><strong>What Is My Best Insurance Option If I Have A History Of Lung Cancer? </strong></h2>

<p><br><strong>YOU’RE CANCER-FREE FOR MORE THAN 24 MONTHS</strong></p>

<p>If it’s been more than two years since your last lung cancer treatment, and you are now lung cancer-free.  You can now access the best option since most insurance companies only ask about lung cancer in the last two years.</p>

<p>You are now eligible for a level death benefit plan with first-day coverage and no waiting period from those life insurance companies with a two-year lookback period on lung cancer.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Level death benefit plan with first-day coverage</p>

<p><br><strong>DIAGNOSED WITH LUNG CANCER AND UNDERGOING TREATMENT IN THE LAST 24 MONTHS</strong></p>

<p>With a current lung cancer diagnosis or recent treatment, most life insurance companies will only offer you a guaranteed issue burial insurance with a waiting period. A waiting period is unavoidable under this condition.</p>

<p>If you currently have lung cancer or still undergoing treatment, your lowest-cost option is to take a guaranteed issue whole life insurance. You can even get a low premium with only a two-year waiting period.</p>

<p>These are the instances where guaranteed issue whole life
insurance is your best option:</p>

<ul>
<li>Currently diagnosed with lung cancer</li>
<li>Currently undergoing treatment</li>
<li>Current surgery to remove lung cancer</li>
</ul>

<p><br><strong>A few of these medical treatments include:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Surgery</strong> – operations performed by doctors to cut out cancer tissues from the lungs.</li>
<li><strong>Chemotherapy</strong> – This treatment uses special medicines to kill cancer. The drugs can be taken orally, through the veins, or sometimes both.</li>
<li><strong>Radiation therapy</strong> – uses high-energy rays or radiation to kill the cancer</li>
<li><strong>Targeted therapy</strong> – use drugs to hinder the growth and spread of cancer cells. The drugs can be taken orally or given through the veins.</li>
<li><strong>Immunotherapy</strong> – uses the immune system to fight cancer.</li>
<li><strong>Radiosurgery</strong> – also known as stereotactic body radiotherapy, uses intense radiation to kill lung cancer cells.</li>
</ol>

<p>If you are undertaking any of these treatments for the last 24 months, you cannot avoid a waiting period on your plan. You must be done with the treatments for over two years before qualifying for a first-day coverage plan.</p>

<p>With a guaranteed issue plan, you don’t need to take a medical exam or answer any health questions to qualify. If you have lung cancer, you can quickly get coverage if you are 50-85 years old and a U.S. citizen. </p>

<p>Guaranteed issue final expense insurance has a graded death benefit clause that creates a 2-year waiting period before the death benefit payout will be given in full for the natural cause or non-accidental death. </p>

<p>If you pass away within the first two years or waiting period due to your lung cancer or other illness, the company will refund all your premiums plus 10% interest. If you live beyond the waiting period, 100% of the death benefit will be paid out to your beneficiaries regardless of the cause of your death.</p>

<p>However, this plan will pay 100% of the death benefit if you pass away due to an accident, even during the waiting period.</p>

<p><strong>Best Option:</strong> Guaranteed issue burial insurance</p>

<h2 id="do-i-need-medical-exam-to-qualify"><br><strong>If I Have Lung Cancer, Do I Need A Medical Exam To Qualify For Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<p>You are NOT required to take a medical exam to qualify for burial insurance with lung cancer.</p>

<p>There are two types of lung cancer. These are small cell lung cancer (SCLC), and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). </p>

<p><strong>Factors that may
affect Lung cancer burial insurance eligibility:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Date of diagnosis</li>
<li>The type of treatment you are undergoing</li>
<li>The time when treatment was completed</li>
</ul>

<p>Your initial diagnosis date is the most crucial factor; the more time passes between your diagnosis and application date, the higher the chance of approval.</p>

<p>Additionally, the company must know how long you’ve been cancer-free. The type of treatment will also affect the type of plan you will qualify for and how much you will pay.</p>

<p>When you apply for burial insurance, you only have to answer some basic questions about your health. The application process is simple; you don’t need to provide medical records or blood and urine samples.</p>

<p>You’ll get the official approval from the insurance company often within minutes!</p>

<h2 id="insurance-underwriting-for-lung-cancer"><br><strong><strong>Burial Insurance Underwriting If You Have Lung Cancer</strong></strong></h2>

<p>If you want to access the lowest premium and get first-day coverage, you must apply for a burial insurance plan with underwriting (health questions).</p>

<p><strong>Burial insurance companies have two ways of underwriting:</strong></p>

<p><strong>FIRST</strong> – They may ask you a series of health questions. Your answers to their questions will determine your eligibility.</p>

<p><strong>SECOND</strong> – They will electronically review your prescription history to verify your health.</p>

<p><br><strong>HEALTH QUESTIONS</strong></p>

<p>The most important part of the final expense underwriting is
answering the health questions on the application. Each life insurance company
has its own set of health questions. You will typically see cancer asked in the
knockout section of the application.</p>

<ul>
<li>Have you ever had or been diagnosed with more than one occurrence of the same or different type of cancer?</li>
<li>During the last 24 months, have you been diagnosed by a physician as having or been treated for internal cancer?</li>
</ul>

<p>Answering “YES” to any of the cancer questions
will limit your life insurance options.</p>

<p><br><strong>PRESCRIPTION HISTORY CHECK</strong></p>

<p>Aside from the health questions, the insurance companies
will also electronically check your prescription history to know your health
status and verify your response to the health questionnaire.</p>

<p>The information they will get from your prescription history includes:</p>

<ul>
<li>Name of your medications</li>
<li>Prescription date</li>
<li>Dosage or quantity prescribed</li>
<li>Frequency taken each day</li>
</ul>

<p>Insurance providers are also on the lookout for specific drugs that will indicate you have lung cancer. Here’s a list of common <a href="https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/drugs/lung" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">medications</a> for lung cancer that are red flags by most insurance companies:</p>

<li>Alectinib</li>
<li>Apadaz</li>

<p>Taking any of these prescription medications will indicate to the insurance company that you are a lung cancer patient. Being a lung cancer patient puts you in a higher risk category.</p>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions"><strong>  Frequently Asked Questions </strong></h2>

<p><strong>Can I get life insurance with lung cancer?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, you can get life insurance if you have had lung cancer. Many of the insurance companies we work with at Final Expense Guy offer insurance coverage to people with a past cancer history. If you’ve been cancer-free for more than 24 months, you may even qualify for a first-day coverage plan with some insurance companies.</p>

<p><br><strong>Can you get life insurance with cancer diagnosis?</strong></p>

<p>Yes, you can get life insurance if you have a recent cancer diagnosis or treatment. However, your options are limited. Most life insurance companies have asked about cancer in the last two years.
