<h1>AIG (Corebridge) Burial Insurance Review – Pros &amp; Cons</h1>

<p><strong>Here’s the Bottom Line:</strong><br>• AIG offers many policy types, but it isn’t always the cheapest option<br>• Term life rates can be competitive, but whole life gets expensive<br>• Guaranteed issue plans cost more and delay payouts for 2 years<br>• Customer complaints are higher than average for a company its size<br>• You’ll likely deal with agents instead of a simple online process<br>• Not comparing companies can leave you overpaying for similar coverage</p>

<p>AIG life insurance company review comes down to flexibility versus trade-offs. AIG, now branded as Corebridge Financial, offers term, whole life, universal life, and burial insurance options, giving you a lot of choices. The upside is competitive term pricing and strong financial stability with an A rating, meaning they can pay claims. The downside is where people get burned. Customer complaints tend to be higher than average, and guaranteed-issue or whole-life policies can be expensive and have waiting periods. AIG can work in certain situations, especially for harder-to-approve cases, but it’s rarely the best deal if you actually shop around.</p>

<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>What to Ask Before Buying AIG&#160; Burial Insurance</strong></h2>

<h3 id="h-what-are-my-policy-options-with-aig"><strong>What Are My Policy Options With AIG?</strong></h3>

<p>Be aware that AIG Life Insurance changed its name to Corebridge Financial in 2022. Your AIG policy is valid with Corebridge Financial as long as you keep your current payments.</p>

<p>Since this name change is fairly recent, we will use the AIG name through this article, but understand that everything we say about AIG also pertains to Corebridge financial.</p>

<p>They offer three whole life insurance plans for people aged 50 to 85.</p>

<p><strong>SimpliNow Legacy Max – First-Day Coverage Plan</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>First-day coverage – protection starts on the first day.</li>
<li>No medical exam – you only need to answer a few health questions</li>
<li>Coverage amount available – $5,000 – $35,000</li>
<li>Permanent whole life insurance that lasts until the age of 121 years old</li>
<li>Builds cash value that you can borrow against</li>
<li>Fixed pricing that never increases</li>
<li>Coverage that never decreases</li>
<li>Available riders:
<ul>
<li>Nursing home confinement</li>
<li>Terminal illness</li>
<li>Accidental death benefit rider</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Limited-pay feature – you will not have to pay your premium if you reach the maximum payment age between 90 and 95. Maximum payment age is determined by gender, age, and face amount.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>SimpliNow Legacy – Graded Benefit Plan</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>2-year waiting period</li>
<li>If you answer “Yes” to select health questions, you will be forced into this plan</li>
<li>Permanent whole life insurance that lasts until the age of 121 years old</li>
<li>Builds cash value that you can borrow against</li>
<li>Fixed pricing that never increases</li>
<li>Coverage that never decreases</li>
<li>Coverage amount available: $5,000 – $25,000</li>
<li>100% death benefit if you die from an accident</li>
<li>110% of the premium will be paid if you die during the first two years from natural or medical-related death.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Guaranteed Issue Whole Life Insurance</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>2-year waiting period</li>
<li>No medical exam and no health questions</li>
<li>Permanent whole life insurance that lasts until the age of 121 years old</li>
<li>Builds cash value that you can borrow against</li>
<li>Fixed pricing that never increases</li>
<li>Coverage that never decreases</li>
<li>Coverage amount available: $5,000 – $25,000</li>
<li>Available riders:
<ul>
<li>Chronic illness rider</li>
<li>Terminal illness rider</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="h-does-aig-have-any-hidden-fine-print-in-their-policy"><strong>Does AIG Have Any Hidden “Fine Print” In Their Policy?</strong></h3>

<p>The SimpliNow Legacy Graded Plan and their Guaranteed Issue Whole Life Insurance both have 2-year waiting periods. </p>

<p>“The AIG Guaranteed Issue Whole Life policy referenced offers a limited death benefit in the first two years of the policy. Should death occur in the first two years, a death benefit will be equal to 110% of the premiums paid. In the event of suicide, the death benefit is limited to a refund of premiums only.”</p>

<p>Who would ever accept a 2-year waiting period and expensive pricing when you could get inexpensively priced 1st-day coverage elsewhere?</p>

<p>You are advised to avoid these plans, and there are better coverage options from other companies with better benefits than these two graded plans.</p>

<p>If you die from a natural cause in the first two years with these plans, your beneficiary will only get your previously paid premiums back, plus an extra 10%. They will only pay 100% if you die from an accident.</p>

<p>There is also no coverage for suicide in the first two years either (this is standard with all life insurance policies).</p>

<h2 id="other-benefits"><strong>Are There Any Riders Or Other Benefits With AIG Burial Insurance?</strong></h2>

<h3><strong>Riders &amp; Add-Ons</strong></h3>

<p>Depending on the plan an agent puts you in, you may have other riders included.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Terminal Illness Rider: </strong>Death benefit can be accessed if the insured is diagnosed with a terminal illness resulting in death in 12 months or less.</li>
<li><strong>Nursing Home Confinement Rider: </strong>Death benefit can also be accessed if the insured is continuously confined in a nursing home and expected to be confined until death.</li>
<li><strong>Chronic Illness Rider</strong> – Access up to 25% death benefit when insured is diagnosed with chronic illness.</li>
<li><strong>Limited-Pay Feature</strong> – you will not have to pay additional premiums and your coverage will remain in-force until age 100 when you reach your maximum payment age. Maximum payment age is determined by gender, face amount and age.</li>
<li><strong>Policy Loans: </strong> You can borrow up to 100% of your cash value. This is tax-free. Loans reduce your cash value amount and are subtracted from your death benefit. The interest on policy loans varies by state.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>If the proposed insured answers yes to any of the following questions, the proposed insurance is not eligible for any coverage.</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Is the Proposed Insured currently bedridden or confined to any hospital facility or skilled nursing facility; or receiving or been advised to receive care in a nursing home, hospice care, or home health care?</li>
<li>Is the Proposed Insured receiving assistance with activities of daily living, including eating, bathing, toileting, or dressing due to a chronic or debilitating condition?</li>
<li>Does the Proposed Insured require any of the following due to a debilitating condition: wheelchair, electric scooter,<br>or oxygen equipment to assist breathing (excluding use for sleep apnea)?</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Inured been diagnosed by a licensed member of the medical profession with a terminal illness or terminal condition that is expected to result in death within 12 months or less?</li>
<li>Has the Proposed Insured been diagnosed with brain aneurysm or transient ischemic attack (TIA) in the past 6 months, or<br>EVER had recurrent episodes of TIA (more than once)?</li>
<li>Is the Proposed Insured currently incarcerated in a prison or jail?</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Has the proposed insurance EVER been diagnosed as having , been treated for, or consulted a licensed member of the medical profession of any of the following:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Alzheimer’s Disease, Dementia, Huntington’s Disease, Sickle Cell Anemia, Myelodysplastic Syndrome (MDS), Lou Gehrig’s Disease (ALS), Mental Incapacity, Cirrhosis, Quadriplegia or Paraplegia</li>
<li>HIV infection AIDS or AIDS-Related Complex (ARC)</li>
<li>Advanced or End Stage Renal Disease or in need of Dialysis</li>
<li>Bone Marrow, Organ Transplant or Lymphoma</li>
<li>Metastatic or Recurrent Cancer of the same type (Stage III or Stage IV cancer)</li>
<li>Amputation due to diabetic complications or a hospitalization in the past 24 months due to diabetes</li>
<li>Heart Failure or Defibrillator device implanted</li>
<li>Suicide attempt</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>In the last 12 months has the Proposed Insured:</strong></p>

<ol>
<li>Been diagnosed or treated for, or consulted a licensed member of the medical profession for stroke, or EVER had a stroke AND Diabetes and/or Coronary Artery Disease?</li>
<li>Been advised by a licensed member of the medical profession to have a surgical operation, diagnostic testing other than for routine screening purposes or for those related to HIV/AIDS, treatment, hospitalization, or other procedure which has not been done or for which results are not known?</li>
