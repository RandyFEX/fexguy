<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>What to ask before buying Guarantee Trust Life insurance</strong></h2>

<h3 id="h-what-are-my-policy-options-with-guarantee-trust-life">What are my policy options with Guarantee Trust Life?</h3>

<p>Guarantee Trust Life offers Heritage Plan graded benefit whole life insurance designed for people 50-90 years old with significant health problems who do not want a policy with a two-year waiting period. </p>

<h3>What insurance plan does Guarantee Trust Life offer?</h3>

<p><strong>Guarantee Trust Life offers their whole life Heritage Plan offers the following benefits:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>No Medical Exam</li>
<li>Limited Health Questions</li>
<li>Ages 50-90 years old are accepted</li>
<li>Permanent Coverage that never expires</li>
<li>Your monthly premium will never increase</li>
<li>$2,500 – $20,000 available coverage amount</li>
<li>Cash value buildup that can be borrowed against in emergencies</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="h-how-does-heritage-plan-work">How does Heritage Plan work?</h3>

<p>Guaranteed Trust Life Heritage Plan is a burial insurance policy designed to pay for burial, cremation, funeral, or final expenses. </p>

<p>The Heritage Plan has simple health questions and easy underwriting, and they approve most health issues.</p>

<p>If you qualify for this plan, your insurance benefits will begin the day you pay your first premium. However, your death benefit will be phased in during the first two years of the policy.</p>

<p><strong>Death Benefit Payout Schedule:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li><strong>1st-Day Coverage </strong>– Any accidental or catastrophic cause of death</li>
<li><strong>Months 1-12</strong> – Death benefit is equal to premiums paid plus 5%</li>
<li><strong>Months 13-24</strong> – Death benefit is 50% of the coverage amount</li>
<li><strong>After 24 Months</strong> – Death benefit is 100% of the coverage amount</li>
</ul>

<p>For accidental death (during the first two years) GTL would pay the full coverage amount.</p>

<h3 id="h-does-guarantee-trust-life-have-any-fine-print-in-their-policy">Does Guarantee Trust Life have any “Fine Print” in their policy? </h3>

<p>There is no “fine print” with the GTL Heritage plan.</p>

<p>However, there are some policy details to be aware of to ensure this is the right plan for you.</p>

<p>Although coverage begins on the first day, they phase in the death benefit according the timeline below:</p>

<ul>
<li>Issue Ages: 50-90 years</li>
<li>Death Benefit Amounts: $2,500 to $20,000<br>• Death Benefit in year one is Premiums Paid plus 5%<br>• Death Benefit in year two is 50% of the face amount<br>• Death Benefit after year two is 100% of the face amount</li>
</ul>

<p>GTL will pay the full face amount for any accidental death. Accidental death coverage is a first-day benefit.</p>

<h2 id="getting-approved"><strong>How do I get approved by Guarantee Trust Life?</strong></h2>

<h3 id="h-what-are-guarantee-trust-life-s-application-questions">What are Guarantee Trust Life’s application questions?</h3>

<p>If any answer to questions 1 through 6 is YES, you are not eligible for coverage.</p>

<ol>
<li>Within the last twenty four (24) months, have you been receiving kidney dialysis, require daily oxygen use (excluding CPAP), have an implanted defibrillator or received or been advised by a medical professional to get an organ transplant?</li>
<li>Have you ever been diagnosed with or treated by a medical professional for Alzheimer’s disease or dementia or are currently being treated for memory loss? </li>
<li>Within the last twenty four months, have you been diagnosed as having, or been prescribed medication by a medical professional or been treated by a medical professional for cancer (excluding Stage A Prostate Cancer, Carcinoma in Situ, Squamous Cell or Basal Cell Carcinoma)?</li>
<li>Have you ever had or been recommended to have an amputation due to complications from diabetes? </li>
<li>Are you currently bedridden, confined to a hospital, nursing home, mental care facility, long term care facility, hospice or have you been diagnosed with an end-stage or terminal illness, or been told by a medical professional that you have less than 12 months to live? </li>
<li>Have you been diagnosed by a medical professional as having the Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV), ARC or AIDS?</li>
</ol>

<p>To qualify for the Guarantee Trust Life Heritage Plan, you must answer “No” to the health questions above.</p>
