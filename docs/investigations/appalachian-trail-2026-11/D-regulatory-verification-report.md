# D. Regulatory Verification Report

**Guide:** One Last Amazing Adventure: The Most Beautiful Places to Scatter Ashes Along the Appalachian Trail
**Checked:** November 23, 2026
**Status:** research only. Nothing on FuneralFunds.com was changed.

## 1. How to read this report

Every destination gets one of four classifications. Silence is never treated as permission.

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PERMITTED | The land manager publishes conditions under which a family may scatter without a permit. The conditions still apply. |
| PERMIT OR PERMISSION REQUIRED | The land manager allows it only with a permit, a letter, or approval. |
| PROHIBITED | The land manager says it is not allowed (including "not allowed unless officially approved"). |
| UNCONFIRMED | No published rule was found. The family must ask before any ceremony. |

Evidence labels:

- **OFFICIAL (fetched):** the official page was opened and quoted directly.
- **OFFICIAL (via search summary):** an official page, seen only through a search summary because the sandbox could not open it. It needs a direct check before publication.
- **SECONDARY:** a non-official source (hiking club, third-party host of a regulation, guidebook). Used only where labeled.

A **remembrance place** is a beautiful spot to visit, read a letter, or hold a moment of silence. A **scattering place** is a spot where the land manager actually allows ashes to be scattered. The guide keeps the two separate.

## 3. National Park Service units (verified from primary sources)

The Appalachian Trail crosses seven NPS units. Each publishes its own rule under 36 CFR 2.62(b), which prohibits scattering "except pursuant to the terms and conditions of a permit, or in designated areas according to conditions which may be established by the superintendent."

### 3.1 Appalachian National Scenic Trail (APPA): PERMITTED

- **Source:** https://www.nps.gov/appa/learn/management/compendium.htm (approved 4/08/2026, Scott McCormick, Acting Superintendent). OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Conditions:** pulverized remains; at least **100 yards** from any trail, road, developed facility, historic or archeological site, or body of water. Memorials are prohibited without the NPS Director's authorization.
- **Scope:** only "lands and waters administered by the National Park Service" within the Trail's boundaries. On national forest, state park, game land, ATC or club land, that manager's rules govern.
- **Contact:** Appalachian Trail Park Office, Harpers Ferry, 304 535-6278.
- **Practical note:** 100 yards from the trail itself is a long way off a narrow corridor. On much NPS corridor land it may be hard to meet. The guide should say that plainly.

### 3.2 Great Smoky Mountains National Park (GRSM): PERMISSION REQUIRED

- **Sources:** https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/scatter-cremated-ashes.htm (updated November 19, 2026); the downloadable Letter of Permission (July 21, 2026); the 2026 compendium (page updated June 6, 2026). All OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Process:** download and carry the Letter of Permission while on site. No fee. No other contact needed for 25 people or fewer. Over 25 people needs a Special Use Permit.
- **Setbacks, letter (July 2026):** 300 ft from buildings, towers, historic sites and cemeteries; 150 ft from roads, trails, campgrounds and picnic areas; 150 ft from water.
- **Setbacks, compendium:** 200 ft, 100 ft and 150 ft. The compendium also calls the authorization a "permit".
- **Discrepancy (confirmed):** the two documents conflict. **Recommendation:** publish the stricter letter figures and tell readers to confirm with the park at (865)436-1200.
- **Also:** no releases of birds, butterflies or other objects.
- **Destinations:** Kuwohi (formerly Clingmans Dome; the observation tower is on the National Register, so the 300 ft tower setback applies) and Charlies Bunion (8 mi round trip from Newfound Gap per NPS).
- **Only official coordinates found in this research:** the Kuwohi parking area, 35.556209, -83.495269, from the GRSM compendium's ceremony-location table.

### 3.3 Blue Ridge Parkway (BLRI): PERMITTED

- **Sources:** https://www.nps.gov/blri/learn/management/compendium-cfr-2-62.htm (updated January 14, 2026); visitor page https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/memorialization.htm (2016). OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Conditions:** no permit for 25 people or fewer; pulverized; at least 100 ft from trails, roads, developed facilities and water. A permit is needed for ceremonies that might conflict with park operations.
- **Prohibited locations:** Craggy Gardens, Devils Courthouse and Rough Ridge.
- **Contact:** 828 348-3400.
- **Destination:** Thunder Ridge Overlook (milepost 74; NPS pages also say 74.7). The adjoining Thunder Ridge Wilderness is not NPS land. Apple Orchard Falls trail starts at a BLRI overlook, but the falls' land manager is not established.

### 3.4 Shenandoah National Park (SHEN): PERMITTED

- **Source:** https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/management/compendium.htm (last updated June 8, 2026; approved by Tracy Stakely, Superintendent). OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Conditions:** no permit; cremated and pulverized; scattered on the ground at least 100 ft from any trail, road, developed facility or body of water; spread over a large enough area that ashes do not accumulate; no scattering from the air; no commercial distribution; no marker, urn or container left. Permits for ceremonies or assemblies that might conflict with park operations. The park entrance fee applies.
- **Contact:** (540) 999-3500.
- **Destinations:** Blackrock (Skyline Drive mile 84.4) and Compton Peak (mile 10.4).
- **Bear Den Mountain:** resolved as the SHEN peak (Beagle Gap, milepost 99.3; see section 10).

### 3.5 Harpers Ferry National Historical Park (HAFE): PERMIT REQUIRED

- **Sources:** compendium https://www.nps.gov/hafe/learn/management/superintendent-s-compendium-live.htm (signature dated 9/10/2026); permits page https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm. OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Designated areas only (three):** the Shenandoah River shoreline at the Point; the Potomac River shoreline at Potoma Wayside; vegetation adjacent to Jefferson Rock. These are areas, not GPS pins. No pins are placed.
- **Process:** special use permit. Contact 304-535-6232.
- **Fee:** the permits page lists scattering of ashes as a special use permit activity and requires a non-refundable $50.00 application fee, at least one month's notice and up to 30 days to process (see section 10).
- **Jefferson Rock:** the rock itself is closed to climbing, standing and sitting. It is a remembrance place; scattering is only in the adjacent vegetation, with a permit.

### 3.6 Chesapeake and Ohio Canal NHP (CHOH): PERMIT REQUIRED

- **Sources:** compendium https://www.nps.gov/choh/learn/management/superintendent-s-compendium.htm (dated 09/10/2026); letter permit https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/upload/CHOH-Scattering-Ashes-Permit-2.pdf (2025). OFFICIAL (fetched).
- **Process:** download the letter, notify the permits office in advance, receive a permit number. No fee.
- **Setbacks:** 50 ft from developed locations (roads, towpath, trails) and 50 ft from the Potomac River and other water.
- **Contact:** (301) 745-5815.
- **Relevance:** the A.T. follows the towpath near Harpers Ferry and Weverton. Whether Weverton Cliffs themselves are CHOH land is not established.

### 3.7 Delaware Water Gap NRA (DEWA): UNCONFIRMED

- **Sources:** compendium (June 2026), permits page, restricted activities page. OFFICIAL (fetched). None mentions scattering ashes.
- **Default:** under 36 CFR 2.62(b), without a designated area or conditions, a permit is needed. The park has not published either.
- **Contact:** special use permits 570-426-2440.
- **Destinations:** Sunfish Pond appears to be in Worthington State Forest (New Jersey), which would put it outside DEWA rules. Wolf Rocks (PA) appears to be south of the park boundary at Totts Gap. Both land managers are not fully established.

## 4. Appalachian Trail Conservancy

- **Finding:** no published ATC position on scattering human ashes was found. ATC's Leave No Trace pages discuss campfire ashes only.
- A forum post claims ATC says scattering on NPS lands needs a permit from the NPS Director. That claim is unverified and conflicts with the APPA compendium. It is not used.
- **Recommendation:** ask ATC directly before publication. Until then the guide may cite ATC's general Leave No Trace ethic, never as an ash policy.

## 8. Discrepancies found

1. **GRSM setbacks:** letter (300/150/150 ft) vs compendium (200/100/150 ft). The article names both.
2. **BLRI milepost:** Thunder Ridge at 74 vs 74.7 on two NPS pages.
3. **ATC distances:** Annapolis Rock (2.2, 3 and 4.8 mi), Blackrock (1.0 vs 2.0 mi), Woody Gap to Big Cedar (2.4 vs 2.0 mi), Cobble Hill (2.8 vs 3.4 mi), Falls Village (1.1 vs 1.4 mi).
4. **ATC difficulty ratings:** Springer, Long Creek Falls, Max Patch and Laurel Fork have different ratings on different ATC pages.
5. **Thundering Falls parking:** the Forest Service describes an accessible lot; ATC says the parking area has no designated ADA spaces.
6. **v6 duplicates merged:** two Sunrise Mountain records; Falls Village and Great Falls; Mount Greylock summit and Bascom Lodge; the three Harpers Ferry designated-area records moved into the Harpers Ferry and Jefferson Rock cards.

## 10. Follow-up research (November 23, 2026)

Randy asked for these to be resolved by research rather than phone calls.

1. **Appalachian Trail Conservancy.** Further searches found no ATC position on scattering ashes. Its Leave No Trace guidance covers campfire ashes and asks hikers to leave natural and historical objects where they find them. The article now says ATC has not published a position and does not manage most Trail land.
2. **National forests (Chattahoochee-Oconee, Pisgah and Nantahala, Green Mountain and the others).** Further searches found no forest-specific rule. Secondary sources conflict: some say the Forest Service has no national rule and generally needs no permit; others say it is prohibited. Official pages from other forests (Gifford Pinchot, Uinta-Wasatch-Cache, Black Hills) say it is not authorized. These stay UNCONFIRMED, and the article tells readers to call the ranger district themselves. No one at Funeral Funds needs to call.
3. **Great Smoky Mountains.** The article now names both documents: the Letter of Permission (300/150/150 ft) and the park's own rulebook, the Superintendent's Compendium (200/100/150 ft), and notes that following the letter satisfies both.
4. **Harpers Ferry fee: resolved.** The park's permits page (https://www.nps.gov/hafe/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm, last updated January 29, 2026, fetched) lists "Memorialization/scattering of ashes" among activities that need a special use permit, and says: "A non-refundable application fee of $50.00 is required at the time of application. Please submit a completed application to the park a minimum of one month in advance of the event. It may take up to 30 days to process a special use permit application." The article now states this.
5. **Bear Den Mountain: resolved.** The ATC hike page (via search summary) starts it at a small dirt lot at Beagle Gap, Skyline Drive milepost 99.3, 2.2 miles round trip. That is inside Shenandoah National Park, and an NPS news release places Bear Den Mountain in the park's South District. It is not the Bears Den trail center near Bluemont. It is now a published Shenandoah destination (PERMITTED under the park's conditions).
6. **Remaining direct checks.** Items labeled "OFFICIAL (via search summary)", mostly ATC and Forest Service pages this sandbox could not open, are still worth opening once in a normal browser before publication.
